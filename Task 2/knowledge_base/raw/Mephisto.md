# Mephisto

"The evil of Mephisto, Lord of Hatred, was so pervasive that even after he had been defeated and entombed in a soulstone, his demonic essence oozed upwards into Travincal and corrupted the Zakarum priests. Though he fell to the same heroes who killed his brothers, I fear for us should he ever return."

**Dul'Mephistos, the Lord of Hatred**, commonly referred to as **Mephisto**, is one of the three Prime Evils.

## Biography

"I am eternal, creature. Hatred always survives."


### The Great Conflict

"All worlds will fall before the burning standards of Hell. I have seen it.

Mephisto

Like all the Great Evils, Mephisto was born from one of the seven heads of the great dragon Tathame. His realm in the Burning Hells was the Realm of Hatred, and he watched with joy as its denizens schemed and warred against each other. He, Diablo, and Baal were the three Prime Evils, and from the outset, warred amongst each other to maintain dominion and rule over the legions of the Burning Hells. He acted as the mentor of Belial.

As with all of demonkind, Mephisto warred against the High Heavens in the Eternal Conflict. However, he preferred to operate from the shadows rather than lead an army in the open.

One battle bore angels right into the heart of his lair, making an attack against Mephisto himself. One of the angels managed to draw blood, causing it to spill onto the ground. This led to the creation of the Magma Demons, who rose up to defend their master and drove the angels back.

Mephisto and his brothers almost achieved victory against the High Heavens at the Fifth Battle of the Diamond Gates. However, confident of victory, the Primes began arguing over who would get the spoils of war, and thus, their attack failed.

### The Sin War

"Cursed am I to lead an army of the blind. They do not perceive that the angels are fleeing this realm, and the ones they find are merely trapped or lost. A great change is upon us. Withdraw from the fields, my brothers. Some battles can only be won with words."

Mephisto's visage

While leading his forces on the battlefields of Pandemonium, Mephisto noticed that the angels were withdrawing. He ordered his forces to withdraw so he could analyze the situation. Baal, losing patience, stormed the Pandemonium Fortress and discovered that the Worldstone was missing. Thus, the Eternal Conflict ground to a halt. Eventually, tales of demons summoned into a new realm and a new people reached the ears of the Prime Evils. They sensed within humanity the dormant powers of their nephalem forefathers, and thus resolved to corrupt humanity into a weapon to use against the High Heavens. So it was that Mephisto and his brothers cast their influence into mortal minds, spreading their ideas across Sanctuary. Their whispers led to the creation of the Triune, where each Prime Evil took on the role of a seemingly compassionate deity. Mephisto took on the persona of Mefis, Spirit of Love. Their actions were countered by the rogue angel Inarius, who formed the Cathedral of Light to counter their influence. Thus began the Sin War, as the Prime Evils and Inarius secretly vyed for influence over humanity.

Mephisto is gifted Inarius

The Sin War ended with a final battle between the forces of Heaven, Hell, and the Edyrem, after which Mephisto met with the Angiris Council. Mephisto offered to let Sanctuary be untouched by either angel or demon to see what the children may yet become on their own for good or ill. It was agreed that Mephisto cast his mark on the ruins of the Cathedral of Light to seal the flux between worlds and in return, Mephisto was given Inarius. Humanity would finally be allowed to find its own path, free of the interference from angels and demons.

### The Dark Exile

"Follow my orders, Azmodan, as you did in ages immemorial."  
"You will lead us only to failure, brother! I make my own way."

The Lord of Hatred

In the millennia following the Sin War, the Lesser Evils grew discontent, believing that the Prime Evils had forsaken the Eternal Conflict to focus on humanity. The four Lesser Evils rose up against the Primes, and despite the efforts of Mephisto and his brothers, they were cast out of Hell into the mortal realm, in what would become known as the Dark Exile.

The banishment of the Prime Evils from Hell to the mortal plane also left them incorporeal. In order to acquire a physical form, they required a body for their spirit to possess. Over time their demonic presence twisted the victims human flesh into grotesque simulacrums of their physical form.

In response to the most evil of beings walking Sanctuary, the Archangel Tyrael rallied several of the disparate Mage Clans into an alliance known as the Horadrim, and gave them the soulstones to bind the Three forever. In truth, this series of events had been masterminded by the Prime Evils. Through the soulstones they would corrupt the Worldstone itself and with it, all of humanity. A new army to be used against the forces of Heaven.

At some point during the Exile, Mephisto was present in Aranoch. He bound the spirits of demons into the mummies that slept beneath the sands of Lut Gholein, hordes of undead emerging to bring woe to the living.

Mephisto

Mephisto was the first of the Three to be captured. During their hunt for him, the Horadrim led an army against his Fortress of Bone, but the common soldiers found themselves hard pressed to advance against the seemingly endless tides of undead that he summoned, and were nearly overwhelmed. Eventually, Mephisto was found in or around one of the urban centers of Kehjistan, Ahb Bukeir. The fight cost thousands innocent lives and the city of Ahb Bukeir was leveled, but eventually, the Horadrim succeeded in defeating the Lord of Hatred and imprisoning his essence inside the soulstone. It was then entrusted to the Zakarum priests and kept sealed in the Guardian Tower of the Temple of Light in Travincal.

### The Corruption of Zakarum

"Hatred is pure. Hatred does not falter. It gives you purpose when all else fails! Let the fire in your hearts become a weapon! Hate the unbelievers! Hate the weak! Hate until there is nothing left but glory!"

Over the generations, Mephisto's darkness steadily seeped from his soulstone and was able to corrupt the High Council of Zakarum, and the body became a mockery of its former glory. The first signs of this were as early as the 11th century. Two centuries after this, Mephisto cemented his control over the Church. He had been able to overcome the soulstone enough to have the Zakarum priests to work spells to shatter the soulstone into seven shards, breaking the mystic seal and freeing him. The Que-Hegan Khalim proved immune to his corruption, so Mephisto had the priesthood slay and dismember him. The priest Sankekur became the new Que-Hegan and consequently, Mephisto's embodiment on the mortal plane. The corrupted High Council fashioned the Compelling Orbto control the rest of the Zakarum faithful and used their powers to conceal the lair of their master. Years passed, and eventually, Lazarus was sent west to reawaken Diablo.

Mephisto in the depths of the temple

Thus, Diablo arrived in the form of the Dark Wanderer, with Baal at his side. The two entered the temple's innermost chamber where Mephisto's soulstone was kept, and released him. Mephisto possessed Sankekur (already under Mephisto's control), whose body was twisted to represent Mephisto's hideous visage. After nearly three centuries, the Prime Evils were united. Within the chamber, the Three devised a final stratagem to reclaim the Burning Hells and claim vengeance upon those who overthrew them, along with enslaving humanity to their will. Diablo, assuming his true form, crossed through the Infernal Gate into Hell to rally those still loyal to the Three. Baal began his journey north to corrupt the Worldstone while Mephisto remained behind, bent on destroying anything that could oppose their plan.

Mephisto is confronted

It wasn't long before a group of mortal heroes arrived intent on doing such a thing. In the fevered combat that followed, Mephisto worked diligently to turn his foes' own anger and distrust against them. But despite the long hunt and many hours of battle, the heroes defeated Mephisto and imprisoned his essence within his soulstone. The artifact was later destroyed at the Hellforge, which would, in theory, cast his spirit into some unseen netherworld.

Even after death, Sankekur's corpse still seeped corruption from Mephisto's deep rooted demonic influence, and it was sealed in a chamber at Hawezar by one of the heroes who defeated him.

### The Black Soulstone

"When I found the Lord of Hatred's soul, it fought back with a rage I had not anticipated. More than once, it seized control of my mind, and if my ritual did not have safeguards built into it, I would have been lost. Nevertheless, I completed my task."


Mephisto, as sketched by Adria

Mephisto's essence ended up in the Black Soulstone, as per the machinations of Adria. He was the fourth and final Great Evil to be marked by her in her preparation for Diablo's return. When she found his soul, Mephisto fought back with a rage that she had not anticipated. More than once, he was able to take control of the witch's mind, and if not for the safeguards Adria had employed in the ritual, she would have been lost. Nevertheless, she succeeded in marking his essence.

Mephisto was briefly merged into Diablo as a single Prime Evil, but Diablo was defeated by a new hero and the Great Evils were drawn back into the Black Soulstone, their souls screaming for release. Because of Malthael, the stone was destroyed, and Diablo was freed, still containing Mephisto's essence and those of the other Evils. Eventually however, Mephisto's spirit was able to break free from Diablo, just as all the other Evils.

### The Great Enmity

"He is still weak, vulnerable. I must strike before he can resist."

A statue of Mephisto in Hell

Mephisto slowly reformed in Hell. His demonic influence bubbled up from Hell, causing the formation of the Fields of Hatred.

At some point, Lilith was summoned into Sanctuary by a former Horadrim, Elias. This brought a resurgence to the Cult of Triune, who now worshipped her. Meanwhile, Mephisto's essence remained in Hell, still reforming, which made him vulnerable. Lilith intended to strike her father while he was vulnerable and claim his power as her own. To counter Lilith, Mephisto dispatched his herald to Sanctuary, where it aided a mortal known only as the Wanderer.

Finally, his essence began to reform within his temple in the Realm of Hatred. Unfortunately for him, Lilith sensed this, and made efforts to reach him before he fully reforms and then devour him to take his essence. According to Mephisto himself, Elias' meddling with the power at the Temple of the Primes caused imbalance to the realms of Hell; the Vision of Sescheron and its deceased inhabitans ended up in his realm, while in truth they belonged in Baal's domain, not his.

When the Wanderer came to a Triune shrine dedicated to him to request his blessings to use against Lilith, the Bloodied Wolf revealed himself to them, and told them his true identity. He agreed to grant them the blessing if they put the renegade souls to rest.

From there he continued to help them (dialogues adapting accordingly to what order the player chooses to go with the campaign), now without the need to hide himself. He came to an agreement with them; he would lead them to his cathedral where his essence gathered ahead of Lilith. He would help them subdue Lilith after they freed him, after which she would be sealed in a shard of the Worldstone, which had been fashioned into a soulstone. He would keep the soulstone to make sure she didn't return. After that, he would return them to Sanctuary and seal the gates of Hell.

#### Resealed

"Your lives and those of all humanity depends on what you do next. Choose wisely."


Neyrelle seals Mephisto's essence, tainting herself in the process

Unfortunately, when the time arrived, the Wanderer was accompanied by Neyrelle, who had the new soulstone in her possession. She recognized the runes on the wolf's head as that of Mephisto, and was aghast when she was told its true identity. She argued that between Lilith and Mephisto, he was the greater threat. The Wanderer decided to leave the final decision to her. Within his temple, Mephisto's essence could be seen as a giant floating sphere of blood; within which a shade of his visage could be glimpsed. Mephisto tried to persuade her again to stick to the agreed plan, but she refused, and stabbed his essence into the new soulstone, thereby sealing him. In the process, Neyrelle was also tainted by his essence.


Mephisto, in the form of the Bloodied Wolf, watches Neyrelle and his soulstone

After Lilith was defeated, Neyrelle travelled away, promising to find a way to put an end to the Primes before she is lost. Possibly unknown to her, and unseen to all, Mephisto stalked her in the form of the Bloodied Wolf, watching as she lost a bit of herself with each passing day.

At some point, Mephisto imprisoned Astaroth in a cell guarded by the Bloodied Wolf, as punishment for his lieutenant betraying him to help Lilith.

#### Vessel of Hatred

Still contained within the soulstone, Neyrelle took Mephisto to Nahantu. With every step she took, Mephisto grew stronger, constantly pushed against Neyrelle, attempting to break her spirit, whispering to her even as she slept. By the time Neyrelle neared the River Gate, Mephisto's influence over her had become dire, both physically and psychologically. Seeking to manipulate her, Mephisto appeared before Neyrelle as her mother, telling her that "she" was proud of her daughter, but that she needed to give up. Neyrelle saw through Mephisto's deception, and the Lord of Hatred psychologically tormented her. Neyrelle managed to escape Mephisto's hallucination, but the corruption that was spreading from his soulstone had slain her boatman. With a heavy heart, Neyrelle took his oar and continued on to Nehantu. Mephisto continued to whisper to her.

As Neyrelle carried Mephisto's soulstone into Nahantu, his corruption began to seep into the Spirit Realm and out of Nahantu across Estuar, leaving monsters twisted by Hatred's influence. His corruption decimated the Zakarum Remnants.

### Age of Hatred

"Oh, Akarat, beloved Shepherd of Light. After an eternity of vigilance, you are betrayed. And by your own flock. Pity.  
Now the wolf shall rise anew. Wrapped in the guise of man, I shall walk amongst the innocent. And there will be no salvation in the light."

Mephisto takes control of Akarat's body.

Mephisto was able to whisper into the ears of Eru, the Spiritborn who saved the Wanderer and was helping them to find Neyrelle. By blaming all the tragedies he had suffered on the arrival of Neyrelle with the soulstone, Mephisto managed to turn Eru to his side; they made a pact in which Mephisto would spare Nahantu should Eru help him. In a timely betrayal, Eru stole Akarat's corpse from the depths of the Vault of Light, as well Mephisto's soulstone. In the hall of the dedicants beneath Travincal, Akarat's corpse was laid on the ground, and the soulstone on its chest. Tar leaked from the stone, forming a pool which submerged the corpse. Shortly after, Akarat's corpse rose again, with Mephisto now inhabiting it, as the nearby statues began to melt due to his corruption.

Having "learnt his lesson" from his failure against the group of heroes that had bested him in Travincal decades prior, Mephisto made a "bigger play" by taking Akarat's persona. Whereas he had previously corrupted Zakarum from within, this time, the entire world would be his stage as he played the role of the prophet.(#cite_note-HatredDeepDiveNarrative-41) To this end, he created the Hands of Akarat, which would do his dirty work, while he continued to play the role of "Akarat."

As "Akarat," Mephisto gathered a 'flock' in a remarkably short amount of time, as he promised to not only purify their lands, but their very souls. Such was people's desparation for a better world, even the most cunning Warriors found themselves in his thrall, becoming his disciples.

#### Hatred Rising

"Beware false prophets donned as sheep yet bearing the stench of wolves. Thou shalt know them by their fruits. For a tree of good cannot produce evil, nor thorns yield a harvest sweet and fair."

Mephisto's influence continued to corrupt areas of Estuar. In accordance to his pact with Eru, he spared Nahantu and the Spirit Realm. Realmwalkers in service to Mephisto emerged in the mortal realm, spawning[monsters.

#### Dawn of Hatred

"A wandering prophet, Akarat leads his followers throughout Sanctuary to share his teachings and encourage peace and love through devotion to the Light — condemning violence and greed. Thousands of years ago, Akarat’s teachings formed the foundation of the Zakarum faith, though the church has turned away from Akarat and now believes the man currently wandering Sanctuary to be a false prophet. Unknown to the world, they are right. After spending centuries lying in the Spirit Realm, Akarat’s body was stolen and possessed by the prime evil Mephisto, Lord of Hatred. Using Akarat’s body, Mephisto hopes to mold a new age in Sanctuary — the Age of Hatred."

Mephisto gathers followers

As Akarat, Mephisto claimed to have returned to spread peace, love, and the Light. In the vein of a wandering prophet, Mephisto led his followers throughout Sanctuary, sharing his teachings and encouraging peace and love through devotion to the Light — condemning violence and greed. Many truly believed Akarat to have returned, though ironically, the Zakarum church was not among them, correctly concluding that this "Akarat" was a false prophet (though not necessarily aware that Mephisto was using his body). However, they couldn't assassinate "Akarat" directly, as they knew that doing so would make him a martyr. Despite his outwardly benign appearance, Mephisto intended to use Akarat's body to mold a new age in Sanctuary. The Age of Hatred.

Regardless, Mephisto continued to play the role. He continued to heal the sick and feed the hungry. In one case, Selviar of the Fox Tribe was brought before him. Despite the longstanding enmity between the Barbarians and Zakarum, Selviar was willing to be healed. As "Akarat," Mephisto did so, and Selviar pledged her tribe to serve him. The Fox Tribe became some of Mephisto's most loyal followers. While "Akarat" claimed peace and love, violence and suspicion shadowed his every step.

#### Sins of the Horadrim

"Is it true you put down Mephisto's dog? As you did my Amalgam. Ah. How cruel. I wondered where the beast was. There is some other wretched thing guarding my cell. Mephisto imprisoned me, you see. A just reward for my betrayal."

In Cerrigar, the Wanderer encountered a priest named Yvaine who had claimed to have met Akarat, who she called "the Prophet." The priest and the gathered crowd both ignored the Wanderer when they warned them they were being lied to and that they had witnessed Akarat's corpse. The priest countered by saying that Akarat himself preached the importance of doubt, and encouraged the people to take a pilgrimage with her so they could see Akarat as she had, and "drink of his miracles" as she had.

The Wanderer later learns that many families in Cerrigar have packed up their belongings and are heading west on a pilgrimage to go to Akarat.

As Bryona and the Wanderer attempted to deal with Astaroth's ever-burning blood, they eventually came to realize the only solution was to summon Astaroth himself. By touching the Horadric relics containing Astaroth's blood, the demon was able to psychically communicate with the Wanderer, and he revealed that he had been imprisoned by Mephisto as punishment for betraying him to help Lilith. As they prepared to summon him into Sanctuary, Astaroth told the Wanderer that Mephisto had the power to prevent him from being summoned, however in this case Mephisto would allow it, solely due to the Wanderer's involvement. Astaroth believed this was because Mephisto would take joy in seeing him be slain by the Wanderer.

#### Infernal Chaos

As Mephisto continued to travel across Sanctuary, his presence tore at the fabric of reality. Chaos rushed in to fill the void, gnawing at reality.

#### On Nightmare's Wings

Mephisto's power continued to grow as he continued to travel Estuar.

#### Lord of Hatred

"The path to domination is paved with the souls of the willing. Soon, all of Sanctuary will be bathed in hatred. And none shall escape the flood. There will be those who believe that they alone can stand against the inevitable. And I invite them to try."


Mephisto's faithful continued to spread, turning devotion into devastation. Mephisto was able to win over his flock through performing genuine miracles, thereby earning devoted followers rather than enthralling them.

Mephisto leaves the archive in flames

Because of how long she had carried the soulstone containing his essence, Neyrelle remained mentally linked with Mephisto. He was able to track her down, confronting her in the Yshari Archive in Gea Kul. He taunted her with visions, stating that he wanted her to willingly submit to him, but if not, he had an eternity to break her will. Neyrelle revealed that she had seen inside Mephisto's mind. Inside his dreams, and the twisted horrors contained within. In all that chaos, in all his plans, she had found what Mephisto feared. Mephisto scoffed at the idea of him having to fear anything, but Neyrelle began to recite Rathma's Prophecy. This enraged Mephisto to the point he killed her by snapping her neck. Disappointed by such wasted potential, Mephisto soon caught on to the Prophecy's fragment where he beheld his defeat by "a spear of light piercing Hatred's heart." Undeterred, he left, claiming his victory was inevitable. He set the Archive ablaze as he left, intent on burning away any trace of what Neyrelle had found that might threaten him.

Having spent a period of time travelling across Estuar, all of the pieces were in place for Mephisto's plan. Confident in victory, he was happy to "play with his food" before moving on. In Gea Kul, he continued to play the role of kindly prophet, hailing all manner of wounds, treating the survivors of both Caldeum and Guulrahn. He spoke of a coming miracle; an eclipse that would herald the dawn of a new age. He sailed to Skovos to finish his quest to shatter mankind. His goal; corrupt the Pools of Creation and bathe the world in Hatred. A bonus of sorts for Mephisto was that by this point, Skovos was the last functioning human kingdom on Sanctuary. Ergo, he would tear it down. Furthermore, Askari culture was (and always had been) highly unified. Mephisto sought to prove that not even the Askari were immune from his ability to sunder bonds between mortals.

The Wanderer sailed after Mephisto. Mephisto used one of his Hands to summon merfolk and other aquatic creatures to attack the ship. Mephisto later spoke through the Hand, mocking the Wanderer, before sacrificing his servant to create a monstrous creature. The Wanderer defeated the creature, and the ship later reached Skovos.

#### Banishment to the Void

"Here it is. Victory.  So much effort simply to return where we began. You, alone. Just as I found you. You sacrificed everything, but changed nothing. I will be reborn in Hell, and you will be forgotten."  
"You're not going back to Hell."

As Mephisto laid defeated, he both congratulated and taunted the Wanderer of their victory, stating that for all their sacrifices and efforts to stop him, they changed nothing. With a final taunt, he declares he will simply be reborn in Hell in time while the Wanderer would be forgotten by everyone. However, with Lilith's blade in hand, the Wanderer revealed Mephisto was not going back to Hell and promptly stabbed his heart. In shock and pain, a fleshy veil is formed and pulls Mephisto behind it, covering him up as he sent into the Void.
