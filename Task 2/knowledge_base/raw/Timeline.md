# Timeline

The following is a **timeline** of the Diablo series. Absolute dates are based on the numbering system of the Anno Kehjistani.

## The Dawn

-   In the beginning, there is nothing, save a Pearl containing a dreaming, mighty, unfathomable spirit, the One, Anu.

-   At some point, Anu considers itself. Desiring perfection, it casts all evil from itself, creating the Prime Evil, Tathamet.

-   Now two separate beings, Anu and Tathamet war against each other within the Pearl for countless millennia, until finally exhausted from the battle, they both deliver their final blows. The resulting cataclysm destroys them both and lays the foundation for all Creation. Pandemonium marks the epicentre of this event.

## The Great Conflict

-   Anu's shining spine forms the Crystal Arch and the High Heavens form around it. Tathamet's corpse forms the basis for the Burning Hells. The two realms give birth to angels and demons respectively. Anu's eye becomes the Worldstone, an object of great power that can create entire worlds.

-   Angels and demons wage war over the Worldstone, the artifact changing hands many times. The Pandemonium Fortress is built to house it, but it too changes hands many times.

-   After eons of battle, the angel Inarius seeks to abandon the Great Conflict. He gathers like-minded angels and demons, most notably Lilith. His renegades steal the Worldstone and use it to create their own realm where they will be free of the conflict—Sanctuary.

-   The Great Conflict grinds to a halt, as without the Worldstone, Heaven and Hell are left without a goal.

## The Creation of Sanctuary

-   After forming Sanctuary, Inarius and Lilith mate and create offspring called nephalem. Other renegade angels and demons do likewise. Thus are born those who would later be known as the Ancients, from whom all humans are distant descendants.

-   The renegade angels and demons fear the power of the nephalem and consider exterminating them all. Lilith is driven into a mad frenzy at this prospect of her children being exterminated, so kills every single renegade angel and demon in Sanctuary except for her once lover Inarius.

-   Inarius is horrified by Lilith's actions but can't bring himself to kill her, so banishes her from Sanctuary into the Void.

-   Inarius twists the powers of the Worldstone to weaken the powers of the nephalem over the following generations until no special powers remain. Thus emerges the race of humanity, which bears no knowledge of its birthright.

## Legends of Sanctuary

-   Bul-Kathos fathers twin children, Naarah and Hiram. Hiram eventually leaves his people.

-   Hiram returns after seven years, sworn to Lilith. His army attacks Mount Arreat, intent on seizing the Worldstone. His attack is repelled, but at the cost of many Barbarian deaths. Bul-Kathos orders the unification of the tribes and the start of the Vigil. Work begins on what will become Harrogath, as the Barbarians swear to defend Mount Arreat and the Worldstone.

## The Birth of Civilization

-   Millennia pass and mankind begins to populate the world, giving rise to various civilizations.

### c. -2300

-   Recovered artifacts indicate that by this time, writing, art, and science had become an integral part of human culture. Modern scholars agree that this time marks the beginning of Kehjan, humanity's first civilization.

### c. -2200

-   The cultures of Kehjan formalize the study of magic. This leads to the formation and growth of the Mage Clans.

### c. -2100

-   The growing power of the clans leads to the formation of the Al'Raquish. It rules Kehjan alongside the kingdom's monarchy and trade guilds.

### \-1992

-   Jere Harash summons the first demon into Sanctuary. His fellow Vizjerei perfect this summoning art, establishing demonology and the enslavement of demons as the basis of their power. Consequently, the Prime Evils learn of the existence of Sanctuary and humanity's latent power.

### \-1880

-   The Temple of the Triune is founded as a front to spread the Prime Evils' influence. It steadily grows in power.

### \-1820

-   Inarius forms the Cathedral of Light to counter the Triune's growing influence.

### \-1819

-   A plague sweeps through Seram. Among the dead are the bulk of Uldyssian's family.

## The Sin War

### \-1809

-   An ideological battle between the Cathedral of Light and Triune begins. Thus begins the Sin War.

### Later Events

-   A simple farmer, Uldyssian, is dragged into the conflict between the Triune and Cathedral of Light cults, when Lilith, who somehow manages to return from the Void, unlocks the hidden nephalem powers within him. She plans to use him as a slave against Inarius.

-   Uldyssian and his followers give rise to the Edyrem. The group sets out to dismantle both the Triune and Cathedral and end their dominance. In the process, the High Heavens learn of the existence of Sanctuary and humanity.

-   The Triune and Cathedral are destroyed, Lilith cast back to the Void, and the Worldstone is altered, removing Inarius's 'blanket' over humanity's potential. However, the High Heavens invade Sanctuary, seeking to eradicate the "abomination" of humanity. The Burning Hells openly invade as well, and thus begins a three-way battle between the Heavenly Host, Demonic Legion, and Edyrem.

-   Uldyssian becomes more powerful than any angel or demon after unlocking his full nephalem power, but he realizes he is losing his humanity and decides to sacrifice himself to expel both Heaven and Hell from Sanctuary. The release of energy strips the Edyrem of their powers and resets the Worldstone to the state Inarius left it in.

-   The Angiris Council votes on the issue of Sanctuary. Tyrael, moved by Uldyssian's sacrifice, makes the deciding vote that spares both. The Council subsequently makes a pact with Mephisto that both Heaven and Hell will recognize Sanctuary as its own realm, that humanity will be left free to choose its own path between Order and Chaos. To seal the deal, Mephisto is given Inarius as a slave, to be tortured for all eternity.

-   Thus ends the Sin War. Only a few select individuals recall the events of Uldyssian's uprising.

## The Age of Magic

"The Age of Magic shone brightly but soon caught fire in the wars of the mages. Much of their splendor, lost to ash."

\- Anonymous

### \-1799

-   Believing the Sin War to merely be a clash of faiths, the majority of Kehjan's populace turn away from religion. The land is renamed Kehjistan in order to distance itself from the conflict.

-   More and more people turn to the Mage Clans for guidance. Thus begins the Age of Magic; a short lived golden age for the mortal realm.

-   Mages who retain their memories from the Sin War declare that no mage must ever again summon a demon into the world, that humanity must remain free of angelic and demonic influence. The Vizjerei, Ennead, and Ammuit clans are left as the dominant mage clans and suppress popular religions.

## The Mage Clan Wars

### \-210

### \-203

## The Age of Faith

-   The fallout from the conflict occurs as the people turn against the mages. Some mage clans (including the Vizjerei) survive but in diminished form. Numerous tomes of lore are destroyed, the order of the Viz-Jaq'taar is formed to deal with rogue mages, and laws are passed forbidding the use of magic. The Age of Magic thus comes to be replaced with the Age of Faith, and numerous new religions spring up.

-   Sometime during this period, the Bloodied Wolf ravages Teganze. It leaves disease and corruption in its wake, the Hollows spreading along its path. Teganze is reduced to desert, and Umbaru refugees are scattered across Sanctuary.

## The Rise of Zakarum

### 1025

-   The town of Tristram is established around the Horadric monastery. In the following years, the town attracts farmers and settlers from the region.

### 1042

-   Emperor Tassara of Kehjistan converts to the increasingly influential Zakarum faith, thus restoring his popularity amongst his restless subjects and stabilizing the region.

-   The empire's capital is moved to Kurast. Tassara codifies the Zakarum faith's core beliefs and elects the first Que-Hegan. Records describe the Zakarum as a bona fide church and organized religion from this point forward.

### 1045

-   After three years of codified Zakarum faith, Tassara continues to be overshadowed by his childhood friend and Zakarum zealot, Rakkis. After defeating a coup of renegade nobles who seek to overthrow the church, Rakkis becomes a living legend. He uses his influence to replace government officials with Zakarum bishops, upsetting the region's balance of power.

-   Wary of Rakkis's growing popularity, Tassara dispatches the general on a crusade to spread Zakarum to the lands of the West. With the departure of Rakkis and his most loyal forces, Tassara is able to secure his control over Kehjistan.

-   Around the time of Rakkis's departure, the cleric Akkhan forms the Crusader order. He sends them to the east to seek out a means to purify the Zakarum faith, as per his belief that the church had strayed from Akarat's teachings.

### 1060

-   After years of warring with the peoples of the West and North, Rakkis ends his journey by founding Westmarch and becoming the nation's first king. Just prior to this he discovers the ruins of Corvus, an ancient city of the Nephalem, and learns of humanity's ancestry.

### 1070

-   A decade after becoming king, Corvus continues to frustrate Rakkis in regards to his search for the nephalem's powers. Hearing of the existence of the Worldstone, he marches north to invade the Northern Steppes.

## The Zakarum Reformation

### 1150

The act is well received by the people and it sparks a surge in independent worship, secularism, and mysticism. However, the archbishops of the Zakarum High Council view these events as an erosion of the church's power but are unable to keep Zebulon in check.

-   Around this time (mid-12th century), the church dispatches clerics to the west to spread the word of Zakarum. The rigours of travel lead to the assignment of the Protectors of the Word to protect future missionaries.

This marks the start of the Zakarum Inquisition, as the church purges various sects and brutally suppresses other religions such as Skatsim.

### 1258

-   At the behest of the Zakarum High Council and urging of Lazarus, Leoric is sent to govern the land of Khanduras. He repurposes the Horadric monastery into a Zakarum cathedral and declares himself king.

-   Upon arriving at Tristram, Lazarus frees Diablo. Once freed, Diablo attempts to possess Leoric, but without success. However, Diablo is able to strip away the king's sanity, and over time, Leoric descends into madness.

### 1263

-   Adria arrives in Tristram.

-   Increasingly deranged, King Leoric begins imprisoning all who question his authority and executing them as traitors. He declares war on the kingdom of Westmarch.

-   The Seekers of the Light order falls into decline due to the chaos Leoric's actions are stirring.

-   Archbishop Lazarus kidnaps Leoric's youngest son, Prince Albrecht, to be used as a vessel for Diablo himself. Diablo's possession of Albrecht spawns a multitude of demons from Albrecht's worst nightmares and twists the deepest levels of the cathedral into a scene out of Hell itself.

-   The loss of Albrecht shatters what remains of Leoric's sanity, and Tristram is plunged into horror as the king seeks to torture and execute all he suspects of conspiring against him.

-   Lachdanan, captain of King Leoric's army, returns from the disastrous war with Westmarch and is forced to kill Leoric. The fallen monarch curses Lachdanan and his followers with his dying breath. Not long afterward, Lachdanan and the king's guards are attacked by Leoric, resurrected as the Skeleton King, as they attempt to bury him. The king's curse transforms Lachdanan and his men into the dreaded Hell Knights in the deepest parts of the cathedral.

-   Archbishop Lazarus leads a group of villagers into the cathedral on a mission to rescue Albrecht and abandons them to a bloodthirsty demon called The Butcher. Only a few survive.

-   Prince Aidan returns to Tristram. With his allies, he ventures into the depths of the cathedral.

-   The Butcher is defeated. Subsequently, so are the Skeleton King and Lazarus.

-   Aidan defeats Diablo, albeit at the cost of Albrecht's life. Aidan tries to contain Diablo within himself through the shattered soulstone, but fails to fully contain the demon.

-   Adria seeks out Aidan, seeking to console him following the ordeal of the Labyrinth. She sees the power of Diablo within the young warrior, and pledges herself to his service, giving herself to him in the encounter that would lead to the conception of her daughter, Leah.

-   Adria and Gillian leave for Caldeum.

-   Aidan becomes the Dark Wanderer, leaving Tristram behind for the East, shortly before legions of foul demons attack and destroy the town and its inhabitants.

## The Reunion of the Three

### 1264

-   The daughter of James is born sometime this year.

-   Adria gives birth to Leah during this year. She leaves her in the care of Gillian in Caldeum and promptly disappears.

They destroy Blood Raven, a Rogue who accompanied Aidan into the labyrinth of Tristram and was corrupted by the demoness Andariel, and open a portal to Tristram to destroy the demons and rescue Deckard Cain. The heroes defeat the demoness Andariel, whose forces have overrun the monastery of the Sisterhood of the Sightless Eye and corrupted and killed many Rogues, before journeying east, following the Wanderer.

-   With the help of Kara Nightshadow, Norrec frees himself from the armour. At around the same time, the Wanderer arrives at Lut Gholein.

-   The heroes journey to Lut Gholein, where they destroy the demon lord Duriel within the Tomb of Tal Rasha and rescue the archangel Tyrael; it is discovered that Baal is already free from his soulstone.

-   The Dark Wanderer completes his transformation into Diablo; Mephisto, and the corrupted Zakarum high priesthood are defeated beneath the jungles of Kurast by the band of heroes.

-   The heroes journey into the Pandemonium Fortress, where they defeat Izual, the betrayer of the High Heavens, and storm Hell itself. Diablo is defeated in the Chaos Sanctuary, and his soulstone is destroyed, along with Mephisto's, upon the Hellforge.

-   A mercenary company led by Kentril Dumon, hired by a Vizjerei Mage named Quov Tsin, enters the fabled city of Ureh. Along with the Necromancer Zayl, Dumon discovers and survives the city's dark secrets.

### 1265

-   Cornelius the Younger perishes from a spider bite, King Cornelius dies a few months after from illness and Justinian IV is crowned.

-   Baal and his army march on Mount Arreat.

-   The heroes follow Baal to Mount Arreat for the final conflict between man and the last of the Prime Evils. Although the heroes defeat Baal, his demonic army continues to advance.

-   The angel Tyrael discovers that Baal has succeeded in corrupting the Worldstone housed within Mount Arreat. Realizing that the Worldstone cannot be restored, Tyrael reluctantly destroys it. The resulting explosion destroys Mount Arreat, ravages much of the countryside, and decimates Baal's army.

-   A large shard of the Worldstone crashes into the sea near Stormpoint, and is swept up to a tidal cavern beneath its prison.

-   The heroes that have defeated the Prime Evils return to their homes and families. Cain, knowing that Sanctuary is now open thanks to the destruction of the Worldstone, begins searching for a means to save the mortal realm.

-   Zayl saves Westmarch from the machinations of Astrogha.

-   Deckard Cain, with the help of an adventurer seemingly banishes Y'kkathra. On a road outside the town, a merchant picks up an ornate mirror and adds it to his merchandise.

-   With Baal's defeat, Skarn beholds a power vacuum he can take advantage of. Over the next few years, he forms and directs the Cult of Damnation, which seeks to gather Worldstone shards.

-   Over the years, merchants and adventurers are drawn to the ruins of Tristram, having heard tales of the treasures that lie within its cathedral. The structure is stripped of all valuables. New Tristram, having been built on this practice, falls into ignominy.

-   The Blood Knights emerge from the shadows and engage the forces of Hell directly.

-   Construction on training grounds inside Westmarch is concluded.

-   In dark corners of Westmarch, the people whisper of change. The streets bristle with tension.

-   Cain departs for a journey along with the Paladin Akarat, to pursue rumors pertaining to the End of Days.

-   The Shard-seeker departs for the Ancients' Cradle along with Zatham, in order to stop the Cult of Terror. The events culminate in the release of Baal and Tyrael from the Worldstone shard. Baal is defeated by the Shard-seeker, and Tyrael ends up as a spectre within the Shard-seeker's mind.

-   With the destruction of the Pathstone, the mists around the Ancients' Cradle begin to fade.

-   Bailey buys an antique mirror to hang in his tavern. One of his customers is influenced by the mirror and falls into a rage. The situation is diffused and—with instructions from one of Cain's journals—the Shard-seeker banishes the demonic influence from the mirror.

-   A coalition of demons invade Sanctuary. Their power rendered the earth, creating fissures from which influence corrupted surroundind wildlife. They were hunted down by the Shard-seeker.

-   The people of Wortham start having strange dreams, and some disappear in the night. The Cult of Terror meanwhile arrives at the ruins of Tristram Cathedral. Despite the efforts of the Shard-seeker, they are re able to deliver the stone to their master, who uses its power to start reconstituting his body.

-   The masses of Westmarch have grown restless. Time and again, their concerns are ignored by their monarch. This leads to champions rising up amongst Westmarch's citizens to revolt against the injustice.

-   Demons loyal to Baal gather in an attempt to resurrect their master.

-   Cain takes Leah under his wing. The two foil the plans of Belial, who has since entered the mortal realm. However, it is a temporary setback, and Belial remains undeterred in his efforts.

-   Shardstorms surge from the Dreadlands to most of the Western continent. The storms ravaged the landscape, attracting monsters which were corrupted into Shardborne.

## The Age of Unmaking

### 1273

-   Night Howlers in the Dark Wood infiltrate adventuring parties, sabotaging them and causing confusion.

-   Diablo sets his sights on Sanctuary, and musters an invasion force to tear away the fabric of the mortal realm. Meanwhile on Sanctuary, foreboding creeps across the world as thousands find their dreams plagued by unexplained nightmares. The dread of the afflicted gives way to madness. Tales of self-mutilation and depraved crimes spread like silent fire.

-   In shadowed places across the world. the air rends as hellish maws burst out of the world's topsoil, and swathes of Sanctuary are turned into nightmarish hellscapes. Heroes charge bravely into the rifts hoping to seal them before the evils within flood the land.

-   Setting aside their long-standing enmity, the Immortals and Shadows put out contracts on their shared enemy.

-   A mysterious creature emerges from the Dark Wood. The local townsfolk name it "Bramblestalker."

-   The Tempests sail from the Cold Isles, ready to stand against Hell's impending invasion.

-   Across Sanctuary, Treasure Goblins become restless, multiplying and dashing with overflowing bags of riches.

-   Diablo's manifestation in Sanctuary draws near; demons pave the way through dark deeds, in preparation for his arrival. Sanctuary's greatest minds frantically search for a way to escape the coming doom, leading to discoveries of new power.

-   Fish in the waters of the Shimmering Tropic begin to attract monsters.

-   Something drives numerous warriors of Sanctuary into a frenzy, causing them to turn on each other.

#### Late Year

-   Having retrieved El'druin from the Dreadlands, the Shard-seeker is tormented by their inability to wield it, at least in part due to the effects of exposure to corrupted Worldstone shards. To contain the evil festering within, and to prepare for their confrontation with Diablo, the Shard-seeker travels to the Cold Isles.

-   Hordes of monsters amass in Lidwinter Woods. Looking at the seemingly endless ranks of invaders, adventurers band together in order to fend off this threat to Sanctuary.

-   Autumn nears its end. Defeated demonic forces lick their wounds and gather strength for a future assault, while the people of Sanctuary, having reaped their fields, prepare for winter's chill.

-   Diablo finishes reconstituting his body. He moved his forces to the World's Crown in preparation for the Unmaking of Sanctuary. Diablo's presence and the activities of his forces warped the land, creating pyramids of corpses, lakes of blood, fouled pools of acid and inverted rain.

-   Diablo uses his Worldstone shard to enthrall the entirety of the Shardborne.

-   Amongst Diablo's forces are some of his strongest allies; the Dreadbirth.

-   Verathiel finally breaks to Diablo's torment. She is turned into a weapon essential to Diablo's plans to unmake Sanctuary.

-   Dark mists spread from the World's Crown to some of Sanctuary northern regions including the Dreadlands, Frozen Tundra and Ivgorod.

-   The Shard-seeker amasses an army including factions like the Immortals, Shadows,' Barbarian of Bitter Hearth, Veradani of the Sanctified Earth Monastery, rogues of the Sisterhood of the Sightless Eye, soldiers of Staalbreak and some Xiansai mages.

### 1274

-   After months of sailing they arrive at the World's Crown, only to be shipwrecked by a veil of darkness near the shore.

-   As the army of humanity's defenders approach the heart of Diablo's realm, he forces Verathiel to sing a Song of Unmaking. Amplified in potency and range by a shard of the Worldstone and a demonic monolith, each tone of the song turns the living to ash, and the fabric of Sanctuary begins to unravel in its wake.

-   The effects of the Unmaking affect the northern regions of Sanctuary, including the Frozen Tundra and Southern Dreadlands. Fortunately, the spread Unmaking is halted after Verathiel is killed/purified.

-   The Shard-seeker eventually kills Diablo and destroy his shard, reversing the Unmaking.

-   After that, the Shard-seeker sets out on a pilgrimage. Their first stop is a cave somewhere in Aranoch, where they keep El'druin. The sword will later be wielded by Jacob Staalek.

-   The Sisterhood of the Sightless Eye return to Eastgate. Unknown to them, Andariel's influence lingers there, and their trauma from the war serves as a feast for it.

## Epoch of Madness

-   Prima Helwig of the Untameable Wilds calls for for a convocation of all witches of the Sharval Wilds.

-   Now free from Diablo's control, Albrecht leads the Shardborne into the Sharval Wilds, intent on making it their home.

-   At round about the same time, fey spirits emerge from Sharval in record numbers, abducting numerous humans and consuming entire villages. The fey refuse all attempts to appease them.

-   Rumors begin to spread in Sharval of a hooded Prophet traveling the land, healing the lame and sick, and breathing new life into the land.

-   Corruption begins to spread through Sharval Wilds. With the intervention of the Shard-seeker, two clashing factions; the Untameable Wilds and the Talons of Ifeh, are made to cooperate to solve the issue.Prima Helwig gives her life to stem the corruption. In the aftermath, both factions come to a truce.

-   Westmarch seeks to restock its armory.

-   Tensions run high in Sharval as clashes between its people deepen wounds inflicted on the very land they stand upon. As desperation mounts, the citizens of Highfork, a town in the Sharval Wilds, seek aid wherever it may present itself.

-   The conflict in Sharval causes the Bloomhollow fey to awaken.

-   The Harvest draws near as all of Sharval prepares for a bountiful reaping.

-   Albrecht's supply of Worldstone splinters run low, limiting his ability to create new Shardborne. In response to this, he constructs a massive flesh hive, within which humans can be transformed into more Shardborne.

-   After the destruction of the hive, Albrecht and Lethes search for the resting place of Aughild.

-   While demons still riddle the countryside of Sharval, the destitute farmers still manage to eek out a meager harvest, even as their numbers continue to fall.

-   Albrecht leads his growing army of Shardborne through the populous areas of Entsteig. The country's capital is besieged.

-   The militia of Ensteig are barely able to hold back the Shardborne with the help of, the Untameable Wilds, Talons of Ifeh and the the Shard-seeker. Albrecht is defeated, but uses his dying breath to create a realm to be a haven for the Shardborne.

-   A coup occurs in the aftermath, and King Isbrand, is deposed. Monarchy was replaced with a ruling council consisting of people from all works of life.

### 1275

-   Mishka is born.

## Nation in Agony

-   With the departure of Shardborne, the effects of the shattered Worldstone on Sanctuary begin to fade into memory.

-   A wave of unsettling disappearances ripples outward from Sanctuary’s port cities. Entire communities vanish without warning, leaving only fear and unanswered questions in their wake.

-   Anguish begins to take root across Sanctuary. As the corruption spreads, Sanctuary's treasures change and old horrors learn new ways to torment the living.

-   The Eastgate Monastery is beset by horrific manifestations, while beyond the walls of Lut Gholein, demonic forces gather beneath the desert sun.

-   Corvus is once again besieged by demonic forces.

-   Lut Gholein is conquered by Andariel and occupied by demonic forces. She uses it as a base to send out barges and abduct people.

### 1278

-   Sahmantha Halstaff is born.

### 1281

-   Jagged Liang becomes the advisor for the Great Families in Zhou. She reforms the city watch, turning it into her personal army. What follows is four years of ever-escalating violence between her and the Tenth Family.

-   **10th of Jerharan**: Monk Kislek of the Scholars of Orbei reports that the order's sacramental wine has grown weak. Accuses Monk Meston of drinking the stores and replacing the loss with water.

### 1284

-   Jacob and Shanar part ways.

-   Ralyn Halstaff is born.

## The End of Days

### 1285

-   After twenty years, Tyrael succeeds in regaining his physical form. He returns to the High Heavens, and summons El'druin to his side.

-   Jacob is left despondant at El'druin's disappearance. Shanar leaves him soon afterwards.

-   Hakan II withdraws the support of Caldeum from the Borderlands. Open to the deprivations of the Coven, refugees begin streaming into Caldeum. Hakan sends the Iron Wolves out into the wastes to battle the Coven, while the Imperial Guard is created to "protect" the citizens of Caldeum from the refugees.

-   Abd al-Hazir departs Caldeum to record the wonders and perils of the world. He spends months travelling before finally returning home, shaken by his experiences.

-   Samuel Drest is executed for his murder of Marlowe.

-   Lidra and Rigley transport a demon across Aranoch. The demon possesses Lidra, but Rigley kills her.

-   Kehr Odwyll returns to the Dreadlands.

-   The demon Valdraxxis plagues the towns of both Holbrook and Havenwood before he is slain by Valla. Afterwards, she heads for New Tristram, having heard that a star will fall upon the town in seven days time.

#### Late Year

-   Autumn/Winter: Tyrael is held to account for his actions by Imperius. He takes on mortal form and descends to Sanctuary, plummeting into the old cathedral as a falling star. The power of the fallen star causes the dead to rise around New Tristram and Leoric rises once more as the Skeleton King, swearing to make all suffer as he has suffered.

-   Zhota guides Mishka through the Gorgorra.

-   Li-Ming leaves Caldeum, pursuing the fallen star.

-   Covetous Shen leaves Xiansai, after warning Jia that civil war is looming for the island nation.

-   The Nephalem arrives in New Tristram. With the aid of Leah and Deckard Cain, the Skeleton King and undead are silenced and Tyrael is saved, albeit at the cost of Cain's life at the hands of Maghda. They subsequently head east to deal with Belial.

-   The group arrives in Caldeum. They track down Maghda and the Coven to the ruined city of Alcarnus and destroy them to avenge Deckard Cain, then rescue Leah from the grip of the Imperial Guard, who are serpent demons of Belial. They escape into the sewers, where they learn that Leah's mother, Adria, is alive, and rescue her from Belial's minions.

-   With the aid of Adria, the heroes are able to recover the Black Soulstone from Zoltun Kulle. After saving the people of the city from the assault of Belial, they penetrate into the Imperial Palace, defeat Belial, and use the Black Soulstone to imprison him.

-   Azmodan, now the last lord of Hell, reveals to Leah that his army will emerge from the Arreat Crater, prompting the group to head north.

-   Azmodan's armies besiege Bastion's Keep, but thanks to the heroes, the attack is turned back. The heroes subsequently take the fight to Azmodan, defeat him, and imprison him in the Black Soulstone.

-   Adria reveals her true colours, turning the Black Soulstone on Leah and awakening Diablo's presence within her. The Great Evils become one, Diablo now a singular Prime Evil. He departs for the High Heavens, with the heroes again in pursuit.

-   Diablo besieges Heaven, but again, the heroes are the deciding factor. Diablo himself is cast down and the Heavens saved. Tyrael returns to his brethren, taking on the aspect of Wisdom and declaring that Heaven and Sanctuary shall stand together.

-   Months after his travels, late in the year, al-Hazir is abducted by the Coven. He is taken to Sescheron.

## The Great Enmity

-   In the aftermath of Malthael's defeat, humanity is beset by religious schisms, war, and famine, such is the death toll of the Reapers. At the same time, Heaven, having also suffered, shutters its gates to the realms beyond.

-   Tyrael, Lorath Nahr, and Donan set sail to Skovos to discover the fate of the Horadrim sent there years ago, and unearth a Horadric vault left on the islands. However, the Askari are beset by the Drowned, and have little time for the affairs of mainlanders.

-   Tyrael departs from the Horadric Vault for reasons unknown and leaves Lorath Nahr in charge of the Horadrim but eventually the group falls apart without the angel's leadership.

-   The Great Enmity descends on Sanctuary; a time of strife between kings and warlords.

-   Decades after Malthael's defeat, Sanctuary has been reduced to a bleak and shattered hellscape. Demons roam with impunity, and Sanctuary's people huddle in the darkness, struggling to survive demon and fellow Man alike.

-   The empire of Kehjistan collapses due to demonic invasion and various wars. Hakan II orders the gates of Caldeum sealed in a bid to weather the storm.

-   The Drowned invade the coasts of Sanctuary. They successfully conquer the entire coast of Scosglen.

## The Age of Hatred

### 1336

Main article: Diablo IV


-   The Knights Penitent garrison at Yelesna stockpiles supplies in preparation for the winter.

-   Lilith is summoned back into the mortal realm. Her return spawns a new wave of cultists and worshippers. Thus begins the Age of Hatred.

-   Lorath Nahr is visited by a young scholar on many a night. Lorath narrates tales of the history of Sanctuary and the realms beyond.

-   With the death of Inarius, the Cathedral of Light is rocked by a schism. While Prava retains control of the Cathedral proper, the schism leads to the creation of the Burnt Knights.

-   After the Wanderer abdicates their choice to Neyrelle and has her decide to use the Soulstone on Lilith or Mephisto, Neyrelle decides Mephisto is the greater threat and captures him in the Soulstone. Neyrelle sets off on her own, and as she journeys across Sanctuary to find a way to deal with Mephisto she is being unwillingly transformed into an agent of pure chaos and hatred. She leaves letters for Lorath Nahr, who she knows is partaking on his own journey, in case they cross paths.

-   After losing the Horadric Vault to the Cathedral of Light, Lorath Nahr begins writing a book he intends to give to Neyrelle and anyone else who may need the wisdom of the Horadrim. Lorath travels across Sanctuary to collect relics in order to create protective wards for his book and guard it from those with demonic or dark intent. When the book is finished, Lorath vows to find Neyrelle and help her no matter the cost.

-   After Lilith's death, her malignancy spreads through Sanctuary, creating the Malignant. Cormond finds a way to counter their dark power, but the adventurers he allies with steadily fall in battle. In desparation, he writes to the Wanderer, requesting aid.

-   More demons manifest on Sanctuary following Lilith's defeat, ushering in a new era of death and wanton destruction.

-   Zir and his fellow vampires awaken from their long slumber. Zir orders the Blood Seekers to conquer Scosglen, Hawezar, and the Dry Steppes. Erys spends the following months tracking the Blood Seekers.

-   Zir's vampire plague spreads through the Fractured Peaks. Ignored by the Cathedral of Light, it reaches epidemic conditions. Erys seeks a new way to fight them. Ultimately, Zir is slain, and the Blood Harvest is stopped thanks to the efforts of numerous adventurers.

#### Late Year

-   The Midwinter Blight festival is revived by the people of Kyovashad.

-   A mysterious phenomenon manifests throughout the shrines of Sanctuary, enchanting them with immense and prosperous power. Celebrators believe this to be their ancestors, blessing them from beyond the grave to celebrate this joyous occasion.

-   Inspired by the Blood Harvest, the legions of Hell initiate a new type of Helltide.

-   Endless hordes of demons gather in the Realm of Hatred, growing more powerful with each passing day. The Wanderer is forced to enter Hell and confront the Fell Council.

-   Still carrying Mephisto's soulstone, Neyrelle enters Nahantu. Monsters twisted by Mephisto's influence begin to appear in Sanctuary, and the Zakarum Remnants are decimated by his corruption.

-   With the aid of Eru, Mephisto acquires Akarat's corpse which he makes his new vessel. The Age of Hatred is protracted by Mephisto's re-emergence in Sanctuary.

### 1337

-   Mephisto's influence continues to affect Sanctuary. Realmwalkers in service to the Lord of Hatred emerge in Sanctuary, twisting areas of Estuar with his Hatred.

-   In accordance to his pact with Eru, Mephisto spares Nahantu and the Spirit Realm.

-   The Witches of Hawezar discover that many of the captive heads of the Tree of Whispers are missing. The heads travel Sanctuary to seek bodies. The Wanderer is summoned by the Witches to uncover the mystery of the missing heads.

-   Rumors spread of Akarat's return, while in truth, his body continues to be possessed by Mephisto.

-   Belial makes a bid for Sanctuary. He sends Apparition Monsters—illusory foot soldiers to sew his lies into reality.

-   Chaos Rifts begin appearing all over Sanctuary, heralding the return of Bartuc.

-   The Lesser Evils work in concert to conquer Sanctuary.
