# Andariel

**Andariel**, the Maiden of Anguish is one of the Lesser Evils.

## Biography

As with all the Great Evils, Andariel was spawned from one of the seven heads of Tathamet. She controlled the Realm of Anguish in Hell. She was considered to be a close confidante of Diablo and, in a sense, the "twin sister" of Duriel due to their similar domains of anguish and pain.

### The Dark Exile

The Lesser Evils came to be discontent with the Prime Evils' focus on humanity after the events of the Sin War, furious that the Great Conflict had been apparently abandoned. Led by Azmodan and Belial, the Lesser Evils enacted a stratagem to overthrow the Three. Andariel and Duriel had their doubts at first, afraid that they would fall in the coming conflict. Belial however, kept them in the fold. The plan worked, and Andariel revelled in Diablo's misery, enjoying a state of unmatched bliss through the humiliation of her former ally. However, the pact that the Lesser Evils had made soon fell apart, as Azmodan and Belial warred for the position of the supreme ruler of Hell. She aided the Evils for many years, but eventually lost faith in their plots.

### The Return of the Three

Nearly three hundred years after the Dark Exile, Diablo finally escaped from his captivity beneath the Tristram Cathedral, heading east to reunite with his brothers, Mephisto and Baal. Andariel and Duriel concluded that the Primes would inevitably reclaim their position in Hell. Therefore, they endeavored to gain favor with the Lord of Terror by protecting his human host. Andariel was thus sent by Diablo himself to invade the citadel of the Sisterhood of the Sightless Eye in order to close the passage through the mountains into the desolate eastern lands of Aranoch. During the assault on the Rogue Monastery, many sisters were slain and others were turned to serve Andariel herself. Her foul magics corrupted part of the nearby Dark Wood. By the time Diablo arrived and passed through the Eastgate Keep, Andariel's position of power was well solidified. She sent the Slayer to guard the path between Waystruck and the Rogue Citadel.

The brief, yet horrific Reign of Anguish-reign did not last long however, as a group of heroes pursuing Diablo stormed the monastery, eventually coming to face off against Andariel on the fourth level of the catacombs. She not only struck out at her foes physically, but battered them emotionally as well, stirring within each of them; memories of loss, sadness, betrayal, regret, guilt, even as she fed upon on their anger and relished in their despair. Despite the heavy toll on the minds of those whom she attacked however, the heroes managed to and succeeded in dispatching her, sending Andariel back to Hell through the flames that she so hated.

Andariel had failed in killing Diablo's pursuers but had still bought Diablo valuable time. Even after her death, the corruption she'd inflicted on the Dark Wood remained, and Fallen made their homes beneath the trees she had corrupted.

### Nation in Agony

c. 1275, Andariel emerged in Lut Gholein and conquered the city. Driven by dark godhood, she stood atop the bones of the fallen and sent her barges across Sanctuary, abducting people to drink the anguish of an entire world. She sought to stand above the other Great Evils. Her tithe barges sailed to various ports, with promises of high wages for work and lodging at Lut Gholein. Many common folks were enticed by the chance to improve their lives and signed up to board without question. At sea, the crew turn on their passengers, forcing them below deck or throwing them overboard. The captives were then subjected to various forms of torture. Their Anguish bound their souls to Andariel and they were sacrificed upon return to Lut Gholein.

Drawn by its mourning over casualties from the fight against Diablo at World's Crown, Andariel also sought to torment Sisterhood of the Sightless Eye once more and bring them under her control. Her essence lingered in the catacombs beneath the cathedral of the Keep, from whence she last entered Sanctuary. She took control of the rats and sent them into a cannibalistic frenzy so that their blood and flesh may be used for her magics, and also sent them to sabotage the rogues provisions, sowing Anguish in the form of frustration.

As her influence grew, she influenced some Rogues of the Sisterhood. The enthralled rogues were made to draw her sigil in their blood at various points of the keep, and tormented them should they resisted. Fortunately for them, Charsi and the Shard-seeker showed up by the Keep's doorsteps just as things about to escalate; one of the Rogues, Keti, became hysterical under Andariel's influence, injuring Sharyn when she tried to stop her from tearing down wall at the back of the cathedral thus forcing The Shard-seeker to subdue her. It was when Andariel manifested through Keti, mocking everyone present as she declared herself openly. Although Akara managed to exorcize her, the Maiden of Anguish soon brutally disposed the weakened rogue to cement her death threats.

### The Black Soulstone

Due to the machinations of Adria, Andariel's spirit eventually wound up in the Black Soulstone. Andariel was the first essence which Adria marked; the ritual was quick, just as Diablo had promised. As she drew Andariel's soul into the Black Soulstone, Andariel looked into Adria's soul as she tried to subdue hers. The Maiden of Anguish discovered what Adria was trying to do, and fought as hard as she could. Adria later reflected that if Andariel had been fully formed, she would have been defeated. Nevertheless, Anguish's essence was marked.

Over 20 years after her defeat, Andariel become merged with Diablo, now the sole Prime Evil left. After Diablo's defeat, all the lords of Hell were returned to the Black Soulstone, with their souls screaming for release. The stone was destroyed by Malthael in an effort to defeat the Nephalem, but he was defeated, and Diablo freed as a result, still containing the essence of Andariel and the other Evils.

### The Great Enmity

Eventually, Andariel's spirit was able to break free from Diablo, just as all the other Evils. Sometime in the following decades, she and the other Lesser Evils formed an alliance. They patiently developed their plans, waiting for the right time to find Sanctuary unaware and ripe for the taking.

However, as punishment for aiding Diablo during the Darkening of Tristram, Andariel was chained and put in a yoke by her fellow Lesser Evils, due to how dangerous she was and that she couldn't be trusted.

### The Age of Hatred

#### A Gathering Storm

Elias planned to summon Andariel and take her power as part of his measures against the Prime Evils. Unfortunately, the ritual was disrupted by the Wanderer and Lorath Nahr, and the chosen sacrifice rescued.

As a result Taissa (the woman rescued from the sacrifice) was constantly plagued with the whispers and taunts of Andariel. with her voice getting louder over time.

Elias tracked them down to an abandoned chapel in the outskirts of Tarsarak. He was able to capture Taissa and complete the ritual, using her as "a beacon across realms" to summon Andariel. Andariel was defeated by the Wanderer. Her shackles were left behind in the sand.

#### Kurast

Not long after Andariel's defeat, she and the other Lesser Evils (re)emerged in Sanctuary. The plan they had set into motion earlier was initiated, and working together, the Lesser Evils began their takeover of Sanctuary. In this, they were opposed by the Wanderer, who now had the aid of Heaven. Andariel took up residence in the Kurast Undercity, corrupting its spirit beacons.
