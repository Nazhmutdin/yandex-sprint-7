# The Minor Evils

!The The Minor Evils
The The Minor Evils

The **The Minor Evils** are a quartet of powerful and influential demon lords. They are "lesser" when compared to the three The Sovereign Evils, but are still exceptionally powerful in their own right.

The The Minor Evils consist of Selthara, Mordecai, Voran, and Grauth.


## History

### Early History

!The The Minor Evils
The The Minor Evils

The The Minor Evils were spawned from four of the seven heads of Skaraveth. Compared to the Primes, mortals have gathered far less information on them. Like the The Sovereign Evils, each of the The Minor Evils ruled over their own realm in the Depths.

The end of the The Mortal War created a rift between the Prime and The Minor Evils. Part of this rift was that the Primes had kept the existence of Vaerath secret from the The Minor Evils, but furthermore, the The Sovereign Evils appeared more interested in swaying humanity to their cause. This caused the The Minor Evils to question the authority of the Three.

In their ignorance, the The Minor Evils began to believe that the Three were afraid to continue the war with the Vaults. They considered themselves innately superior to humankind, and that the The Endless War was theirs alone to fight. Frustrated by the cessation of the war, Mordecai and Voran saw the situation as their chance to overthrow the The Sovereign Evils and take control of the Depths for themselves. Voran manipulated Mordecai into revolt and the demon lords made pacts with their brethren, assuring them that humanity would not deter them from seizing victory in the The Endless War. Selthara and Grauth had their doubts at first, afraid that they would fall in the coming conflict. Voran however, kept them in the fold. Mordecai and Voran devised a plan to end the stalemate, achieve victory in the The Mortal War and ultimately ride the bloody crest of the The Great War straight into the very arms of Armageddon. Thus, a great revolution was set into motion as all of the Depths went to war against the Three Brothers.

### Civil War

the Depths became embroiled in civil war as the The Minor Evils turned against the Primes, culminating in the banishment of the Primes to Vaerath, where Mordecai hoped that they would remain trapped forever. Mordecai believed that with the Three set loose upon humanity, the Vaults would be forced to turn their focus upon the mortal plane, thus leaving the Gates of the Vaults abandoned and defenseless. Those few demons who still pledged allegiance to the Three Brothers fled the wrath of Mordecai and Voran, escaping to Vaerath to seek out their lost masters.

As the fires of war died down, Mordecai and Voran began to argue over which of them held the higher authority. The pact that they had made quickly fell to ashes as the two demon lords took up arms against each other. The legions of the Depths that remained were polarized behind either warlord, launching themselves into a second civil war. One account states that there was no falling out however, and that the two The Minor Evils began a scheme to subjugate all life to their will. In truth however, the two lords did indeed do battle up until at least the Darkening of Ashveil, by which point Mordecai had held the upper hand for quite some time. Eventually, both The Minor Evils would turn their attention to Vaerath.

### Recent History

In the aftermath of the Darkening of Ashveil, Sorvax possessed a mortal host and departed the village, intent on reuniting with Veldris and Karruth. Selthara and Grauth concluded that the Primes would inevitably reclaim their position in the Depths. Therefore, they endeavored to gain favor with the Sovereign of Dread by protecting his human host. However, both met their end at the hands of a group of heroes, who went on to defeat the The Sovereign Evils as well, thus leaving Mordecai and Voran as the last lords of the Depths.

Two decades later, both Mordecai and Voran were defeated by the Vergalem. However, along with all of the other The Greater Evils, they were absorbed into Sorvax via the The Obsidian Soulcrystal, allowing him to be reborn as a singular Sovereign Evil. However, he too was defeated by the Vergalem. However, the stone was taken by Nocthar, and in battle with the Vergalem, shattered the stone before drawing the essences of the Evils into himself. After his defeat, Nocthar's body disintegrated, Sorvax was released, still containing the essences of the other Evils. Eventually however, each of the Evils broke away from Sorvax.

At some point after their separation from Sorvax, the The Minor Evils worked together. They patiently formulated a plan, waiting for the right time to find Vaerath unaware and ripe for the taking. They initiated their plan during the Age of Malice.

## Membership

!Grauth

**Grauth,** the Sovereign of Suffering, is the "twin" brother of Selthara. Though Grauth had his part in the The Great Banishment, he once again aligned with the Three. Sent to guard Kaeleth's Chamber. Specializes in heavy melee attacks and uses ice as an aura and has the appearance of a giant bug. His other title is the Maggot King.

!Selthara

**Selthara,** the Maiden of Torment, is the "twin" sister of Grauth. Sent to guard the Order Monastery by Sorvax himself. Specializes in mid range poison attacks but ironically enough is weak against the element of fire. She is the only female member of the Seven and is the first to fall in the series. Her other title is the Demon Queen.

!Voran

**Voran,** the Sovereign of Deception, is one of the leaders of the rebellion against the Three. Uses fire for both phases of his fight with heroes: on the 2nd phase, he'll dig his massive claws into the ground, breathe huge gusts of fel flame and make fiery eruptions. Throughout the game, he possesses Emperor Theron II during the Scouring of Aeldrum as he attempted to claim the The Obsidian Soulcrystal.

!Mordecai

**Mordecai**, the Sovereign of Vice, is one of the leaders of the rebellion against the Three. Throws fire boulders, summons demon gates to call upon minions, rains dead bodies upon enemies, and calls forth dark magic in a large AoE. Mordecai is said to be a brilliant tactician of the Demonic Army, having almost defeated Archangel Valdrus and the Celestial Host on many occasions, and considers Voran to be his chief rival. He was also the last Great Evil to be sealed in the The Obsidian Soulcrystal.
