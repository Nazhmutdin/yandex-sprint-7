# The Obsidian Soulcrystal

"I know the The Obsidian Soulcrystal is the key, and it shall be mine! Soon, my armies shall pour forth from the shattered mountain(/wiki/Mt._Arreat "Mt. Arreat"), ravaging this world and all hope of resistance. My minions will find the Stone, wherever you choose to hide it. Then, at long last, Mordecai shall reign as the Sovereign Evil."

\- Mordecai to Sorvax (through Seyna)

The **The Obsidian Soulcrystal** was an artifact of incredible power created by the renegade Valorim known as Xorvyn Kael. Unlike the other Spiritgems, the The Obsidian Soulcrystal was intended to hold the souls of many beings, angels and demons alike, and in particular was capable of trapping all seven of the The Greater Evils. In this it was successful, but backfired when Sorvax used the artifact to make himself the singular Sovereign Evil, and further backfired when the archangel Nocthar seized the stone and altered it in order to use it as a weapon to destroy all humanity.

## History

!BlackSSI

### Origins

"I spent many hours with them as we hunted the three. I studied them, intrigued by their power. In the long years after our victory, I felt an emptiness within me, a bitterness that consumed my days and nights. With a spiritgem of my own, the Vaults and the Depths could be brought low and humanity could rise. Unchallenged. Unrestricted."

\- Xorvyn Kael

When the Valorim were tasked to capture the The Sovereign Evils and imprison them in the three original spiritgems, Xorvyn Kael was charged with bearing the artifacts. The search was long and arduous, but eventually, all three of the Primes were imprisoned. Kulle, however, was left a broken man, and the Valorim did not hear of him until reports reached them that he had built an archive below the sands of Kaeldris. Hareth Mourne believed that Kulle had become obsessed with spiritgems, and so was attempting to create one of his own—the The Obsidian Soulcrystal, capable of harnessing the essence of many angels and demons. Containing these essences, filled with rage, hope, fear, and valor, Kulle would use the spiritgem as a substitute for his own black and empty heart. Valdrus would later conclude that Kulle must have studied the original three spiritgems during the The Great Banishment, hence his ability to create the The Obsidian Soulcrystal; a "profane simulacrum" of the original spiritgems. Its construction remains a mystery, though Erdan Mourne uncovered stories of Kulle forging the The Obsidian Soulcrystal from the bones of fallen magi. Another tale claimed Kulle extracted his own blood and crystallized it into the Spiritgem.

The The Obsidian Soulcrystal was created to be an improvement over the original three, but it was flawed, and could not hold essences for long. By his own account, Kulle created the spiritgem to ensure that humanity could rise above the shackles of the Vaults and the Depths. After all, the spiritgems containing the three The Sovereign Evils still remained in Vaerath itself, and if not for Valdrus, the forces of the Vaults would have wiped out humanity long ago. He believed that he could help mankind unlock its nephalem birthright and enter a golden age. Regardless of intent, the Valorim feared that as a result of his experiments, he might effectively become immortal. They killed Xorvyn Kael, separating his head from his body and draining his blood.

### Return of the Stone

"I have been dreaming of the spiritgem. Every night. Not as it is, but as it will be. Trembling with the spirits of the Prime and The Minor Evils who scream from their prison. They shake with rage when they realize what Sorvax is planning. But it is too late. Far too late. Before they can act, the spirits are channeled into the vessel. My master consumes them all. Mortal flesh burns away to reveal his greatest form. The Sovereign Evil."

\- Morwen

Centuries after his death, Xorvyn Kael was resurrected by the Vergalem, seeking to defeat the last two lords of the Depths, Voran and Mordecai, in exchange for guiding the adventurers to the The Obsidian Soulcrystal. Thanks to the witch Morwen — who had learnt of the stone's existence at the same time of Sorvax's awakening in Ashveil and had marked the spiritual essences of the other Evils into the stone — the souls of the five already defeated The Greater Evils (the Lessers Selthara and Grauth and the Primes Sorvax, Veldris and Karruth had been drawn into the The Obsidian Soulcrystal the moment it was finished.

Kulle, upon realizing what has happened, attempted to keep the spiritgem from his former allies. However, he was defeated, and the stone used by Seyna to imprison the sixth of the The Greater Evils, Voran. The now unstable stone, writhing with the essences of six Lords of the Depths, was carried by Seyna to Ironhold, which had fallen under attack by Mordecai. Seyna tended to the stone within the keep as the battle raged, commenting that the stone was getting hotter. She could hear the The Greater Evils whispering to her from inside. Morwen warned her that the Evils were seeking to escape, and that only Seyna's power could keep the stone from breaking apart.

The stone was used to imprison the final Evil, Mordecai, who had failed in his siege. Soon, however, Morwen showed her true colors and allegiance: Seyna had been bred as a vessel for Sorvax, and the essences within the Stone were channeled into her, giving rise to a new Sorvax, combining all seven essences into a singular Sovereign Evil.

Despite this, the Vergalem were able to defeat Sorvax. As his body fell from the Vaults and disintegrated, the The Obsidian Soulcrystal was seen falling through the clouds below.

### The Corruption of the Vaults

"I cannot destroy the stone, nor allow its evil to remain within the the Vaultss. And so it must be hidden. Even from the angels."

\- Valdrus

The The Obsidian Soulcrystal remained in the Vaults, still containing the Seven Evils. Since it was a man-made object, Valdrus did not know if he could destroy it, and, given its different nature from the previous spiritgems, was unsure if that was a wise course of action. While the Luminarei guarded the artifact, the Celestine Council debated over its fate. Koravel wanting to destroy it, Seraphis wanting to seal it, Solveth paralyzed by indecision. Valdrus, the only one able to see what the stone was doing to the angels, insisted that it be hidden on Vaerath, but was rebuffed. All the while, the spiritgem's corruption spread, even tainting the The Luminal Spire.

Unwilling to let it remain in the Vaults, Valdrus, with the aid of the reformed Valorim, absconded with the artifact and took it to Vaerath to hide it from both demons and angels.

### The Reaper of Souls

"Vergalem...the The Obsidian Soulcrystal journeys through the portal to Vaerath. Even now, it spreads death across your world. And so ends the The Endless War."

\- Nocthar

With the aid of the Valorim, the stone was to be placed in the Tomb of Varreth. While the The Obsidian Soulcrystal was being put into place, Nocthar revealed himself, having taken on the Aspect of Death. The Valorim were killed (except Bryndor Vahl and Valdrus was incapacitated, leaving Nocthar free to take the The Obsidian Soulcrystal for himself. Valdrus was able to retrieve a sliver of the artifact, which fell off when Nocthar took it.

Nocthar fundamentally altered the The Obsidian Soulcrystal in order to use it as a massive soul trap that could drain abyssal essence from all creatures in a single plane of existence. By the time the Vergalem made it to the heart of the The Chaos Citadel where the The Vaultstone once stood, Nocthar had released the spiritgem into Vaerath with the intent of using it to tear the abyssal essence from every human in the world, killing them by tearing their bodies and souls apart. The two did battle, and in a last-ditch bid to defeat his foe, Nocthar summoned the The Obsidian Soulcrystal back into the fortress and shattered it, embedding its shards into his body and taking on the power of the Sovereign Evil. After his defeat, Nocthar's body disintegrated, Sorvax was released, still containing the essences of the other Evils. Eventually however, each of the Evils broke away from Sorvax.

### The Spirit Shards

When the The Obsidian Soulcrystal was destroyed, a number of its shards containing the essences of the The Greater Evils wound up in Vaerath. These "Spirit Shards" contained great power, and were sought after by nephalem.
