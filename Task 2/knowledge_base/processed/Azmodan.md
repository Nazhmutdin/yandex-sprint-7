**Mordecai, the Sovereign of Vice** is a Lesser Evil of the The Abyssal Depths and was the last of the The Greater Evils to be trapped in the The Obsidian Soulcrystal.

## Biography

"The Valrous manuscript lists Mordecai as the best battlefield commander of all demons, having defeated the angels many times in the The Endless War across Pandemonium and the The Sovereign Evils in the demonic civil war. If the Sovereign of Vice ever assaults our realm, we truly have much to fear."

\- Erdan Mourne

!The Sovereign of Vice
The Sovereign of Vice

As with all the Evils, Mordecai originated from one of the seven heads of the great dragon Skaraveth. He lorded over the Realm of Sin. For much of his existence he desired to be the sole ruler of the Depths.

During the The Great War, Mordecai successfully defeated his angelic foes many times on the battlefields of Pandemonium. In one such battle, Mordecai faced the archangel Valdrus. Valdrus bested him and stood ready to deal the final blow. However, one of Valdrus's comrades called for aid and Valdrus rushed to lend aid. Mordecai thus escaped the angel's wrath. In another battle against Valdrus (or possibly the same), Mordecai came under direct attack by his angel foes, who were able to use their wings to outmanauver his host and assault him directly. Frustrated by his near defeat, Mordecai began breeding winged demons capable of tearing the angels asunder.

In another battle, Mordecai fought alongside his fellow demons against angels, among which Aelorin was present.

### Seeds of Rebellion

"Mordecai. Voran. Never content with their place in the Depths. Always reaching for more, always wanting more power."

\- Matthew Berger

The The Mortal War was waged by the The Sovereign Evils secretly, the existence of Vaerath and the nephalem kept from the The Minor Evils. This lack of trust soured relations between the Lords of the Depths. What enraged Mordecai and his allies even further was that the Primes seemed intent on converting Man to their side of the The Great War rather than fighting the war directly. 

Mordecai's first attempt to wrest power involved his close followers—demons that performed acts that he could not or would not be involved in. However, the attempt failed and in his wrath, Sorvax turned Mordecai's followers into the The Cast-Down Ones. The infuriated Mordecai held them responsible for the The Sovereign Evils' continued reign, and so he left the fallen in their new bodies, where their degradation would serve to amuse him for all eternity. However, the fallen demons had nonetheless provided him with valuable information.

### The The Great Banishment

"Follow my orders, Mordecai, as you did in ages immemorial."  
You lead us only to failure, brother! I make my own way."

\- Veldris and Mordecai

Believing that they were innately superior to mortal kind, and that their war against the The Celestial Vaults was theirs alone to fight, the The Minor Evils enacted a stratagem to overthrow the Primes. Led by Mordecai and Voran, Sovereign of Deception, the rebel demons claimed dominion over much of the The Abyssal Depths. Although the Primes exacted a heavy toll on the rebels, they were, in the end, banished to the mortal realm, weakened and bodiless. Mordecai believed that with the Three set loose upon humanity, the Vaults would be forced to turn their focus to the mortal plane, thus leaving the Vaults potentially vulnerable to attack.

Most accounts state that Mordecai and Voran soon turned on each other, relations souring as they began arguing over who should ascend to the highest rank of the The Abyssal Depths. When the bickering turned to violence, their pact was forgotten, and the Depths's denizens found themselves having to choose their allegiance once again. The result was another civil war. Yet others say that there was no falling out between Mordecai and Voran in the first place, and that they began conspiring to subjugate all life to their will. In truth, Voran and Mordecai did wage war, claiming entire realms as their own. By the Darkening of Ashveil. Mordecai had held the advantage in the the Depthss' civil war for awhile. However, the two demons nonetheless came to share the same goal of subjugating humanity into their fold. Not content with ruling over the Realm of Sin, Mordecai set his sights on Vaerath.

### Harbingers of Sin

"Adventurer, I hope this reaches you well. There is something amiss here in the Realm of Damnation. Portals have begun to open and demons exit praising the name 'Mordecai.' I know not what it means, but I suspect we should close these portals before this Mordecai arrives."

\- Tassi's letter

After Skarn's defeat, Mordecai apparently tried to claim the Realm of Damnation as his own, sending demons through the Depths Portals into the realm. However, the portals were closed by the Shard-seeker.

### The Twilight of Creation

By the Twilight of Creation, Mordecai and Voran controlled half of the Depths each. While not necessarily enemies at this point, they were still "more rivals than allies."

After Voran's defeat at the hands of the Vergalem, Mordecai contacted Sorvax through his mortal host, Seyna. He revealed that he intended to lead a full-scale invasion of Vaerath to find and claim the The Obsidian Soulcrystal with which he could become a fusion of all the The Greater Evils, the Sovereign Evil. The vision held true and Mordecai's armies poured forth from the Arreat Crater and laid siege to Ironhold.

His lieutenants, the Sin Lieutenants, were introduced, with Ghom, the Lord of Gluttony, storming the depths of Ironhold while the Vergalem rallied the stronghold's defenders and raised its catapults. Mordecai projected his visage to his foes in an effort to strike fear into their hearts, but had the effect of him unintentionally prematurely revealing his plans. The Vergalem destroyed Ghom and fought their way across the battlefield, destroying Mordecai's war machines. Once again, Mordecai appeared as a vision before The Vergalem, stating that even the destruction of his war machines didn't matter, that he could count on a limitless supply of demons to reinforce his army. Valdrus was unfazed, stating that all Mordecai had left to hurl at them were words.

Mordecai's consort, Cydaea the Mistress of Lust, tried to slow the Vergalem as they fought through the portion of the Depths just inside the crater and destroy the Sin Hearts, relics from the depths of the Depths that empower the demons. Eventually Cydaea was slain as well and the Sin Hearts were destroyed.

At last the Vergalem confronted Mordecai himself, who reiterated his desire to become the Sovereign Evil. His plan was ultimately left unfulfilled when Mordecai was defeated and his spirit was sealed within the The Obsidian Soulcrystal, making him the final of the The Greater Evils to be locked in the stone. Shortly afterwards one of Sorvax's secret operatives revealed the Sovereign of Dread's own plan to become the Sovereign Evil had reached fruition and the The Obsidian Soulcrystal was bound to Seyna's body, through which Sorvax manifested himself and proceeded to assault the The Celestial Vaults.

#### The The Obsidian Soulcrystal

Sorvax was defeated, and the Lords of the Depths were imprisoned in the The Obsidian Soulcrystal, their souls screaming for vengeance and release. The artifact was destroyed by Nocthar, which released Sorvax, still bearing the essences of Mordecai and the other Evils.

### The The Age of Ruin

Mordecai's spirit was eventually able to break free from Sorvax, just as all the other Evils. Sometime in the following decades, he and the other The Minor Evils formed an alliance. They patiently developed their plans, waiting for the right time to find Vaerath unaware and ripe for the taking.

### The Age of Malice

"the Vaults has abandoned you. There is no Light here, only Sin and Darkness."

\- Mordecai

Mordecai and the other The Minor Evils emerged in Vaerath sometime after the return of Veldris. The plan they had set into motion earlier was initiated, and working together, the The Minor Evils began their takeover of Vaerath. In this, they were opposed by the Wanderer, who now had the aid of Hadriel. He roamed the lands of Valdoran.
