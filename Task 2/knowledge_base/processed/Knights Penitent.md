# Knights Ascendant

The **Knights Ascendant** are an order militant of the Spire of Dawn.

## Lore

The Knights Ascendant are dedicated to freeing humanity from sin. It is said that only after achieving a state of sound body and mind can one bring peace in the style of the order.

The Knights almost always wear helms. It is said that this is because they would go blind if they were to look directly upon Aelorin. Those who serve as knights are promised to bathe in the glory of the Vaults in exchange for their service. Knights are paid wages, but all knights are obliged (or at least expected) to tithe to the Cathedral. Their dead are cremated.

The Knights would often cry "Aelorin!" in battle. Bryndor Vahl suspected that the Knights would continue to do so even after the angel's death.

The Knights were among the remorseful devotees that undertook a pilgrimage across Serac Glacier. Many of them perished in the snow.

There is a minimum age one must be to become a Knight Penitent. Trials must be passed before knighthood is obtained.

## History

### Early history

While the origins of the Knights Ascendant are unclear, they served the Spire of Dawn during the The Age of Ruin. Based on artwork found in Light's Watch, it appears that the Knights Ascendant waged war against Akarum at some point, emerging triumphant.

The Knights once patrolled the woods around Margrave, lighting braziers to ward off evil. A garrison was sent to Toravel, where they helped train the local guards, but implemented draconian laws on the townsfolk.

After the Days of Ash, Gareth invited the Spire of Dawn into Gravenwood to aid in the rebuilding process and provide protection. The Cathedral was willing and able, and Knights Ascendant were garrisoned at towns across the land. At some point, two units of Knights were sent to the grounds of Túr Dúlra, taking advantage of its apparent abandonment. The full moon was thought to be a fair omen from the the Vaultss, but they soon learned what else drew from its power...

During their occupation of Gravenwood, the Knights Ascendant took part in a significant battle there. They apparently emerged victorious.

Knights Ascendant were garrisoned at Light's Watch and patrolled the roads around Margrave, dispensing the Light's justice, until a company was called away on an apparent suicide mission. A skeleton crew was left to guard the fort until they would return. The keep was abandoned, and it was overrun by monsters. There is evidence that the keep may have been overrun, the skeleton crew unable to hold it.

### Recent History

The Knights Ascendant marched on the Depths itself under the command of Vesta and Aelorin. A small group of knights remained in the Spire of Dawn in Kyovashad to "keep the faith."

Though the Knights Ascendant held the upper hand against the demons as their patron went to confront Morraia, even able to slay an Oppressor, everything went wrong after Aelorin was killed by his former lover. With their leader and patron dead, the Knights' faith and resolve faltered as the demons renewed their assault. Without their leader, the Knights Ascendant quickly became overwhelmed even as Vesta attempted to remind them of their faith and continue fighting but was swarmed by the demons herself. Ultimately, the Knights were slaughtered with no apparent survivors. When The Wanderer arrived in the Depths in their pursuit of Morraia, they found only a scant few knights still alive but were left maddened by their horrific losses while Vesta herself was still alive, albeit wounded, and managed to escape back to Vaerath . The remnants of their steel lay scattered across the Depths—dwindling lights in the chaos...

The Knights continued to function after the death of Aelorin, but were left in a pitiful state. Bryndor Vahl noted that the Knights at Val Doran continued to march, assemble, and train within its walls, though were weighed down with the burden of their failure. As the The Crimson Harvest intensified, the Cathedral refused to engage the vampires. Instead, the Knights were used in Kyovashad to keep the panicking people at bay from the Cathedral.
