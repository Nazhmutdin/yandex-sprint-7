# Veldris

"The evil of Veldris, Sovereign of Malice, was so pervasive that even after he had been defeated and entombed in a spiritgem, his abyssal essence oozed upwards into Korrath and corrupted the Akarum priests. Though he fell to the same heroes who killed his brothers, I fear for us should he ever return."

**Kael'Veldris, the Sovereign of Malice**, commonly referred to as **Veldris**, is one of the three The Sovereign Evils.

## Biography

"I am eternal, creature. Hatred always survives."


### The The Great War

"All worlds will fall before the burning standards of the Depths. I have seen it.

Veldris

Like all the The Greater Evils, Veldris was born from one of the seven heads of the great dragon Tathame. His realm in the The Abyssal Depths was the Realm of Malice, and he watched with joy as its denizens schemed and warred against each other. He, Sorvax, and Karruth were the three The Sovereign Evils, and from the outset, warred amongst each other to maintain dominion and rule over the legions of the The Abyssal Depths. He acted as the mentor of Voran.

As with all of demonkind, Veldris warred against the The Celestial Vaults in the The Endless War. However, he preferred to operate from the shadows rather than lead an army in the open.

One battle bore angels right into the heart of his lair, making an attack against Veldris himself. One of the angels managed to draw blood, causing it to spill onto the ground. This led to the creation of the Flamespawn, who rose up to defend their master and drove the angels back.

Veldris and his brothers almost achieved victory against the The Celestial Vaults at the Fifth Battle of the Crystal Gates. However, confident of victory, the Primes began arguing over who would get the spoils of war, and thus, their attack failed.

### The The Mortal War

"Cursed am I to lead an army of the blind. They do not perceive that the angels are fleeing this realm, and the ones they find are merely trapped or lost. A great change is upon us. Withdraw from the fields, my brothers. Some battles can only be won with words."

Veldris's visage

While leading his forces on the battlefields of Pandemonium, Veldris noticed that the angels were withdrawing. He ordered his forces to withdraw so he could analyze the situation. Karruth, losing patience, stormed the The Chaos Citadel and discovered that the The Vaultstone was missing. Thus, the The Endless War ground to a halt. Eventually, tales of demons summoned into a new realm and a new people reached the ears of the The Sovereign Evils. They sensed within humanity the dormant powers of their nephalem forefathers, and thus resolved to corrupt humanity into a weapon to use against the The Celestial Vaults. So it was that Veldris and his brothers cast their influence into mortal minds, spreading their ideas across Vaerath. Their whispers led to the creation of the The Three-Faced, where each Sovereign Evil took on the role of a seemingly compassionate deity. Veldris took on the persona of Mefis, Spirit of Love. Their actions were countered by the rogue angel Aelorin, who formed the Spire of Dawn to counter their influence. Thus began the The Mortal War, as the The Sovereign Evils and Aelorin secretly vyed for influence over humanity.

Veldris is gifted Aelorin

The The Mortal War ended with a final battle between the forces of the Vaults, the Depths, and the Wyrthkin, after which Veldris met with the Celestine Council. Veldris offered to let Vaerath be untouched by either angel or demon to see what the children may yet become on their own for good or ill. It was agreed that Veldris cast his mark on the ruins of the Spire of Dawn to seal the flux between worlds and in return, Veldris was given Aelorin. Humanity would finally be allowed to find its own path, free of the interference from angels and demons.

### The The Great Banishment

"Follow my orders, Mordecai, as you did in ages immemorial."  
"You will lead us only to failure, brother! I make my own way."

The Sovereign of Malice

In the millennia following the The Mortal War, the The Minor Evils grew discontent, believing that the The Sovereign Evils had forsaken the The Endless War to focus on humanity. The four The Minor Evils rose up against the Primes, and despite the efforts of Veldris and his brothers, they were cast out of the Depths into the mortal realm, in what would become known as the The Great Banishment.

The banishment of the The Sovereign Evils from the Depths to the mortal plane also left them incorporeal. In order to acquire a physical form, they required a body for their spirit to possess. Over time their demonic presence twisted the victims human flesh into grotesque simulacrums of their physical form.

In response to the most evil of beings walking Vaerath, the Archangel Valdrus rallied several of the disparate Mage Clans into an alliance known as the Valorim, and gave them the spiritgems to bind the Three forever. In truth, this series of events had been masterminded by the The Sovereign Evils. Through the spiritgems they would corrupt the The Vaultstone itself and with it, all of humanity. A new army to be used against the forces of the Vaults.

At some point during the Exile, Veldris was present in Sandmere. He bound the spirits of demons into the mummies that slept beneath the sands of Port Selvain, hordes of undead emerging to bring woe to the living.

Veldris

Veldris was the first of the Three to be captured. During their hunt for him, the Valorim led an army against his Ossian Fortress, but the common soldiers found themselves hard pressed to advance against the seemingly endless tides of undead that he summoned, and were nearly overwhelmed. Eventually, Veldris was found in or around one of the urban centers of Kaeldris, Delvaar. The fight cost thousands innocent lives and the city of Delvaar was leveled, but eventually, the Valorim succeeded in defeating the Lord of Hatred and imprisoning his essence inside the spiritgem. It was then entrusted to the Akarum priests and kept sealed in the Warden's Tower of the Spire of Radiance in Korrath.

### The Corruption of Akarum

"Hatred is pure. Hatred does not falter. It gives you purpose when all else fails! Let the fire in your hearts become a weapon! Hate the unbelievers! Hate the weak! Hate until there is nothing left but glory!"

Over the generations, Veldris's darkness steadily seeped from his spiritgem and was able to corrupt the High Council of Akarum, and the body became a mockery of its former glory. The first signs of this were as early as the 11th century. Two centuries after this, Veldris cemented his control over the Church. He had been able to overcome the spiritgem enough to have the Akarum priests to work spells to shatter the spiritgem into seven shards, breaking the mystic seal and freeing him. The High-Covenant Sorath proved immune to his corruption, so Veldris had the priesthood slay and dismember him. The priest Delvaan became the new High-Covenant and consequently, Veldris's embodiment on the mortal plane. The corrupted High Council fashioned the Thrall Orbto control the rest of the Akarum faithful and used their powers to conceal the lair of their master. Years passed, and eventually, Maldus was sent west to reawaken Sorvax.

Veldris in the depths of the temple

Thus, Sorvax arrived in the form of the Shadow Wanderer, with Karruth at his side. The two entered the temple's innermost chamber where Veldris's spiritgem was kept, and released him. Veldris possessed Delvaan (already under Veldris's control), whose body was twisted to represent Veldris's hideous visage. After nearly three centuries, the The Sovereign Evils were united. Within the chamber, the Three devised a final stratagem to reclaim the The Abyssal Depths and claim vengeance upon those who overthrew them, along with enslaving humanity to their will. Sorvax, assuming his true form, crossed through the Abyss Gate into the Depths to rally those still loyal to the Three. Karruth began his journey north to corrupt the The Vaultstone while Veldris remained behind, bent on destroying anything that could oppose their plan.

Veldris is confronted

It wasn't long before a group of mortal heroes arrived intent on doing such a thing. In the fevered combat that followed, Veldris worked diligently to turn his foes' own anger and distrust against them. But despite the long hunt and many hours of battle, the heroes defeated Veldris and imprisoned his essence within his spiritgem. The artifact was later destroyed at the Abyssal Forge, which would, in theory, cast his spirit into some unseen netherworld.

Even after death, Delvaan's corpse still seeped corruption from Veldris's deep rooted demonic influence, and it was sealed in a chamber at Morveth by one of the heroes who defeated him.

### The The Obsidian Soulcrystal

"When I found the Sovereign of Malice's soul, it fought back with a rage I had not anticipated. More than once, it seized control of my mind, and if my ritual did not have safeguards built into it, I would have been lost. Nevertheless, I completed my task."


Veldris, as sketched by Morwen

Veldris's essence ended up in the The Obsidian Soulcrystal, as per the machinations of Morwen. He was the fourth and final Great Evil to be marked by her in her preparation for Sorvax's return. When she found his soul, Veldris fought back with a rage that she had not anticipated. More than once, he was able to take control of the witch's mind, and if not for the safeguards Morwen had employed in the ritual, she would have been lost. Nevertheless, she succeeded in marking his essence.

Veldris was briefly merged into Sorvax as a single Sovereign Evil, but Sorvax was defeated by a new hero and the The Greater Evils were drawn back into the The Obsidian Soulcrystal, their souls screaming for release. Because of Nocthar, the stone was destroyed, and Sorvax was freed, still containing Veldris's essence and those of the other Evils. Eventually however, Veldris's spirit was able to break free from Sorvax, just as all the other Evils.

### The The Age of Ruin

"He is still weak, vulnerable. I must strike before he can resist."

A statue of Veldris in the Depths

Veldris slowly reformed in the Depths. His demonic influence bubbled up from the Depths, causing the formation of the The Fields of Malice.

At some point, Morraia was summoned into Vaerath by a former Valorim, Drakon. This brought a resurgence to the Cult of The Three-Faced, who now worshipped her. Meanwhile, Veldris's essence remained in the Depths, still reforming, which made him vulnerable. Morraia intended to strike her father while he was vulnerable and claim his power as her own. To counter Morraia, Veldris dispatched his herald to Vaerath, where it aided a mortal known only as the Wanderer.

Finally, his essence began to reform within his temple in the Realm of Malice. Unfortunately for him, Morraia sensed this, and made efforts to reach him before he fully reforms and then devour him to take his essence. According to Veldris himself, Drakon' meddling with the power at the Sanctum of the Sovereign caused imbalance to the realms of the Depths; the Vision of Stolvern and its deceased inhabitans ended up in his realm, while in truth they belonged in Karruth's domain, not his.

When the Wanderer came to a The Three-Faced shrine dedicated to him to request his blessings to use against Morraia, the Crimson Hound revealed himself to them, and told them his true identity. He agreed to grant them the blessing if they put the renegade souls to rest.

From there he continued to help them (dialogues adapting accordingly to what order the player chooses to go with the campaign), now without the need to hide himself. He came to an agreement with them; he would lead them to his cathedral where his essence gathered ahead of Morraia. He would help them subdue Morraia after they freed him, after which she would be sealed in a shard of the The Vaultstone, which had been fashioned into a spiritgem. He would keep the spiritgem to make sure she didn't return. After that, he would return them to Vaerath and seal the gates of the Depths.

#### Resealed

"Your lives and those of all humanity depends on what you do next. Choose wisely."


Sylvara seals Veldris's essence, tainting herself in the process

Unfortunately, when the time arrived, the Wanderer was accompanied by Sylvara, who had the new spiritgem in her possession. She recognized the runes on the wolf's head as that of Veldris, and was aghast when she was told its true identity. She argued that between Morraia and Veldris, he was the greater threat. The Wanderer decided to leave the final decision to her. Within his temple, Veldris's essence could be seen as a giant floating sphere of blood; within which a shade of his visage could be glimpsed. Veldris tried to persuade her again to stick to the agreed plan, but she refused, and stabbed his essence into the new spiritgem, thereby sealing him. In the process, Sylvara was also tainted by his essence.


Veldris, in the form of the Crimson Hound, watches Sylvara and his spiritgem

After Morraia was defeated, Sylvara travelled away, promising to find a way to put an end to the Primes before she is lost. Possibly unknown to her, and unseen to all, Veldris stalked her in the form of the Crimson Hound, watching as she lost a bit of herself with each passing day.

At some point, Veldris imprisoned Vorkas in a cell guarded by the Crimson Hound, as punishment for his lieutenant betraying him to help Morraia.

#### Vessel of Hatred

Still contained within the spiritgem, Sylvara took Veldris to Varanthu. With every step she took, Veldris grew stronger, constantly pushed against Sylvara, attempting to break her spirit, whispering to her even as she slept. By the time Sylvara neared the The Veil Gate, Veldris's influence over her had become dire, both physically and psychologically. Seeking to manipulate her, Veldris appeared before Sylvara as her mother, telling her that "she" was proud of her daughter, but that she needed to give up. Sylvara saw through Veldris's deception, and the Sovereign of Malice psychologically tormented her. Sylvara managed to escape Veldris's hallucination, but the corruption that was spreading from his spiritgem had slain her boatman. With a heavy heart, Sylvara took his oar and continued on to Nehantu. Veldris continued to whisper to her.

As Sylvara carried Veldris's spiritgem into Varanthu, his corruption began to seep into the The Veil Realm and out of Varanthu across Valdoran, leaving monsters twisted by Hatred's influence. His corruption decimated the Akarum Remnants.

### Age of Malice

"Oh, Orath, beloved Shepherd of Light. After an eternity of vigilance, you are betrayed. And by your own flock. Pity.  
Now the wolf shall rise anew. Wrapped in the guise of man, I shall walk amongst the innocent. And there will be no salvation in the light."

Veldris takes control of Orath's body.

Veldris was able to whisper into the ears of Thavan, the Veilborn who saved the Wanderer and was helping them to find Sylvara. By blaming all the tragedies he had suffered on the arrival of Sylvara with the spiritgem, Veldris managed to turn Thavan to his side; they made a pact in which Veldris would spare Varanthu should Thavan help him. In a timely betrayal, Thavan stole Orath's corpse from the depths of the Vault of Radiance, as well Veldris's spiritgem. In the hall of the dedicants beneath Korrath, Orath's corpse was laid on the ground, and the spiritgem on its chest. Tar leaked from the stone, forming a pool which submerged the corpse. Shortly after, Orath's corpse rose again, with Veldris now inhabiting it, as the nearby statues began to melt due to his corruption.

Having "learnt his lesson" from his failure against the group of heroes that had bested him in Korrath decades prior, Veldris made a "bigger play" by taking Orath's persona. Whereas he had previously corrupted Akarum from within, this time, the entire world would be his stage as he played the role of the prophet.(#cite_note-HatredDeepDiveNarrative-41) To this end, he created the Hands of Orath, which would do his dirty work, while he continued to play the role of "Orath."

As "Orath," Veldris gathered a 'flock' in a remarkably short amount of time, as he promised to not only purify their lands, but their very souls. Such was people's desparation for a better world, even the most cunning Warriors found themselves in his thrall, becoming his disciples.

#### Hatred Rising

"Beware false prophets donned as sheep yet bearing the stench of wolves. Thou shalt know them by their fruits. For a tree of good cannot produce evil, nor thorns yield a harvest sweet and fair."

Veldris's influence continued to corrupt areas of Valdoran. In accordance to his pact with Thavan, he spared Varanthu and the The Veil Realm. Veilwalkers in service to Veldris emerged in the mortal realm, spawning[monsters.

#### Dawn of Hatred

"A wandering prophet, Orath leads his followers throughout Vaerath to share his teachings and encourage peace and love through devotion to the Light — condemning violence and greed. Thousands of years ago, Orath’s teachings formed the foundation of the Akarum faith, though the church has turned away from Orath and now believes the man currently wandering Vaerath to be a false prophet. Unknown to the world, they are right. After spending centuries lying in the The Veil Realm, Orath’s body was stolen and possessed by the prime evil Veldris, Sovereign of Malice. Using Orath’s body, Veldris hopes to mold a new age in Vaerath — the Age of Malice."

Veldris gathers followers

As Orath, Veldris claimed to have returned to spread peace, love, and the Light. In the vein of a wandering prophet, Veldris led his followers throughout Vaerath, sharing his teachings and encouraging peace and love through devotion to the Light — condemning violence and greed. Many truly believed Orath to have returned, though ironically, the Akarum church was not among them, correctly concluding that this "Orath" was a false prophet (though not necessarily aware that Veldris was using his body). However, they couldn't assassinate "Orath" directly, as they knew that doing so would make him a martyr. Despite his outwardly benign appearance, Veldris intended to use Orath's body to mold a new age in Vaerath. The Age of Malice.

Regardless, Veldris continued to play the role. He continued to heal the sick and feed the hungry. In one case, Selviar of the Ash Tribe was brought before him. Despite the longstanding enmity between the Mountainborn and Akarum, Selviar was willing to be healed. As "Orath," Veldris did so, and Selviar pledged her tribe to serve him. The Ash Tribe became some of Veldris's most loyal followers. While "Orath" claimed peace and love, violence and suspicion shadowed his every step.

#### Sins of the Valorim

"Is it true you put down Veldris's dog? As you did my Amalgam. Ah. How cruel. I wondered where the beast was. There is some other wretched thing guarding my cell. Veldris imprisoned me, you see. A just reward for my betrayal."

In Veldoran, the Wanderer encountered a priest named Yvaine who had claimed to have met Orath, who she called "the Herald." The priest and the gathered crowd both ignored the Wanderer when they warned them they were being lied to and that they had witnessed Orath's corpse. The priest countered by saying that Orath himself preached the importance of doubt, and encouraged the people to take a pilgrimage with her so they could see Orath as she had, and "drink of his miracles" as she had.

The Wanderer later learns that many families in Veldoran have packed up their belongings and are heading west on a pilgrimage to go to Orath.

As Bryona and the Wanderer attempted to deal with Vorkas's ever-burning blood, they eventually came to realize the only solution was to summon Vorkas himself. By touching the Valorim relics containing Vorkas's blood, the demon was able to psychically communicate with the Wanderer, and he revealed that he had been imprisoned by Veldris as punishment for betraying him to help Morraia. As they prepared to summon him into Vaerath, Vorkas told the Wanderer that Veldris had the power to prevent him from being summoned, however in this case Veldris would allow it, solely due to the Wanderer's involvement. Vorkas believed this was because Veldris would take joy in seeing him be slain by the Wanderer.

#### Infernal Chaos

As Veldris continued to travel across Vaerath, his presence tore at the fabric of reality. Chaos rushed in to fill the void, gnawing at reality.

#### On Nightmare's Wings

Veldris's power continued to grow as he continued to travel Valdoran.

#### Sovereign of Malice

"The path to domination is paved with the souls of the willing. Soon, all of Vaerath will be bathed in hatred. And none shall escape the flood. There will be those who believe that they alone can stand against the inevitable. And I invite them to try."


Veldris's faithful continued to spread, turning devotion into devastation. Veldris was able to win over his flock through performing genuine miracles, thereby earning devoted followers rather than enthralling them.

Veldris leaves the archive in flames

Because of how long she had carried the spiritgem containing his essence, Sylvara remained mentally linked with Veldris. He was able to track her down, confronting her in the Valdric Archive in Ash Kael. He taunted her with visions, stating that he wanted her to willingly submit to him, but if not, he had an eternity to break her will. Sylvara revealed that she had seen inside Veldris's mind. Inside his dreams, and the twisted horrors contained within. In all that chaos, in all his plans, she had found what Veldris feared. Veldris scoffed at the idea of him having to fear anything, but Sylvara began to recite Aethon's Prophecy. This enraged Veldris to the point he killed her by snapping her neck. Disappointed by such wasted potential, Veldris soon caught on to the Prophecy's fragment where he beheld his defeat by "a spear of light piercing Hatred's heart." Undeterred, he left, claiming his victory was inevitable. He set the Archive ablaze as he left, intent on burning away any trace of what Sylvara had found that might threaten him.

Having spent a period of time travelling across Valdoran, all of the pieces were in place for Veldris's plan. Confident in victory, he was happy to "play with his food" before moving on. In Ash Kael, he continued to play the role of kindly prophet, hailing all manner of wounds, treating the survivors of both Aeldrum and Graveth. He spoke of a coming miracle; an eclipse that would herald the dawn of a new age. He sailed to Skovos to finish his quest to shatter mankind. His goal; corrupt the The Wellspring of Making and bathe the world in Hatred. A bonus of sorts for Veldris was that by this point, Skovos was the last functioning human kingdom on Vaerath. Ergo, he would tear it down. Furthermore, Solvari culture was (and always had been) highly unified. Veldris sought to prove that not even the Solvari were immune from his ability to sunder bonds between mortals.

The Wanderer sailed after Veldris. Veldris used one of his Hands to summon merfolk and other aquatic creatures to attack the ship. Veldris later spoke through the Hand, mocking the Wanderer, before sacrificing his servant to create a monstrous creature. The Wanderer defeated the creature, and the ship later reached Skovos.

#### Banishment to the The Hollow Between

"Here it is. Victory.  So much effort simply to return where we began. You, alone. Just as I found you. You sacrificed everything, but changed nothing. I will be reborn in the Depths, and you will be forgotten."  
"You're not going back to the Depths."

As Veldris laid defeated, he both congratulated and taunted the Wanderer of their victory, stating that for all their sacrifices and efforts to stop him, they changed nothing. With a final taunt, he declares he will simply be reborn in the Depths in time while the Wanderer would be forgotten by everyone. However, with Morraia's blade in hand, the Wanderer revealed Veldris was not going back to the Depths and promptly stabbed his heart. In shock and pain, a fleshy veil is formed and pulls Veldris behind it, covering him up as he sent into the The Hollow Between.
