# The Shard-seeker

Legend of the Shard-seeker's demon\-slaying prowess became known across the furthest reaches of Vaerath. The Memory of Nor Tiraj regarded the Shard-seeker as potentially the greatest Valorim provided they "learn something new."

## Biography

Main article: Adventurer's Chronicle#Summary

### Wortham

In 1270, the hero traveled to Wortham, which was under attack by the Cult of Damnation. The hero saved the town, and prevented Skarn from manifesting in Vaerath. During the fighting, the Shard-seeker met Erdan Mourne, who revealed that he had come to Wortham to search for the The Vaultstone shard that had landed nearby, and advised them of the existence of the Ashwold shard. Their meeting was interrupted by Xul;talking through a skeleton, the Necromancer told Cain that his apprentice, Lethes, had acquired a shard of the The Vaultstone, and brought ruin upon Ashwold. Cain told the hero to retrieve the shard and then meet him at Irenmarch, where it would be safer to destroy them.

### Ashwold

Arriving at Ashwold, the hero found the dead rising en masse. The hero was able to save Xul from Lethes, as well as the Ashwold Guard, but could not save Asylla's handmaiden from being murdered by Lethes in her quest to find the tomb of Queen Asylla. It was discovered that this was part of Lethes's plan to resurrect King Aldrath, and the hero entered Ashwold Manor to slay Leoric in his own domain. Upon exiting, they found that Lethes had escaped Xul; pursuing her, they fought the rogue Necromancer and defeated her, but Xul explained that Lethes was likely still alive. Xul pursued Lethes, while the hero carried on to Irenmarch.

### Irenmarch

At Irenmarch, the hero met Brynis and later, Cain at his workshop. The Valorim explained that there was no safe way to destroy the shard as of yet, but directed them to the The Shadowwood, as he'd detected a third shard there.

### The The Shadowwood

The hero arrived at Blackstone Village in the The Shadowwood, which had been destroyed by the Bloodsworn. They allied with the Order of the Blind Oracle, which were fighting against the Bloodsworn. Selveth informed the hero that the Bloodsworn were being led by an ex-Rogue named Lakrii, and that a The Vaultstone shard could be the source of the cult's power. After entrusting them with the Valorim Bestiary, Selveth introduced the hero to Hemlir, a Druid, who would conduct a ritual to reveal the means of cleansing the corruption that plagued the The Shadowwood.

The hero gathered the reagents Hemlir required, but the ritual failed. Hemlir advised them to head to the Tree of Inifuss; along the way, they came across a confrontation between Lakrii and a member of the Cult of Damnation, confirming that Lakrii's mistress possessed the The Shadowwood's The Vaultstone shard. At the tree, Hemlir conducted the ritual at the cost of his life, but in so doing, he revealed that the Countess possessed the shard lay in the Forgotten Tower, and was fed blood via the Blood Rose. Hemlir died, and Flavie informed the hero that the Sisterhood had attacked the Forgotten Tower. The hero knew that as long as the Blood Rose survived, the Countess could not be slain. They headed to slay the Blood Rose.

The hero slew the Blood Rose, and at Selveth's direction, went to the Forgotten Tower to aid the Blind Oracles in their assault. They slew the Countess, and in so doing, gained the third The Vaultstone shard. The Sisterhood remained in the The Shadowwood to deal with the Bloodsworn, while the hero returned to Irenmarch.

### Return to Irenmarch

The hero returned with the The Shadowwood shard. With three shards in his possession, Cain attempted to destroy them, but failed. After conducting some research, Cain told the hero about Xorvyn Kael and the spiritgems. One of Kulle's libraries lay in the Shassar Sea, and it was there, Cain theorized, that they might find a means of destroying the The Vaultstone shards. Calling in a favor, Cain had Captain Rehm provide his ship as transport to Shassar.

### The Shassar Sea

"The Shassar Sea...drier than I expected."

\- The Shard-seeker

The hero arrived in Shassar, where they saved Zov from Sand Scorpion bandits. Zov led them to the encampment of the Amber Blades, which was under attack by lacuni. In order to get into Tabri's good graces, the hero slew a lacuni pack leader, and returned its hide to the encampment. Tabri told the hero she would consider helping them find the Archives of Xorvyn Kael if they provided her the Scepter of Fahir. The hero obtained Fahir's Command from the Chamber of Wisdom, Fahir's Visage from the Tomb of Fahir, and the Light of Fahir from the Chamber of Truth. The assembeld scepter was given to Tabri, who through it, claimed dominion over Shassar. Holding up her end of the bargain, she assigned Peth to guide the hero to Kulle's library.

Peth guided the hero to the library's entrance and opened it for them. With that, the hero ventured inside.

### The Archives of Xorvyn Kael

Inside the library, the hero was attacked by the Curator, who refused to believe their protestations that they were no thief. Nevertheless, the Curator did not back down until the hero defeated them. The hero explained that they were trying to find a way to destroy the The Vaultstone shards. The Curator responded that there might be information in Kulle's private archives on such a topic, but that the library had fallen into disrepair, and that it would have to be restored if they were to access the information. The hero succeeded, and with the library now under the Curator's control, they met with the hero in the Archive of Secrets. There, they showed the hero a tome that recounted Kulle's defeat of Karruth centuries ago, during the The Great Banishment. The hero relived Kulle's memories of the event, where it was revealed that only an archangel's weapon could damage the The Vaultstone. That presented a problem, for the Curator only knew of two such weapons, and the first, Lumindral, was beyond their reach. The other, however, was named Yl'nira, and rested inside the Temple of Namari on Bilefen. The Curator used the library's terminus to teleport the hero to Bilefen.

### Bilefen

"That was...an experience. Well then, this must be Bilefen Island. Now to find someone who knows about the Temple of Namari."

\- The hero after arriving at Bilefen

The hero arrived at Bilefen, and was directed to Port Justinian. There, they met with Talva Silvertongue, who directed them to the Temple of Namari. There, they met with Jin; a Xian Wizard who intended to summon Namari in order to access her ancient texts. In this, she succeeded, and the two adventurers met with Namari's spirit. Namari revealed that the "temple" was, in fact, a prison, dedicated to imprisoning Sargoth. However, three beacons from the temple had been stolen, and if they were returned, they would have a chance to destroy Sargoth before he escaped. If they did this, she would provide the texts and Yl'nira.

Following Jin's advice, the pair headed back to the port, where they met a Crimsonblade pirate, who was willing to provide information on one of the beacons. The hero meanwhile headed back to see Talva, but had to save the port from a Fetish attack. Talva told them what he knew of the two beacons. With the aid of Cadeus, the hero recovered the first beacon, and slew Ongori to retrieve the second.

The hero met with Jin at Crimsonblade Haven. Jin explained that her negotiations had gone poorly, but that she'd learnt that the pirates' leader kept the beacon aboard his ship. They made it to the ship and into the captain's cabin, where they confronted and defeated Captain Rhodri. With his death, the third and final beacon was obtained, and they returned to Namari. Returning the three beacons, Namari led the hero into the temple, while Jin took the nephalem's place at the altar.

The hero slew Sargoth, and Namari gave them her tomes and Yl'nira. After exiting, they gave Jin the tomes she'd sought, and the two parted ways. Afterwards, the hero was summoned to the Ancient Arena. After battling through the undead, they were contacted by the Unspoken, who indicated that they might be worthy. The spirit gave them a scroll, and hinted that soon, the hero would understand.

### Irenmarch (Again)

The Shard-seeker returned with Yl'nira to Cain in Irenmarch. Using the dagger the three shards were destroyed. Cain revealed that his last ritual revealed another shard upon Mount Zavain — a mountain located not far from Mount Valdur. However, Rehm's ship wasn't ready to sail. Cain gave The Shard-seeker a cryptic letter addressed to them that he received while they were away. He advised them to address the letter while he secures them passage.

The letter informed The Shard-seeker to head to Selynne's Basilica to learn about an ancient cycle that has protected Vaerath. There they met the Unspoken,who told them about the Cycle of Strife — an ancient war between the Immortals, ancient defenders of Vaerath, and the Shadows, a group dedicated to deposing the Immortals should they become corrupt.

By the time they returned to Cain, Rehm was ready to sail. He informed them that he would take them to a port near Mount Zavain, from where they can use a caravan to travel up the mountain.

### Mount Zavain

Upon arriving at Mount Zavain, the Shard-seeker came upon corpses; both human and khazra littered about. They also met Oza a Veradani further along the road. She warned them to be careful about the khazra, which had been agitated as of recent. After defeating another band of khazra, Oza led the Shard-seeker to the local outpost of the Sons of Rakkis; Sentinel's Watch.

Upon reaching the outpost they inquired about the state of affairs from the captain, who was relieved to receive reinforcements, especially from a monk. As he informed the duo of the current situation, they were interrupted by a soldier, who came with news that a village to the south had been lost to the khazra, who now occupied its ruins, and had captured some of the soldiers sent to repel them. He also informed that more khazra were attacking from the north. The Shard-seeker offered to go to the village with the soldier and rid it of khazra.

The village was already destroyed, and overrun by Moon Clan khazra. The duo were able to save one guard who was trapped in a well. Another guard was tied to a stake at the villages square, near an active summoning ring. The duo was unable to save him from being sacrificed to summon Blood Clan khazra. When the battle was over, The Shard-seeker noticed a carving of an eye in the summoning circle. They recognized it as the symbol of the Cult of Damnation, and guessed that they would be the cause of the khazra aggression. After that, they returned to Sentinel's Watch.

The Shard-seeker reported their findings to Captain Vereks. Vereks told them to help reinforce Oza,who had been fighting khazra non-stop since they left to investigate the Razed Village. The Shard-seeker was able to provide Oza some relief, and told of their findings; that a cult was behind it, and they sought to deliver a shard of the The Vaultstone to Skarn. Oza was surprised by the news as the only shard on Zavain was safeguarded by the Veradani of the Sanctified Earth Monastery; a place she believed the cult would be unable to breach. The duo were then attacked by khazra, and Oza told The Shard-seeker to head into their den and eliminate the cultists inciting the aggression. As the Shard-seeker fought their way through the cultists, they heard them mention a "Dravec," which the Shard-seeker soon confronted within the den. Dravec summoned a demon to battle the adventurer, and while the Shard-seeker prevailed, Dravec slipped away.

The Shard-seeker returned to Oza, telling her that Dravc appeared to have known her. Oza told them about their history. The Shard-seeker departed to track Dravec. Oza led the Shard-seeker into the Misty Valley. Through her powers, they learnt of Dravec's past actions. The Shard-seeker assured her that Dravec's fall wasn't her fault. Moving on, they found the body of a Monk at a shrine. Through her abilities, Oza was able to deduce that the Monk and his fellows had been searching for her, but had been torn apart by demons. Given their number, the Shard-seeker sumized the Cult of Damnation had opened a rift to the Depths itself. It was the only thing that could account for an assault of such magnitude. Oza headed to the Sanctified Earth Monastery to warn the Monks of the coming assault. Meanwhile, the Shard-seeker continued tracking Dravec. Oza directed them to an old temple, where Dravec would be according to the writings they'd uncovered earlier. She told the adventurer to show Dravec no mercy if they found him. Furthermore, it was believed that the temple was being used as the summoning site. If the Shard-seeker could interrupt the ritual, the invasion could be stemmed at its source. The Shard-seeker enter the site and slew the cultists. During the fight, an apparition of Skarn appeared before them. Skarn warned the Shard-seeker that, having destroyed three The Vaultstone shards, they had earned the totality of his ire. Afterwards, the Shard-seeker found a map in a priest's robes, indicating the presence of yet another summoning site. The Shard-seeker successfully destroyed the portal at the second site.

While the Shard-seeker had destroyed the portals, there were still the demons currently attacking the Sanctified Earth Monastery. The adventurer made their way to the monastery to aid in its defense. They found a trail of bodies (both human and demon) on the way there, and upon arriving, found the monastery under siege by catapults. They destroyed the catapults and reunited with Oza. They headed to the monastery shrine holding the The Vaultstone shard, as it was where Dravec would be headed.

They made their way through the grounds; through the butchery the demons had left in their wake. The Shard-seeker could tell Oza had nearly reached her breaking point. The Shard-seeker warned her that her grief would have to wait, that the shard remained their priority. They were interrupted by Dravec, who told Oza that everything he had done had not been for the shard, but rather for Tayev. He stole the shard and summoned demons to waylay them. The pair defeated the demons and pursued Tayev in the company of Veradani acolytes.

### Destruction's Wake

Zatham informed the Shard-seeker that they had discovered how to use the Pathstone. They would now sail to The First Cradle. The Shard-seeker went to see Karshun and Cain, but Cain was leaving to investigate a rumor he had heard of. He said his goodbyes to the Shard-seeker and their fellows. Afterwards, Karshun instructed the Shard-seeker on how to use the Pathstone to make their way through the the Lost Sea.

The Shard-seeker met with Zatham at the Irenmarch docks. He informed them that two ships had departed Irenmarch in the dead of night, likely belonging to the Cult of Terror. They sailed aboard the Black Bower.

### Dark Rebirth

### Splintered Souls

With Sorvax having claimed the spiritgem shard, the Shard-seeker (with Valdrus still within their mind) travelled to the The Southern Blightlands, in hope of finding Lumindral.

### Rite of Cleansing

Caked in demon blood from their conquests, the Shard-seeker turned to Nisza in Irenmarch, in order to keep their mind and body whole.

### Crucible of Justice

"Lumindral. Sword of the Archangel of Order. The weapon I need to defeat Sorvax, and it rejects what I have become. My quest to destroy the The Vaultstone shards transform me into a host for malevolent specters of chaos. I can no longer contain their darkness. Evil pours from my body. Feasts on my soul. Death follows me as I walk this path of deliverance. I must travel to the Land of the Stormcallers. To cleanse my impurity. Only then will I be worthy to wield Lumindral and face Sorvax."

Having retrieved Lumindral from the The Blightlands, the Shard-seeker was tormented by their inability to wield it. The Sword of Justice rejected the darkness that had crept into the Shard-seeker's body and mind with the shattering of the The Vaultstone shards, and their torment began taking a life of its own. They became a host for malevolent specters, and evil poured from their body, feeding on the Shard-seeker's very soul.

To contain the evil festering within and prepare for their confrontation with Sorvax, the Shard-seeker travelled to the the Frosted Isles to cleanse their impurity, and therefore, be worthy to wield Lumindral.

### Shattered Vaerath

### Epoch of Madness

#### The Writhing Wilds

The Shard-seeker travelled into the Sharval Wilds. They find an oppressed people, warring factions, and a forest slowly being overtaken by corruption.ref_Sorvax Immortal, The Writhing Wilds_

#### The Prince of Freedom

Continuing their search for Albrecht, the Shard-seeker searched for a Witch who spoke for the fey. As they travelled from town to town, they found revolt brewing.

#### Flesh Harvest

As Sharval prepared for its harvest Festival, a Shardborne cult led by Albrecht gained prominence. Albrecht creates a flesh hive at the Wicker Fields as a haven for Shardborne. The Shard-seeker banded together with the locals (the Untameable Wilds, Talons of Ifeh and Highfork to destroy the hive, but were stopped by civilians that made up the cult and toxic mists surrounding the hive. Elder Mascen placed a protective spells around the Shard-seeker and some other allies, but Albrecht swiftly dispeled them.

As the others succumbed to the mists the Shard-seeker still pressed forward, despite being affected by it. As a result of the stress they were pushing through, they accidentally projected their spirit into another realm. An abyss began to flash over the surrounding to their sight, and the coils of a great serpent could be seen in the distance. As their condition got worse, their presence in the strange realm became stable. The serpent noticed them and came over to investigate. It commented on the potential of their soul, and made them immune to the mists. Their spirit returned to the physical realm as their health was restored.
