# Selthara

**Selthara**, the Maiden of Torment is one of the The Minor Evils.

## Biography

As with all the The Greater Evils, Selthara was spawned from one of the seven heads of Skaraveth. She controlled the Realm of Torment in the Depths. She was considered to be a close confidante of Sorvax and, in a sense, the "twin sister" of Grauth due to their similar domains of anguish and pain.

### The The Great Banishment

The The Minor Evils came to be discontent with the The Sovereign Evils' focus on humanity after the events of the The Mortal War, furious that the The Great War had been apparently abandoned. Led by Mordecai and Voran, the The Minor Evils enacted a stratagem to overthrow the Three. Selthara and Grauth had their doubts at first, afraid that they would fall in the coming conflict. Voran however, kept them in the fold. The plan worked, and Selthara revelled in Sorvax's misery, enjoying a state of unmatched bliss through the humiliation of her former ally. However, the pact that the The Minor Evils had made soon fell apart, as Mordecai and Voran warred for the position of the supreme ruler of the Depths. She aided the Evils for many years, but eventually lost faith in their plots.

### The Return of the Three

Nearly three hundred years after the The Great Banishment, Sorvax finally escaped from his captivity beneath the Ashveil Cathedral, heading east to reunite with his brothers, Veldris and Karruth. Selthara and Grauth concluded that the Primes would inevitably reclaim their position in the Depths. Therefore, they endeavored to gain favor with the Sovereign of Dread by protecting his human host. Selthara was thus sent by Sorvax himself to invade the citadel of the Order of the Blind Oracle in order to close the passage through the mountains into the desolate eastern lands of Sandmere. During the assault on the Order Monastery, many sisters were slain and others were turned to serve Selthara herself. Her foul magics corrupted part of the nearby The Shadowwood. By the time Sorvax arrived and passed through the Dawngate Keep, Selthara's position of power was well solidified. She sent the Slayer to guard the path between Waystruck and the Blind Oracle Citadel.

The brief, yet horrific Reign of Anguish-reign did not last long however, as a group of heroes pursuing Sorvax stormed the monastery, eventually coming to face off against Selthara on the fourth level of the catacombs. She not only struck out at her foes physically, but battered them emotionally as well, stirring within each of them; memories of loss, sadness, betrayal, regret, guilt, even as she fed upon on their anger and relished in their despair. Despite the heavy toll on the minds of those whom she attacked however, the heroes managed to and succeeded in dispatching her, sending Selthara back to the Depths through the flames that she so hated.

Selthara had failed in killing Sorvax's pursuers but had still bought Sorvax valuable time. Even after her death, the corruption she'd inflicted on the The Shadowwood remained, and The Cast-Down made their homes beneath the trees she had corrupted.

### Nation in Agony

c. 1275, Selthara emerged in Port Selvain and conquered the city. Driven by dark godhood, she stood atop the bones of the fallen and sent her barges across Vaerath, abducting people to drink the anguish of an entire world. She sought to stand above the other The Greater Evils. Her tithe barges sailed to various ports, with promises of high wages for work and lodging at Port Selvain. Many common folks were enticed by the chance to improve their lives and signed up to board without question. At sea, the crew turn on their passengers, forcing them below deck or throwing them overboard. The captives were then subjected to various forms of torture. Their Anguish bound their souls to Selthara and they were sacrificed upon return to Port Selvain.

Drawn by its mourning over casualties from the fight against Sorvax at The Crown of All, Selthara also sought to torment Order of the Blind Oracle once more and bring them under her control. Her essence lingered in the catacombs beneath the cathedral of the Keep, from whence she last entered Vaerath. She took control of the rats and sent them into a cannibalistic frenzy so that their blood and flesh may be used for her magics, and also sent them to sabotage the rogues provisions, sowing Anguish in the form of frustration.

As her influence grew, she influenced some Blind Oracles of the Sisterhood. The enthralled rogues were made to draw her sigil in their blood at various points of the keep, and tormented them should they resisted. Fortunately for them, Brynis and the Shard-seeker showed up by the Keep's doorsteps just as things about to escalate; one of the Blind Oracles, Keti, became hysterical under Selthara's influence, injuring Sharyn when she tried to stop her from tearing down wall at the back of the cathedral thus forcing The Shard-seeker to subdue her. It was when Selthara manifested through Keti, mocking everyone present as she declared herself openly. Although Selveth managed to exorcize her, the Maiden of Torment soon brutally disposed the weakened rogue to cement her death threats.

### The The Obsidian Soulcrystal

Due to the machinations of Morwen, Selthara's spirit eventually wound up in the The Obsidian Soulcrystal. Selthara was the first essence which Morwen marked; the ritual was quick, just as Sorvax had promised. As she drew Selthara's soul into the The Obsidian Soulcrystal, Selthara looked into Morwen's soul as she tried to subdue hers. The Maiden of Torment discovered what Morwen was trying to do, and fought as hard as she could. Morwen later reflected that if Selthara had been fully formed, she would have been defeated. Nevertheless, Anguish's essence was marked.

Over 20 years after her defeat, Selthara become merged with Sorvax, now the sole Sovereign Evil left. After Sorvax's defeat, all the lords of the Depths were returned to the The Obsidian Soulcrystal, with their souls screaming for release. The stone was destroyed by Nocthar in an effort to defeat the Vergalem, but he was defeated, and Sorvax freed as a result, still containing the essence of Selthara and the other Evils.

### The The Age of Ruin

Eventually, Selthara's spirit was able to break free from Sorvax, just as all the other Evils. Sometime in the following decades, she and the other The Minor Evils formed an alliance. They patiently developed their plans, waiting for the right time to find Vaerath unaware and ripe for the taking.

However, as punishment for aiding Sorvax during the Darkening of Ashveil, Selthara was chained and put in a yoke by her fellow The Minor Evils, due to how dangerous she was and that she couldn't be trusted.

### The Age of Malice

#### A Gathering Storm

Drakon planned to summon Selthara and take her power as part of his measures against the The Sovereign Evils. Unfortunately, the ritual was disrupted by the Wanderer and Bryndor Vahl, and the chosen sacrifice rescued.

As a result Taissa (the woman rescued from the sacrifice) was constantly plagued with the whispers and taunts of Selthara. with her voice getting louder over time.

Drakon tracked them down to an abandoned chapel in the outskirts of Ashvaren. He was able to capture Taissa and complete the ritual, using her as "a beacon across realms" to summon Selthara. Selthara was defeated by the Wanderer. Her shackles were left behind in the sand.

#### Narvast

Not long after Selthara's defeat, she and the other The Minor Evils (re)emerged in Vaerath. The plan they had set into motion earlier was initiated, and working together, the The Minor Evils began their takeover of Vaerath. In this, they were opposed by the Wanderer, who now had the aid of the Vaults. Selthara took up residence in the Narvast Undercity, corrupting its spirit beacons.
