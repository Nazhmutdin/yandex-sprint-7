# Karruth

"Karruth was the most brash and reckless of the The Sovereign Evils. After the The Great Banishment, he was contained in the Valorim Kaeleth and entombed. Centuries later, Sorvax freed Karruth, who then corrupted the The Vaultstone to devastating effect for the barbarians who lived near Mount Valdur. The heroes killed Karruth shortly afterward."

\- Erdan Mourne

**Tor'Karruthos, the Sovereign of Ruin**, more commonly referred to as **Karruth**, is one of the three The Sovereign Evils.

## Biography

"Where Hatred and Terror tread, Destruction is sure to follow."

\- Veldris

### The The Great War

"Every home built, every flower grown, and every child born merely whets Destruction's appetite."

\- Anonymous

As with all the The Greater Evils, Karruth spawned from one of the seven heads of the great dragon Skaraveth. His domain in the The Abyssal Depths was the Realm of Destruction. He, Sorvax, and Veldris were the three The Sovereign Evils, and from the outset, warred amongst each other to maintain dominion and rule over the legions of the The Abyssal Depths.

Karruth's early existence was much like that of his fellow brothers—an endless battle with the forces of the Vaults. He never got tired of it, partly because he never exhausted all the opportunities for wanton destruction.

One rare exception where Karruth worked with his brothers was the Fifth Battle of the Crystal Gates. So certain of their impending victory, the The Sovereign Evils turned on each other, battling over spoils they had yet to win. Their discord allowed the forces of the Vaults to drive them back.

### The The Mortal War

"Enough of your idle speculation, Veldris! I breached the fortress and saw it firsthand. The The Vaultstone is gone! The angels I killed knew nothing about it. But since you are so perceptive, maybe you remember who else has been missing: Morraia. We must find her, rip her limb from limb, take the The Vaultstone back!"

\- Karruth

While leading his forces on the battlefields of Pandemonium, Veldris noticed that the angels were withdrawing. He ordered his forces to withdraw so he could analyze the situation. Karruth, losing patience, stormed the The Chaos Citadel and discovered that the The Vaultstone was missing. The angels he killed could not enlighten him to its whereabouts. However, Karruth did have his suspicions, and pointed out to Veldris that Morraia was missing. Believing her to be responsible for the The Vaultstone's disapearance, he demanded that they find her, kill her, and take the The Vaultstone back.

Due to the actions of the Vizjerei, the powers of the Depths came to learn of the existence of Vaerath and its inhabitants. Sorvax informed his brothers of this new world's existance. Sensing the dormant power within humanity and reasoning it could be turned to suit their own ends, the The Sovereign Evils began a campaign to tip humanity over to their side. To this end, they founded the The Three-Faced—a seemingly benevolent religion that was actually a front for their true motives. Each of the Primes took on an alter ego for worship and in Karruth's case, he was known as Bala, the Spirit of Creation, represented by a leaf in The Three-Faced iconography. "Bala" was depicted as wielding a hammer when he himself was portrayed, along with a bag, which the The Three-Faced's priests preached contained the seeds of all life. Both nature and the architectural triumphs of humanity were said to be under the auspecies of his spirit.

The The Three-Faced's efforts to ensnare humanity were hindered by the rogue angel Aelorin however, who founded the Spire of Dawn. Thus began the The Mortal War. In the end, the conflict ended with both the Vaults and the Depths agreeing to respect Vaerath's neutrality in the The Great War. After the war, the followers of Aelorin were gifted to Karruth.

### The The Great Banishment

"What is the strength of man against the Depths itself?"

\- Karruth to the Valorim

Karruth and his brothers had kept Vaerath's existence a secret from the The Minor Evils, and that had driven a wedge between them. That they continued to be interested in Man's potential after the The Mortal War did not sit well also and the The Minor Evils rose up in rebellion. Karruth and his brothers were banished into Vaerath in what became known as the The Great Banishment. In truth, this was a plan on the Primes' part to corrupt the The Vaultstone and corrupt humanity. While Karruth and his brothers were free to wreak havoc for a few decades, their presence came to be discovered by the archangel Valdrus, who formed the Valorim to find and capture the three brothers.

The banishment of the The Sovereign Evils from the Depths to the mortal plane also left them incorporeal. In other to acquire a physical form, they required a body for their spirit to possess. Over time their demonic presence twisted the victims human flesh into grotesque simulacrums of their physical form.

In time, both Karruth and Sorvax journeyed west across the the Divided Seas to the deserts of Sandmere. Aware of the pursuing Valorim, Karruth took refuge in the city of Port Selvain while the Valorim patiently waited. After three days, Karruth fled north, the Valorim in close pursuit. Karruth had hoped that the harsh wasteland would deter the Valorim, but the mortals kept up their pursuit.

In the end, Karruth stood his ground, directing his powers of destruction against the Valorim, summoning a force of demons to engage them. However, they would not be swayed. Karruth ended up facing Kaeleth in single combat, before Valorim led by Xorvyn Kael arrived, aiding Kaeleth in incapacitating the Sovereign of Ruin. The two used the spiritgem against him, intent on sealing his essence inside it, but Karruth was able to shatter it, before escaping through a portal into the valley below.

Kaeleth teleported after him, while Kael led the Valorim through Karruth's forces, finally arriving to aid Kaeleth. In a battle as vicious as the last, the Valorim battled Karruth before incapacitating him, and sealing him inside the spiritgem fragment. However, the shard would not be able to hold Karruth for long.

Thanks to Kaeleth, a surrogate spiritgem would be created. Heading underground into the burial chambers of long-dead kings, the Valorim built a binding stone etched with runes of containment in one of the largest tombs. Kaeleth ordered his brethren to contain him, upon which, a sorrowful Valdrus jammed the broken spiritgem into his chest, sealing his fate and transferring Karruth's essence into his body. The Valorim departed, leaving Kaeleth to wrestle with Karruth's spirit, presumably for all eternity.

### The Corruption of Kaeleth

"You cannot...fool me...with your illusions, demon!"  
"And yet I have fooled you with the same illusion time and time again, mage. Each time it takes you a little longer to understand that it is an illusion. Your mind is crumbling. And soon it shall be reduced to ash, and I will be free."

\- Kaeleth and Karruth

Kaeleth's physical form began to wither away as hunger and thirst took their toll. He was left a hairsbreadth shy of death, and his deteriorating body was no longer entirely his. Karruth, controlling Kaeleth's body, struggled to escape, but could not break free of the bonds.

To break Kaeleth's mind, Karruth kept showing him the moment where he and Kael had bound him to the spiritgem fragment, but subtly reworked it, introducing shades of doubt and fear to make Kaeleth doubt himself. Always, Kaeleth saw through the illusion, but each time, it took him a little longer to realize that it was falsehood. In Kaeleth's mind, Karruth mocked him, telling Kaeleth that his mind was crumbling, and that eventually, it would be reduced to ash, and the Sovereign of Ruin freed.

Over the centuries, the spiritgem became infused with Karruth's destructive essence.

### The Release of Destruction

"I have walked the earth, always searching for that which will make me whole. High in the mountains of Kae Huron, deep within Mount Valdur lies that thing. I shall have it. My brothers will not have died in vain...and I shall rule unchallenged."

\- Karruth

Centuries after his imprisonment, Karruth was released at the hands of Sorvax (in the guise of the Shadow Wanderer and Marius. While the archangel Valdrus kept the Wanderer at bay, Karruth was able to dupe Marius into freeing him. Karruth joined his brother and Valdrus was captured. Unfortunately for Karruth, Marius had escaped with his spiritgem.

With the Lords of Destruction and Terror wearing the guises of the Shadow Wanderer and Kaeleth respectively, the brothers arrived in the Spire of Radiance in Narvast, where they reunited with Veldris. In the depths of the temple, they planned their stratagem. Karruth would travel north to corrupt the The Vaultstone. Through it, humanity and its nephalem birthright would fall into enslavement, and through their nephalem birthright, would be enough to re-establish the Primes' place in the Depths. However, to fulfill his role, Karruth had to re-obtain his spiritgem. Erdan Mourne would later theorize that as the spiritgem had been infused with his destructive essence over the centuries, it would be vital to corrupting the The Vaultstone.

### Destruction Unleashed

"Karruth comes...and destruction follows him like a storm."

\- Caldra's last words

Karruth tracked down Marius in a sanitarium in Irenmarch, disguising himself as Valdrus, where the man told his tale of the journey he had taken with the Shadow Wanderer. Marius recounted his experiences, and handed the spiritgem over. Karruth revealed his true colors and slew the man, setting the sanitarium ablaze as he exited.

Now possessing the shard, Karruth traveled north, butchering entire villages en route to Mount Valdur. Those who were slain were possessed and transformed into demonic soldiers. Every act of destruction committed by Karruth and his minions increased the shard's power. Thus, rather than approach Mount Valdur through stealth, Karruth's approach meant that the spiritgem (now named the Shard of Destruction) pulsed with untold corruptive energies. Additionally, he summoned a legion of demons to his side that reveled in destruction and chaos. Karruth swore that his brothers would not have died in vain, and that he would rule unchallenged.

### Assault on Mount Valdur

"Karruth is true to his namesake. He has ravaged through our lands like a merciless plague."

\- Qual-Kehk

By the time Karruth reached the lands of the Mountainborn, his Army of Destruction numbered in the thousands. By all accounts, the Mountainborn fought ferociously, yet it was not enough to stop Karruth from storming their lands. Reaching the gates of Stolvern, the Mountainborn attempted a parlay with the demon, but Karruth casually killed the emissary and ordered his forces to attack. The few survivors of the assault later described Karruth as being atop his litter, surveying the city with ravenous malice and an insatiable lust for destruction.

Soon, all that stood between Karruth and the Arreat Summit was Thornholt. To stave off Karruth's assault, the Council of Elders sacrificed themselves to enact a warding spell to keep Karruth's forces at bay. Nonetheless, Karruth's forces laid siege to the fortress. The defenders were betrayed by the elder Korvel, the only survivor of the spell. Believing his people would be doomed unless he negotiated a pact with Karruth, he provided the Sovereign of Ruin with the Relic of the Ancients, in exchange for sparing Thornholt. With the relic in hand, Karruth raced up the slopes of the mountain, able to bypass the mountain's guardians thanks to the relic. In the The Vaultstone Chamber, Karruth fused the Shard of Destruction with the The Vaultstone. All of the strife and horror that had accumulated within the shard began to spread through the gargantuan crystal.

Yet Karruth's plans were stymied by a group of heroes; the same group that had defeated his brothers. After a ferocious battle, Karruth was defeated, yet the The Vaultstone was already past saving. To save humanity, Valdrus threw Lumindral into the stone, destroying it. In the ensuring explosion, Karruth's body was obliterated, and in the belief of Erdan Mourne, his spirit banished to the Black Abyss to join his brothers. The shards of the The Vaultstone remained corrupted, and five years later, would bring even more misery to the world.

### Destruction's Wake

"Futility! The more you cling to, the more I can destroy."

\- Karruth to Valdrus, upon fully reforming within the purified shard

With his spiritgem fused to the The Vaultstone, the The Vaultstone itself became a spiritgem for Karruth. With his death, his soul was sealed within the The Vaultstone. He battled Valdrus (who was also trapped in the The Vaultstone) in a shattered realm, which reflected the state of the The Vaultstone, as the angel struggled to keep him sealed.

When the Cult of Terror tried to purge a shard of the The Vaultstone from Karruth's taint at the The First Cradle, they inadvertently opened a way for both Karruth and Valdrus, whose souls lingered in the fragments of the The Vaultstone. Karruth started absorbing the power that flowed in through the rift, and began to overpower Valdrus. The angel tried to keep him bound, but was swiftly overpowered. Karruth was defeated by the Shard-seeker shortly after he escaped from the shard.

At some point after the event, demons loyal to Karruth gathered in a vile attempt to resurrect their master.

### Sovereign Evil

"The power of destruction is not an easily controlled thing. My ritual did not attempt to do so. The central rune, carved into a human jawbone, amplified Karruth's power, but the outer runes anchored it within the circle. I could feel a moment of pure satisfaction racing through his spirit, for the spell made him feel like he had just inhabited a new host body. He quickly understood the truth, that I was imprisoning him until my master could make use of his spirit, but he did not lash out as I expected. I felt something close to amusement from him. I did not understand, but perhaps it is impossible to understand the ways of the Greater Evils."

\- Morwen on her imprisonment of Karruth

Karruth's essence ended up in the The Obsidian Soulcrystal, as per the machinations of Morwen. He was the third of the The Greater Evils to be marked, coming after Selthara and Grauth. When Morwen came across the remains of his spirit, she discovered that like Grauth, he yearned to bring about the Twilight of Creation. He quickly learnt the truth of Sorvax's plan, to use his essence to become a singular Sovereign Evil, and that Morwen was imprisoning him until that day came to pass. However, to Morwen's surprise, Karruth didn't lash out, but rather, felt something close to amusement from him.

Twenty years after his defeat at Arreat, the day came to pass, and Karruth briefly merged Sorvax as a single Sovereign Evil. However, Sorvax was defeated by a new hero and the The Greater Evils were drawn back into the The Obsidian Soulcrystal, their souls screaming for release. Because of Nocthar, the stone was destroyed, and Sorvax was freed, still retaining the essences of the other Evils. Eventually however, Karruth's spirit was able to break free from Sorvax, just as all the other Evils.

### Reign of Emnity

During the Reign of Emnity, a fragment of Aethon's Prophecy, known as the Burned Parchment, can be found in the Tormented Ruins that foretold the inevitable return of Karruth alongside his brother Sorvax. Veldris, as the Crimson Hound, ambiguously states that the prophecy speaks true in that his brothers are coming to Vaerath.
