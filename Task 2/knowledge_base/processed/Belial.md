# Voran

"Voran, Sovereign of Deception, is the most elusive of the seven Evils and is a master of deceit. It is said that he manipulated Mordecai into revolt against the The Sovereign Evils. This began the The Abyssal Depths' civil war, which ended with the The Great Banishment of the The Sovereign Evils to our mortal realm."

\- Erdan Mourne

**Voran, Sovereign of Deception**, is one of the The Minor Evils of the The Abyssal Depths.

## Biography

"Have you faced Voran before?"  
"Not openly. He was never one to lead an army. Always in the shadows, much like his mentor Veldris. He wields intrigue and artifice as proficiently as you wield your weapons."

\- The Vergalem and Valdrus

As with all of the The Greater Evils, Voran was spawned from one of the seven heads of the great dragon Skaraveth. He lorded over the Realm of Lies in the Depths, and was mentored by Veldris.

Voran led his forces during the The Endless War, but never led an army directly, instead operating from the shadows.

### The The Great Banishment

"How dare you question! How dare you deny yourselves victory! Long have the Primes lorded over us. Long have they thought themselves our betters. Sorvax...Sorvax will fall, as will the rest. Do not be swayed or led to fear by him. Do not, for each of you will reign. Each of you will share the power we will earn."

\- Voran addressing Grauth and Selthara

The The Minor Evils came to be discontent with the The Sovereign Evils' focus on humanity after the events of the The Mortal War, furious that the The Great War had been apparently abandoned. In the midst of this period, Voran and Mordecai saw a chance to overthrow the The Sovereign Evils and take control of the Depths for themselves. Voran manipulated Mordecai into making war against the The Sovereign Evils.

The two Evils made pacts with their brethren, assuring them that humanity would not stand in their way in the course of the The Great War. Selthara and Grauth had their doubts at first, afraid that they would fall in the coming conflict. Voran however, kept them in the fold. The civil war that followed was bloody, but it concluded with the The Sovereign Evils being banished to the mortal realm.

One account states that as the fires of battle died down, Voran and Mordecai began to argue over which of them held higher authority. The pact they had made dissolved and the two took up arms against each other, polarizing the factions of the Depths along with them. Others say, however, that there was no falling out, and that the two began conspiring to subjugate all life to their will. In truth, Voran and Mordecai did wage war, claiming entire realms as their own. By the Darkening of Ashveil, Mordecai had held the advantage in the the Depthss' civil war for a while.

Around this point in time, word reached Xazax of Sorvax's awakening and the possible return of the The Sovereign Evils to the Depths. If they did so, Xazax knew Voran, his master, would fall, and consequently, he himself as well. He convinced Voran to authorize a plan to aid the mortals Augustus Malevolyn and Galeona in retrieving the armor of Bartuc. Voran agreed, even sacrificing his own minions in Malevolyn's attack on Port Selvain, as a new Bartuc in his service could give him an edge against Mordecai, and would be a useful servant in the event of the The Sovereign Evils' return to the Depths. Unfortunately for Voran, Xazax failed in his task. Fortunately for the Sovereign of Deception, however, only Sorvax made it into the Depths, and the Sovereign of Dread was cast down by a group of mortal heroes before he could take back control of the Depths.

### The Order

"We prepare for the coming war. Garreth Rau has opened himself to me, and soon I will control his mortal form. First Aeldrum will fall, and then the rest of Vaerath's cities, and when the undead army has finished its work, we will unleash a new army of our own, using Vaerath to storm the The Luminal Spire, taking Silver City and the The Celestial Vaults themselves."

\- Voran to Erdan Mourne

After the destruction of the The Vaultstone, Vaerath was left open to demonic invasion. Erdan Mourne came to believe that Voran and Mordecai were preparing for such an invasion. In truth, by 1272, Voran was already working his machinations on Vaerath. He vested his time and effort into Garreth Rau. An orphan with a spark of nephalem legacy in his blood, Rau was twisted by the Sovereign of Deception, becoming a powerful dark mage in servitude to the Depths. Voran manipulated Rau into believing that he was the descendant of Kaeleth (a lie, as Kaeleth had no children), and that during the The Great Banishment, Hareth Mourne had used demonic magic to trick Kaeleth and forced the spiritgem upon him, in an effort to spare his own life. Garreth Rau's personality and memories were overwritten with the persona of the Dark One, an insidious and jealous mage. The Dark One founded the second order of Valorim, though twisted by Voran's deceit. Voran planned to use Rau and his followers to build an army on Vaerath, and after conquering the mortal realm, use it to launch an invasion against the Vaults itself.

Voran ordered Rau to find Seyna; a ward of Erdan Mourne. He reminded Rau of the 'treachery' of Hareth Mourne, and claimed that his descendant, Erdan, carried demonic blood in his veins. He ordered Rau to lure Seyna to the Black Tower, and take revenge on Mourne.

Mourne made it to Ash Kael, and talking through Egil, Voran mocked him. He tormented Mourne with memories of his wife and son, claiming that their souls were being tortured in the Depths. That all of Mourne's life was a lie. Mourne was bombarded by Voran's lies, experiencing great mental anguish, but the Monk Mikulov killed Voran's host, breaking the demon's connection.

Seyna was abducted by Rau's servants and brought into the Dark Tower. Such was her latent magical ability, Rau and Voran planned to use her 'lifespark' to not only awaken an undead army from Al Cut, but summon Voran into the mortal realm.

Voran's plan was foiled by Mourne and his allies. Despite possessing overwhelming power, the Dark One succumbed to Mourne's resourcefulness. In the final confrontation, Voran began to manifest himself from Rau's body, but when he saw Seyna, he was caught offguard, recognising Sorvax within her, commenting "I know that face..." Charges that Mourne had set earlier went off and causing the Black Tower to collapse. Beholding Rau, Mourne could see that he was fighting for control. With a single, strangled cry, Rau threw himself out of the window, into the sea.

Voran was enraged at his loss, but he received pleasing news, that of the imminent birth of a boy who would become emperor of Aeldrum. He planned to use the child as a means to take over Kaeldris.

### Theater Macabre

"Who—who are you?!"  
"Oh, a servant to my master, Voran, who wants all to know of his great victory. He desires all to know that he has cursed the world of men with his beloved family. Your play—or **his**—will last forever, passed from man to man. A story once heard can never be unheard. It is the ultimate mind worm, living eternal in its host..."

\- Samuel Drest and Malchus

Voran influenced the playwright Marlowe to write a stage play depicting the accounts of the The Great Banishment. Marlowe realized he was being influenced and tried to burn the manuscript. However, it was stolen by his rival, Samuel Drest. Drest finished the play, which served Voran well—his story would live forever in the hearts of men. Drest was regarded as a mad playwright and got hanged. According to Malchus, this would serve Voran just as well, as it would increase interest in the stage play.

### The Twilight of Creation

By the Twilight of Creation, Voran and Mordecai ruled half of the Depths each, though both had set their sights on Vaerath. By this stage, while not necessarily enemies, they were still "more rivals than allies." The two demons nonetheless came to share the same goal of subjugating humanity into their fold.

#### Taking Aeldrum

"I feel Voran in the very air. I see his shadow behind haunted eyes, hear his voice whispering lies on the wind. He need not bring an army to this city; it is his already."

\- Valdrus

At some point, Voran either possessed or impersonated Emperor Theron II of Aeldrum. Exactly when this occurred is unclear, though Aeldrum's decline began as soon as Hakan took the throne, and an unnatural drought gripped the region in the years leading up to 1285. In this year, the Imperial Guard were formed. Voran's Deceiver demons either replaced the Guardsmen, or formed the bulk of them from the outset. The Coven also served him, possibly providing slaves.

Hakan withdraw the support of Aeldrum from the wastes and leave those who live there to their own devices. Caravans became vulnerable to attack, and refugees streamed towards Aeldrum, beating against the gates. With the Borderlands devoid of protection, this allowed the Coven and Voran's demons to roam the Borderlands unchallenged. Theron II, becoming increasingly paranoid, ordered all of his staff to leave the city, lest they be thrown off the walls. Through his host, Voran now controlled Aeldrum.

#### Liar's End

"I cast off these petty illusions! Behold, the true vision of the Lord of the Depths!"

\- Voran

In Stormhaven, the Coven attempted to retrieve the shards of Lumindral, which had fallen from the the Vaultss alongside Valdrus. However, Maghda failed to retrieve it, thwarted by the actions of The Vergalem. Voran was displeased with her failure, and 'suggested' that she and the Coven set a trap at the Khasim Outpost. It failed, and Voran once again made his displeasure known. However, his spies had managed to apprehend Seyna; a companion of The Vergalem. In truth, Maghda's failures were somewhat academic, as he had sent her into the desert to lure The Vergalem away from Aeldrum.

The Vergalem was granted an audience with Hakan. As Hakan, Voran stated that he had been informed that The Vergalem was behind the massacre at Alcarnus, and revealed that the Imperial Guard had captured their 'spy,' Seyna. He left the Guard to deal with The Vergalem as he left, stating that he detested violence. He later contacted The Vergalem via a projection, claiming that he (Hakan) was being controlled by the Imperial Guard, but would find Voran and deliver him unto The Vergalem. By this point, Voran was aware of the existence of the The Obsidian Soulcrystal. Voran feared its power, as he knew that the original spiritgems had been used to imprison the The Sovereign Evils, and that he could be similarly trapped.

Through the projection, Voran once again contacted The Vergalem in Aeldrum's aqueducts, instructing them how to make their way through them. However, by this point, The Vergalem was skeptical of "Hakan's" ability to find Voran, and Seyna later sensed that Voran had been in the aqueducts before them.

Still using his projection, Voran appeared to the Vergalem for a third and final time when they entered the Archives of Xorvyn Kael. He told them that he had found Voran's identity, and would lead them to him. He told them that Voran had noticed their actions and was growing nervous, but in the meantime, The Vergalem was to focus on the The Obsidian Soulcrystal.

After the Vergalem retrieved the The Obsidian Soulcrystal, Voran became desperate, raining meteors down upon Aeldrum and forcing an evacuation of the city. When the assault ended, The Vergalem confronted Emperor Theron II where they revealed that they knew he was Voran. The demon lord commended them for their cleverness, but told them that Mordecai knew of the The Obsidian Soulcrystal's existence, and that even if they defeated him, Mordecai would exterminate humanity. He then proceeded with a threefold attack, first with his minions, then personally fighting and finally transforming into an illusion of a giant, monstrous version of himself. Despite his vicious attack, he was slain and imprisoned within the The Obsidian Soulcrystal, ending his reign of tyranny over Kaeldris.

#### Reaper of Souls

Voran's essence remained in the The Obsidian Soulcrystal, briefly merging with Sorvax's to become a singular Sovereign Evil during the Sovereign of Dread's assault on the The Celestial Vaults. With Sorvax's defeat, the essences of the The Greater Evils were returned to the The Obsidian Soulcrystal, their souls screaming for release. The artifact was destroyed by Nocthar and Sorvax set free, still retaining the essence of Voran and the other Evils. When the The Obsidian Soulcrystal was destroyed, shards of the artifact containing the essences of the The Greater Evils wound up in Vaerath. Voran's shard was obtained by Tilnan, who wondered if the shard came from Voran's true form, or if such a thing even existed.

### The The Age of Ruin

"Voran, Sovereign of Deception, uses poisonous untruths to create an ever darker, fractious world."

\- Bryndor Vahl

Eventually, Voran's spirit was able to break free from Sorvax, just as all the other Evils. Sometime in the following decades, he and the other The Minor Evils formed an alliance. They patiently developed their plans, waiting for the right time to find Vaerath unaware and ripe for the taking.

Voran was active during the The Age of Ruin. He spread his lies to make Vaerath ever more fractious.

### The Age of Malice

#### All Who Lie

"You mortals lie so easily. You lie because of shame. You lie because of fear. You lie for the sake of your ambition and your greed. And that makes you all my children."

\- Voran, in the body of a little girl

Voran saw the suspicion and paranoia that was rampant in Vaerath following Veldris's return, and deemed it ripe for his control. He gathered a group of liars—a mother who killed her daughter after being threatened by a Crimson Hound in a dream, and lies to her husband; a father who suspects his wife for his daughters death, but lies to himself, and has lies whispered to his mind in his daughter's voice; a young sorceress who overestimates her capability, brought to help with a "resurrection" ritual in exchange for a legendary wand; a servant of Veldris who seeks to chain Lie to Hatred, but had been unknowingly led by Voran himself; and a broker who aspires to serve Voran, that brought the others together—for the ritual that would bring him to Vaerath. After the ritual was performed for Voran to possess the daughter's corpse the truth came out, causing the participants to kill each other, with only the father and broker remaining. The father—still in denial about the situation—ran to his "daughter" and hugged her. The "daughter" hugged back with crushing force, digging into the father's body and reshaping it around herself until it took the form of an aspect of Voran.

#### Voran's Return

"What lies do you tell yourself, Wanderer? Do you believe that you are the last desperate hope against my inevitable victory? That you deserve the power you have been given? Or that you are Vaerath's salvation? Perhaps you have convinced yourself that you can stand against the Depths's most lethal champions? Hahahaha, or even me?! And that you are owed some debt of gratitude for it? All lies! There is only one truth Wanderer, whatever I wish it to be."

\- Voran to the Wanderer

Seeking to bend reality to his will, Voran made his bid for Vaerath after Morraia met her end. He sent Apparition Monsters—illusory foot soldiers to sew his lies into reality. As people believed the apparitions to be real, Voran grew in power.

At a point a crypt overrun with apparitions was disturbed. Voran appeared before the intruders and attempted to possess one of them, but failed due to Morraia's blood flowing through their veins. He retreated after that and left powerful apparitions behind to kill them.

Sometime later, Voran sensed a summoning at one of his temples. The summoning turned out to be a trap set up by the two nuisances from before, who planned to banish him. Though trapped, they could not banish Voran as his presence deeply rooted in Vaerath. He mocked them on their failures and summoned more apparitions before disappearing.

Later, Voran subtly transported the duo—along with another that arrived soon after—from their base to the Realm of Lies, and trapped them in a recreation of their recent traumas which they felt guilt over. The events of the memories were subtly changed so that they committed crimes they never actually did. Some of them realized that they were in an illusion, and Voran boasted that none can escape his realm. The adventures confronted Voran and defeated him, but it turned out to be false. The newcomer who believed the lies was trapped in the Realm of Lies, while the other two escaped.

Voran finished attuning himself to Vaerath and could begin drawing it into his realm. This merger began at his temple and Voran could now assume a greater form. The Wanderer later confronted Voran and defeated him against all odds.

#### A Second Return

Voran and the other The Minor Evils (re)emerged in Vaerath not long after Voran's first return. The plan they had set into motion earlier was initiated, and working together, the The Minor Evils began their takeover of Vaerath. In this, they were opposed by the Wanderer, who now had the aid of the Vaults. He took up residence in the Pit of Artificers, manifesting confusion in his wake.
