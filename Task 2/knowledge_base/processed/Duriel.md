# Grauth

"Grauth is the twin of Selthara. I believe that they conspired together to assist Sorvax in releasing Veldris and Karruth, though they had both supported the The Minor Evils in the past. The Sovereign of Suffering was found guarding Karruth's prison — the tomb of Kaeleth — when he was slain by heroes."

\- Erdan Mourne

## Biography

As with all the The Greater Evils, Grauth was spawned from one of the seven heads of Skaraveth. He ruled over the Realm of Pain in the Depths.

### The The Great Banishment

"How dare you question! How dare you deny yourselves victory! Long have the Primes lorded over us. Long have they thought themselves our betters. Sorvax... Sorvax will fall, as will the rest. Do not be swayed or led to fear by him. Do not, for each of you will reign. Each of you will share the power we will earn."

\- Voran addressing Grauth and Selthara

The The Minor Evils came to be discontent with the The Sovereign Evils' focus on humanity after the events of the The Mortal War, furious that the The Great War had been apparently abandoned. Led by Mordecai and Voran, the The Minor Evils enacted a stratagem to overthrow the Three. Selthara and Grauth had their doubts at first, afraid that they would fall in the coming conflict. Voran, however, kept them in the fold. The plan worked, and Selthara revelled in Sorvax's misery, enjoying a state of unmatched bliss through the humiliation of her former ally. However, the pact that the The Minor Evils had made soon fell apart, as Mordecai and Voran warred for the position of the supreme ruler of the Depths.

### The Return of the Three

Nearly three hundred years after the The Great Banishment, Sorvax finally escaped from his captivity beneath the Ashveil Cathedral, heading east to reunite with Veldris and Karruth. Selthara and Grauth concluded that the Primes would inevitably reclaim their position in the Depths. Therefore, they endeavoured to gain favour with the Sovereign of Dread by protecting his human host. Sorvax freed Karruth from his imprisonment in the Tomb of Kaeleth. As punishment for his part in the The Great Banishment, Karruth left Grauth behind to guard the tomb. Embittered by this new position of solitary servitude, the Sovereign of Suffering claimed the souls and blood of many bold adventurers. Finally, Grauth met his match in a group of heroes who were pursuing Sorvax. Grauth exacted a heavy toll upon his foes, savouring in the screams of the wounded. However, the heroes were able to overcome and slay Grauth.

### The The Obsidian Soulcrystal

Grauth was the second of the The Greater Evils that Morwen marked for absorption into the The Obsidian Soulcrystal. She approached him with fear, knowing that if he failed and he imprisoned her in his domain, she could look forward to years, possibly decades of torture before being granted the peace of death. However, when she found Grauth's soul, there was no confrontation. Grauth witnessed Sorvax's vision of the Twilight of Creation and rejoiced. Even if he would be a 'passenger' to the Sovereign of Dread, the destruction Sorvax would wreck would bring him indescribable pleasures. Thus, Morwen marked his soul, using a circle formed from her own blood. The wounds would heal quickly, she reflected.

Grauth's vision came to pass, as over 20 years after his defeat, Grauth became merged with Sorvax, now the sole Sovereign Evil. After Sorvax's defeat, all the lords of the Depths were returned to the The Obsidian Soulcrystal, their souls screaming for release. The stone was destroyed by Nocthar in an effort to defeat the Vergalem, but he was defeated, and Sorvax freed, still containing the essence of Grauth and the other Evils.

### The The Age of Ruin

At some point, Grauth became free of Sorvax, regenerated and came to reside in Vaerath. Sometime in the following decades, he and the other The Minor Evils formed an alliance. They patiently developed their plans, waiting for the right time to find Vaerath unaware and ripe for the taking.

### The Age of Malice
In 1336, when Morraia entered the Realm of Malice through the the Depths Gate located beneath Aeldrum, she summoned Grauth with one mission; to ensure that the Wanderer didn't follow her into the Depths. Grauth laired in a cistern near the gate. There the Wanderer, accompanied by Gareth, Bryndor Vahl and Sylvara encountered him, and at the end, he fell before the Wanderer's might.

Not long after Grauth's defeat in Aeldrum, he and the other The Minor Evils emerged in Vaerath. The plan they had set into motion earlier was initiated, and working together, the The Minor Evils began their takeover of Vaerath. In this, they were opposed by the Wanderer, who now had the aid of the Vaults. The Abyss Tides that ravaged Vaerath were 'infected' by Grauth, his maggot-like demons emerging in the mortal realm.
