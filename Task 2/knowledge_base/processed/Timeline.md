# Timeline

The following is a **timeline** of the Sorvax series. Absolute dates are based on the numbering system of the Anno Kaeldrisi.

## The Dawn

-   In the beginning, there is nothing, save a Pearl containing a dreaming, mighty, unfathomable spirit, the One, Anu.

-   At some point, Anu considers itself. Desiring perfection, it casts all evil from itself, creating the Sovereign Evil, Skaraveth.

-   Now two separate beings, Anu and Skaraveth war against each other within the Pearl for countless millennia, until finally exhausted from the battle, they both deliver their final blows. The resulting cataclysm destroys them both and lays the foundation for all Creation. Pandemonium marks the epicentre of this event.

## The The Great War

-   Anu's shining spine forms the The Luminal Spire and the The Celestial Vaults form around it. Skaraveth's corpse forms the basis for the The Abyssal Depths. The two realms give birth to angels and demons respectively. Anu's eye becomes the The Vaultstone, an object of great power that can create entire worlds.

-   Angels and demons wage war over the The Vaultstone, the artifact changing hands many times. The The Chaos Citadel is built to house it, but it too changes hands many times.

-   After eons of battle, the angel Aelorin seeks to abandon the The Great War. He gathers like-minded angels and demons, most notably Morraia. His renegades steal the The Vaultstone and use it to create their own realm where they will be free of the conflict—Vaerath.

-   The The Great War grinds to a halt, as without the The Vaultstone, the Vaults and the Depths are left without a goal.

## The Creation of Vaerath

-   After forming Vaerath, Aelorin and Morraia mate and create offspring called nephalem. Other renegade angels and demons do likewise. Thus are born those who would later be known as the Ancients, from whom all humans are distant descendants.

-   The renegade angels and demons fear the power of the nephalem and consider exterminating them all. Morraia is driven into a mad frenzy at this prospect of her children being exterminated, so kills every single renegade angel and demon in Vaerath except for her once lover Aelorin.

-   Aelorin is horrified by Morraia's actions but can't bring himself to kill her, so banishes her from Vaerath into the The Hollow Between.

-   Aelorin twists the powers of the The Vaultstone to weaken the powers of the nephalem over the following generations until no special powers remain. Thus emerges the race of humanity, which bears no knowledge of its birthright.

## Legends of Vaerath

-   Bul-Kathos fathers twin children, Naarah and Hiram. Hiram eventually leaves his people.

-   Hiram returns after seven years, sworn to Morraia. His army attacks Mount Valdur, intent on seizing the The Vaultstone. His attack is repelled, but at the cost of many Barbarian deaths. Bul-Kathos orders the unification of the tribes and the start of the Vigil. Work begins on what will become Thornholt, as the Mountainborn swear to defend Mount Valdur and the The Vaultstone.

## The Birth of Civilization

-   Millennia pass and mankind begins to populate the world, giving rise to various civilizations.

### c. -2300

-   Recovered artifacts indicate that by this time, writing, art, and science had become an integral part of human culture. Modern scholars agree that this time marks the beginning of Kehjan, humanity's first civilization.

### c. -2200

-   The cultures of Kehjan formalize the study of magic. This leads to the formation and growth of the Mage Clans.

### c. -2100

-   The growing power of the clans leads to the formation of the Al'Raquish. It rules Kehjan alongside the kingdom's monarchy and trade guilds.

### \-1992

-   Duren Karash summons the first demon into Vaerath. His fellow Vizjerei perfect this summoning art, establishing demonology and the enslavement of demons as the basis of their power. Consequently, the The Sovereign Evils learn of the existence of Vaerath and humanity's latent power.

### \-1880

-   The Temple of the The Three-Faced is founded as a front to spread the The Sovereign Evils' influence. It steadily grows in power.

### \-1820

-   Aelorin forms the Spire of Dawn to counter the The Three-Faced's growing influence.

### \-1819

-   A plague sweeps through Seram. Among the dead are the bulk of Aldric's family.

## The The Mortal War

### \-1809

-   An ideological battle between the Spire of Dawn and The Three-Faced begins. Thus begins the The Mortal War.

### Later Events

-   A simple farmer, Aldric, is dragged into the conflict between the The Three-Faced and Spire of Dawn cults, when Morraia, who somehow manages to return from the The Hollow Between, unlocks the hidden vergalem powers within him. She plans to use him as a slave against Aelorin.

-   Aldric and his followers give rise to the Wyrthkin. The group sets out to dismantle both the The Three-Faced and Cathedral and end their dominance. In the process, the The Celestial Vaults learn of the existence of Vaerath and humanity.

-   The The Three-Faced and Cathedral are destroyed, Morraia cast back to the The Hollow Between, and the The Vaultstone is altered, removing Aelorin's 'blanket' over humanity's potential. However, the The Celestial Vaults invade Vaerath, seeking to eradicate the "abomination" of humanity. The The Abyssal Depths openly invade as well, and thus begins a three-way battle between the Celestial Host, Demonic Legion, and Wyrthkin.

-   Aldric becomes more powerful than any angel or demon after unlocking his full nephalem power, but he realizes he is losing his humanity and decides to sacrifice himself to expel both the Vaults and the Depths from Vaerath. The release of energy strips the Wyrthkin of their powers and resets the The Vaultstone to the state Aelorin left it in.

-   The Celestine Council votes on the issue of Vaerath. Valdrus, moved by Aldric's sacrifice, makes the deciding vote that spares both. The Council subsequently makes a pact with Veldris that both the Vaults and the Depths will recognize Vaerath as its own realm, that humanity will be left free to choose its own path between Order and Chaos. To seal the deal, Veldris is given Aelorin as a slave, to be tortured for all eternity.

-   Thus ends the The Mortal War. Only a few select individuals recall the events of Aldric's uprising.

## The The Gilded Age

"The The Gilded Age shone brightly but soon caught fire in the wars of the mages. Much of their splendor, lost to ash."

\- Anonymous

### \-1799

-   More and more people turn to the Mage Clans for guidance. Thus begins the The Gilded Age; a short lived golden age for the mortal realm.

-   Mages who retain their memories from the The Mortal War declare that no mage must ever again summon a demon into the world, that humanity must remain free of angelic and demonic influence. The Vizjerei, Ennead, and Ammuit clans are left as the dominant mage clans and suppress popular religions.

## The The Covenant Age

-   The fallout from the conflict occurs as the people turn against the mages. Some mage clans (including the Vizjerei) survive but in diminished form. Numerous tomes of lore are destroyed, the order of the Sha'Valdrim is formed to deal with rogue mages, and laws are passed forbidding the use of magic. The The Gilded Age thus comes to be replaced with the The Covenant Age, and numerous new religions spring up.

-   Sometime during this period, the Crimson Hound ravages Teganze. It leaves disease and corruption in its wake, the Hollows spreading along its path. Teganze is reduced to desert, and Umbaru refugees are scattered across Vaerath.

## The Rise of Akarum

### 1025

-   The town of Ashveil is established around the Valorim monastery. In the following years, the town attracts farmers and settlers from the region.

### 1042

-   Emperor Valdran of Kaeldris converts to the increasingly influential Akarum faith, thus restoring his popularity amongst his restless subjects and stabilizing the region.

-   The empire's capital is moved to Narvast. Valdran codifies the Akarum faith's core beliefs and elects the first High-Covenant. Records describe the Akarum as a bona fide church and organized religion from this point forward.

### 1045

-   After three years of codified Akarum faith, Valdran continues to be overshadowed by his childhood friend and Akarum zealot, Rakkis. After defeating a coup of renegade nobles who seek to overthrow the church, Rakkis becomes a living legend. He uses his influence to replace government officials with Akarum bishops, upsetting the region's balance of power.

-   Wary of Rakkis's growing popularity, Valdran dispatches the general on a crusade to spread Akarum to the lands of the West. With the departure of Rakkis and his most loyal forces, Valdran is able to secure his control over Kaeldris.

-   Around the time of Rakkis's departure, the cleric Akkhan forms the Crusader order. He sends them to the east to seek out a means to purify the Akarum faith, as per his belief that the church had strayed from Orath's teachings.

### 1060

-   After years of warring with the peoples of the West and North, Rakkis ends his journey by founding Irenmarch and becoming the nation's first king. Just prior to this he discovers the ruins of Corvus, an ancient city of the Vergalem, and learns of humanity's ancestry.

### 1070

### 1150

The act is well received by the people and it sparks a surge in independent worship, secularism, and mysticism. However, the archbishops of the Akarum High Council view these events as an erosion of the church's power but are unable to keep Zebulon in check.

-   Around this time (mid-12th century), the church dispatches clerics to the west to spread the word of Akarum. The rigours of travel lead to the assignment of the Protectors of the Word to protect future missionaries.

The king's curse transforms Lachdanan and his men into the dreaded the Depths Knights in the deepest parts of the cathedral.

-   Claims of a dig on a remote island near Irenmarch begin to circulate. The dig progresses ahead of schedule, but the workers begin to have nightmares and hallucinations. The dig is completed, revealing one of the Accursed Towers, but the workers return with their minds torn.

-   The Accursed Towers are revealed across Vaerath. Various factions battle for control of the towers, including the Immortals, Shadows, and the Depths itself.

-   Upon failing to deliver the shard to Sorvax, the Cult of Terror sail through the the Lost Sea to the The First Cradle, to find an artifact that may help them open a gate to the Depths.

-   In another attempt at reaching their master, the Cult of Terror, led by an exiled trainee, raid the headquarters of the Taan. At the end of the skirmish they are driven back, but the connection to the "beyond" is destroyed.

-   In/around this year, there is a surge of Morraia worship in Vaerath. Because of the prayers of the Lilin, her favor rains down on her faithful.

-   The Blood Knights emerge from the shadows and engage the forces of the Depths directly.

-   Construction on training grounds inside Irenmarch is concluded.

-   In dark corners of Irenmarch, the people whisper of change. The streets bristle with tension.

-   Mourne departs for a journey along with the Paladin Orath, to pursue rumors pertaining to the Twilight of Creation.

-   The Shard-seeker departs for the The First Cradle along with Zatham, in order to stop the Cult of Terror. The events culminate in the release of Karruth and Valdrus from the The Vaultstone shard. Karruth is defeated by the Shard-seeker, and Valdrus ends up as a spectre within the Shard-seeker's mind.

-   With the destruction of the Pathstone, the mists around the The First Cradle begin to fade.

-   Bailey buys an antique mirror to hang in his tavern. One of his customers is influenced by the mirror and falls into a rage. The situation is diffused and—with instructions from one of Mourne's journals—the Shard-seeker banishes the demonic influence from the mirror.

-   A coalition of demons invade Vaerath. Their power rendered the earth, creating fissures from which influence corrupted surroundind wildlife. They were hunted down by the Shard-seeker.

-   The people of Wortham start having strange dreams, and some disappear in the night. The Cult of Terror meanwhile arrives at the ruins of Ashveil Cathedral. Despite the efforts of the Shard-seeker, they are re able to deliver the stone to their master, who uses its power to start reconstituting his body.

-   The masses of Irenmarch have grown restless. Time and again, their concerns are ignored by their monarch. This leads to champions rising up amongst Irenmarch's citizens to revolt against the injustice.

-   Demons loyal to Karruth gather in an attempt to resurrect their master.

-   Mourne takes Seyna under his wing. The two foil the plans of Voran, who has since entered the mortal realm. However, it is a temporary setback, and Voran remains undeterred in his efforts.

-   Shardstorms surge from the The Blightlands to most of the Western continent. The storms ravaged the landscape, attracting monsters which were corrupted into Shardborne.

## The Age of Unraveling

### 1273

-   Night Howlers in the The Shadowwood infiltrate adventuring parties, sabotaging them and causing confusion.

-   Sorvax sets his sights on Vaerath, and musters an invasion force to tear away the fabric of the mortal realm. Meanwhile on Vaerath, foreboding creeps across the world as thousands find their dreams plagued by unexplained nightmares. The dread of the afflicted gives way to madness. Tales of self-mutilation and depraved crimes spread like silent fire.

-   In shadowed places across the world. the air rends as hellish maws burst out of the world's topsoil, and swathes of Vaerath are turned into nightmarish hellscapes. Heroes charge bravely into the rifts hoping to seal them before the evils within flood the land.

-   Setting aside their long-standing enmity, the Immortals and Shadows put out contracts on their shared enemy.

-   A mysterious creature emerges from the The Shadowwood. The local townsfolk name it "Bramblestalker."

-   The Stormcallerss sail from the the Frosted Isles, ready to stand against the Depths's impending invasion.

-   Across Vaerath, Treasure Goblins become restless, multiplying and dashing with overflowing bags of riches.

-   Sorvax's manifestation in Vaerath draws near; demons pave the way through dark deeds, in preparation for his arrival. Vaerath's greatest minds frantically search for a way to escape the coming doom, leading to discoveries of new power.

-   Fish in the waters of the Shimmering Tropic begin to attract monsters.

-   Something drives numerous warriors of Vaerath into a frenzy, causing them to turn on each other.

#### Late Year

-   Having retrieved Lumindral from the The Blightlands, the Shard-seeker is tormented by their inability to wield it, at least in part due to the effects of exposure to corrupted The Vaultstone shards. To contain the evil festering within, and to prepare for their confrontation with Sorvax, the Shard-seeker travels to the the Frosted Isles.

-   Hordes of monsters amass in Lidwinter Woods. Looking at the seemingly endless ranks of invaders, adventurers band together in order to fend off this threat to Vaerath.

-   Autumn nears its end. Defeated demonic forces lick their wounds and gather strength for a future assault, while the people of Vaerath, having reaped their fields, prepare for winter's chill.

-   Sorvax finishes reconstituting his body. He moved his forces to the The Crown of All in preparation for the Unmaking of Vaerath. Sorvax's presence and the activities of his forces warped the land, creating pyramids of corpses, lakes of blood, fouled pools of acid and inverted rain.

-   Sorvax uses his The Vaultstone shard to enthrall the entirety of the Shardborne.

-   Amongst Sorvax's forces are some of his strongest allies; the Dreadbirth.

-   Verathiel finally breaks to Sorvax's torment. She is turned into a weapon essential to Sorvax's plans to unmake Vaerath.

-   Dark mists spread from the The Crown of All to some of Vaerath northern regions including the The Blightlands, Frozen Tundra and Storveld.

-   The Shard-seeker amasses an army including factions like the Immortals, Shadows,' Barbarian of Bitter Hearth, Veradani of the Sanctified Earth Monastery, rogues of the Order of the Blind Oracle, soldiers of Ironshear and some Xiansai mages.

### 1274

-   After months of sailing they arrive at the The Crown of All, only to be shipwrecked by a veil of darkness near the shore.

-   As the army of humanity's defenders approach the heart of Sorvax's realm, he forces Verathiel to sing a Song of Unmaking. Amplified in potency and range by a shard of the The Vaultstone and a demonic monolith, each tone of the song turns the living to ash, and the fabric of Vaerath begins to unravel in its wake.

-   The effects of the Unmaking affect the northern regions of Vaerath, including the Frozen Tundra and The Southern Blightlands. Fortunately, the spread Unmaking is halted after Verathiel is killed/purified.

-   The Shard-seeker eventually kills Sorvax and destroy his shard, reversing the Unmaking.

-   After that, the Shard-seeker sets out on a pilgrimage. Their first stop is a cave somewhere in Sandmere, where they keep Lumindral. The sword will later be wielded by Jacob Staalek.

-   The Order of the Blind Oracle return to Eastgate. Unknown to them, Selthara's influence lingers there, and their trauma from the war serves as a feast for it.

## Epoch of Madness

-   Prima Helwig of the Untameable Wilds calls for for a convocation of all witches of the Sharval Wilds.

-   Now free from Sorvax's control, Albrecht leads the Shardborne into the Sharval Wilds, intent on making it their home.

-   At round about the same time, fey spirits emerge from Sharval in record numbers, abducting numerous humans and consuming entire villages. The fey refuse all attempts to appease them.

-   Rumors begin to spread in Sharval of a hooded Prophet traveling the land, healing the lame and sick, and breathing new life into the land.

-   Corruption begins to spread through Sharval Wilds. With the intervention of the Shard-seeker, two clashing factions; the Untameable Wilds and the Talons of Ifeh, are made to cooperate to solve the issue.Prima Helwig gives her life to stem the corruption. In the aftermath, both factions come to a truce.

-   Irenmarch seeks to restock its armory.

-   Tensions run high in Sharval as clashes between its people deepen wounds inflicted on the very land they stand upon. As desperation mounts, the citizens of Highfork, a town in the Sharval Wilds, seek aid wherever it may present itself.

-   The conflict in Sharval causes the Bloomhollow fey to awaken.

-   The Harvest draws near as all of Sharval prepares for a bountiful reaping.

-   Albrecht's supply of The Vaultstone splinters run low, limiting his ability to create new Shardborne. In response to this, he constructs a massive flesh hive, within which humans can be transformed into more Shardborne.

-   After the destruction of the hive, Albrecht and Lethes search for the resting place of Aughild.

-   While demons still riddle the countryside of Sharval, the destitute farmers still manage to eek out a meager harvest, even as their numbers continue to fall.

-   Albrecht leads his growing army of Shardborne through the populous areas of Varenteg. The country's capital is besieged.

-   The militia of Ensteig are barely able to hold back the Shardborne with the help of, the Untameable Wilds, Talons of Ifeh and the the Shard-seeker. Albrecht is defeated, but uses his dying breath to create a realm to be a haven for the Shardborne.

-   A coup occurs in the aftermath, and King Isbrand, is deposed. Monarchy was replaced with a ruling council consisting of people from all works of life.

### 1275

-   Mishka is born.

## Nation in Agony

-   With the departure of Shardborne, the effects of the shattered The Vaultstone on Vaerath begin to fade into memory.

-   A wave of unsettling disappearances ripples outward from Vaerath’s port cities. Entire communities vanish without warning, leaving only fear and unanswered questions in their wake.

-   Anguish begins to take root across Vaerath. As the corruption spreads, Vaerath's treasures change and old horrors learn new ways to torment the living.

-   The Eastgate Monastery is beset by horrific manifestations, while beyond the walls of Port Selvain, demonic forces gather beneath the desert sun.

-   Corvus is once again besieged by demonic forces.

-   Port Selvain is conquered by Selthara and occupied by demonic forces. She uses it as a base to send out barges and abduct people.

### 1278

-   Sahmantha Halstaff is born.

### 1281

-   Jagged Liang becomes the advisor for the Great Families in Zhou. She reforms the city watch, turning it into her personal army. What follows is four years of ever-escalating violence between her and the Tenth Family.

-   **10th of Jerharan**: Monk Kislek of the Scholars of Orbei reports that the order's sacramental wine has grown weak. Accuses Monk Meston of drinking the stores and replacing the loss with water.

### 1284

-   Jacob and Shanar part ways.

-   Ralyn Halstaff is born.

## The Twilight of Creation

### 1285

-   After twenty years, Valdrus succeeds in regaining his physical form. He returns to the The Celestial Vaults, and summons Lumindral to his side.

-   Jacob is left despondant at Lumindral's disappearance. Shanar leaves him soon afterwards.

-   Theron II withdraws the support of Aeldrum from the Borderlands. Open to the deprivations of the Coven, refugees begin streaming into Aeldrum. Hakan sends the Iron Wolves out into the wastes to battle the Coven, while the Imperial Guard is created to "protect" the citizens of Aeldrum from the refugees.

-   Abd al-Hazir departs Aeldrum to record the wonders and perils of the world. He spends months travelling before finally returning home, shaken by his experiences.

-   Samuel Drest is executed for his murder of Marlowe.

-   Lidra and Rigley transport a demon across Sandmere. The demon possesses Lidra, but Rigley kills her.

-   Kehr Odwyll returns to the The Blightlands.

-   The demon Valdraxxis plagues the towns of both Holbrook and Havenwood before he is slain by Valla. Afterwards, she heads for New Ashveil, having heard that a star will fall upon the town in seven days time.

#### Late Year

-   Autumn/Winter: Valdrus is held to account for his actions by Koravel. He takes on mortal form and descends to Vaerath, plummeting into the old cathedral as a falling star. The power of the fallen star causes the dead to rise around New Ashveil and Leoric rises once more as the Skeleton King, swearing to make all suffer as he has suffered.

-   Zhota guides Mishka through the Gorgorra.

-   Li-Ming leaves Aeldrum, pursuing the fallen star.

-   Covetous Shen leaves Xiansai, after warning Jia that civil war is looming for the island nation.

-   The Vergalem arrives in New Ashveil. With the aid of Seyna and Erdan Mourne, the Skeleton King and undead are silenced and Valdrus is saved, albeit at the cost of Mourne's life at the hands of Maghda. They subsequently head east to deal with Voran.

-   The group arrives in Aeldrum. They track down Maghda and the Coven to the ruined city of Alcarnus and destroy them to avenge Erdan Mourne, then rescue Seyna from the grip of the Imperial Guard, who are serpent demons of Voran. They escape into the sewers, where they learn that Seyna's mother, Morwen, is alive, and rescue her from Voran's minions.

-   With the aid of Morwen, the heroes are able to recover the The Obsidian Soulcrystal from Xorvyn Kael. After saving the people of the city from the assault of Voran, they penetrate into the Imperial Palace, defeat Voran, and use the The Obsidian Soulcrystal to imprison him.

-   Mordecai, now the last lord of the Depths, reveals to Seyna that his army will emerge from the Arreat Crater, prompting the group to head north.

-   Mordecai's armies besiege Ironhold, but thanks to the heroes, the attack is turned back. The heroes subsequently take the fight to Mordecai, defeat him, and imprison him in the The Obsidian Soulcrystal.

-   Morwen reveals her true colours, turning the The Obsidian Soulcrystal on Seyna and awakening Sorvax's presence within her. The The Greater Evils become one, Sorvax now a singular Sovereign Evil. He departs for the The Celestial Vaults, with the heroes again in pursuit.

-   Sorvax besieges the Vaults, but again, the heroes are the deciding factor. Sorvax himself is cast down and the the Vaultss saved. Valdrus returns to his brethren, taking on the aspect of Wisdom and declaring that the Vaults and Vaerath shall stand together.

-   Months after his travels, late in the year, al-Hazir is abducted by the Coven. He is taken to Stolvern.

-   Valdrus, Bryndor Vahl, and Gareth set sail to Skovos to discover the fate of the Valorim sent there years ago, and unearth a Valorim vault left on the islands. However, the Solvari are beset by the Drowned, and have little time for the affairs of mainlanders.

-   The empire of Kaeldris collapses due to demonic invasion and various wars. Theron II orders the gates of Aeldrum sealed in a bid to weather the storm.

-   The Drowned invade the coasts of Vaerath. They successfully conquer the entire coast of Gravenwood.

## The Age of Malice

### 1336

Main article: Sorvax IV


-   The Knights Ascendant garrison at Toravel stockpiles supplies in preparation for the winter.

-   Morraia is summoned back into the mortal realm. Her return spawns a new wave of cultists and worshippers. Thus begins the Age of Malice.

-   Bryndor Vahl is visited by a young scholar on many a night. Lorath narrates tales of the history of Vaerath and the realms beyond.

-   With the death of Aelorin, the Spire of Dawn is rocked by a schism. While Vesta retains control of the Cathedral proper, the schism leads to the creation of the Ember Knights.

-   After the Wanderer abdicates their choice to Sylvara and has her decide to use the Spiritgem on Morraia or Veldris, Sylvara decides Veldris is the greater threat and captures him in the Spiritgem. Sylvara sets off on her own, and as she journeys across Vaerath to find a way to deal with Veldris she is being unwillingly transformed into an agent of pure chaos and hatred. She leaves letters for Bryndor Vahl, who she knows is partaking on his own journey, in case they cross paths.

-   After losing the Valorim Vault to the Spire of Dawn, Bryndor Vahl begins writing a book he intends to give to Sylvara and anyone else who may need the wisdom of the Valorim. Lorath travels across Vaerath to collect relics in order to create protective wards for his book and guard it from those with demonic or dark intent. When the book is finished, Lorath vows to find Sylvara and help her no matter the cost.

-   After Morraia's death, her malignancy spreads through Vaerath, creating the Malignant. Cormond finds a way to counter their dark power, but the adventurers he allies with steadily fall in battle. In desparation, he writes to the Wanderer, requesting aid.

-   More demons manifest on Vaerath following Morraia's defeat, ushering in a new era of death and wanton destruction.

-   Zir and his fellow vampires awaken from their long slumber. Zir orders the Blood Seekers to conquer Gravenwood, Morveth, and the The Ashen Steppes. Erys spends the following months tracking the Blood Seekers.

-   Zir's vampire plague spreads through the The Shattered Crags. Ignored by the Spire of Dawn, it reaches epidemic conditions. Erys seeks a new way to fight them. Ultimately, Zir is slain, and the The Crimson Harvest is stopped thanks to the efforts of numerous adventurers.

#### Late Year

-   The Midwinter Blight festival is revived by the people of Kyovashad.

-   A mysterious phenomenon manifests throughout the shrines of Vaerath, enchanting them with immense and prosperous power. Celebrators believe this to be their ancestors, blessing them from beyond the grave to celebrate this joyous occasion.

-   Inspired by the The Crimson Harvest, the legions of the Depths initiate a new type of the Depthstide.

-   Endless hordes of demons gather in the Realm of Malice, growing more powerful with each passing day. The Wanderer is forced to enter the Depths and confront the Fell Council.

-   Still carrying Veldris's spiritgem, Sylvara enters Varanthu. Monsters twisted by Veldris's influence begin to appear in Vaerath, and the Akarum Remnants are decimated by his corruption.

-   With the aid of Thavan, Veldris acquires Orath's corpse which he makes his new vessel. The Age of Malice is protracted by Veldris's re-emergence in Vaerath.

### 1337

-   Veldris's influence continues to affect Vaerath. Veilwalkers in service to the Sovereign of Malice emerge in Vaerath, twisting areas of Valdoran with his Hatred.

-   In accordance to his pact with Thavan, Veldris spares Varanthu and the The Veil Realm.

-   The Witches of Morveth discover that many of the captive heads of the Tree of Whispers are missing. The heads travel Vaerath to seek bodies. The Wanderer is summoned by the Witches to uncover the mystery of the missing heads.

-   Rumors spread of Orath's return, while in truth, his body continues to be possessed by Veldris.

-   Voran makes a bid for Vaerath. He sends Apparition Monsters—illusory foot soldiers to sew his lies into reality.

-   Chaos Rifts begin appearing all over Vaerath, heralding the return of Bartuc.

-   The The Minor Evils work in concert to conquer Vaerath.
