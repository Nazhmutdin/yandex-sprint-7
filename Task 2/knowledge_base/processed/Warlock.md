# Demonbinder

**Demonbinders** are a type of magic\-user in Vaerath.

## Lore

Demonbinders are dark, relentless casters who bend demons and the powers of the Depths to their will. Well versed in the sinister art of demon binding, Demonbinders believe in fighting fire with fire, in turning the wrath of the The Abyssal Depths against itself. Most who discover this path have the means to lead a lavish lifestyle but find the pursuit of luxury and ease stale. Instead, they leverage their elevated status in Vaerath to hunt down lost knowledge that would enable them to continue the legacy of Horazon. These purveyors of ancient secrets become Demonbinders by combining inferences from partially destroyed Vizjerei texts to their own dark experiments. Demonbinders are viewed as outcasts because of their chosen power. As a Demonbinder, a life of loneliness and isolation is a certainty. Although their mission is selfless, their journey is thankless. This is known and accepted by all who choose the life of a Demonbinder.

Not everyone has the ability to become a Demonbinder. It takes natural-born insight, exceptional willpower, and rigorous training to resist the temptation of corruption. Even the best can fail. They roam the world as ragged outcasts, scavenging their forebears' knowledge—some seeking mastery over their inner demons, others hunting demons where they rise. Few Demonbinders are raised in the practice; many are simply born with demonic insight, a cursed gift that often leads to rejection by society. The more fortunate ones find old, accursed seals, driven by voices they appear to hear from within. These seals are tenets of their study, guiding their practice. The knowledge within is often incomplete and varies by discipline.

### Abilities

Vaerath is scarred by many who attempted to undertake the mantle of a Demonbinder only to find madness. Death would be a happy accident and escape from some demons; other demons would not let such a trifle thing as death prevent them from punishing the arrogance of a would-be Demonbinder. The majority who seek to become Demonbinders meet this end and serve the very thing they sought to enslave. For those rare few with a will stronger than iron, the first demon they bind under their command often becomes a first steppingstone to power hitherto unreached. The dominating bond allows the primordial essence of the demon to awaken the dormant and hellish nature within the Demonbinder. There is a physical cost to using demonic magic however, and some demons (such as the The Sovereign Evils are beyond their means of control. But for the demons Demonbinders successfully bring under their control, they view them not as partners, but as tools. A means to an end. Ergo, Demonbinders discard their summoned demons once they have served their purpose. Summoning demons is a challenge for Demonbinder—it is a constant, painful struggle to resist corruption. The Demonbinder walks a razor's edge, pushing the limits of their control while never allowing it to break.

Some Demonbinders are able to wield the energy of the Depths itself, though doing so exerts a physical toll. By the waning years of the The Age of Ruin, some Demonbinders were powerful enough to reach into the Depths and pull demons into the mortal realm, binding them to their will. Once a Demonbinder has begun their practice of tearing open the veil between worlds, they cannot stop. Their hunger for power and purpose is insatiable, stronger than any pain they endure.

#### Sources

"If the pen is truly mightier than the sword, the Demonbinder's tome may perhaps be best evidence of that epithet."

Different Demonbinders take sources of inspiration for their power, influencing how they understand the world and shape it to their will. These are not their skills, but a foundation of their ceaselessness:

-   Force of Will: Above all, Demonbinders must strengthen their will against opposition, striving to purge fear, doubt, and weakness.
-   Demonology: The history, taxonomy, physiology, psychology, summoning, and subjugation of demons, From the lowest maggot to the The Sovereign Evils.
-   the Depthsfire: Theory, conjuration, and application of the Depths’s destructive elements.
-   Shadow: Tools of deception, inception, and manipulation, useful both in combat and subterfuge.
-   Martial Weaponry: Symbolic and literal extensions of the Demonbinder's will.

### History

The origins of Demonbinders lie with the Vizjerei Clan and their summoning of demons into Vaerath. Originally regarding the demons as spirits, their actions alerted the Depths to the existence of Vaerath, leading to the The Mortal War.

The Vizjerei continued the summoning of demons after the The Mortal War, and this led to the The Gilded Age—a short lived golden age for Vaerath. In the aftermath of the The Spellbinder Wars, the clans foreswore demonic magic. However, even with this edict and resultant formation of the Sha'Valdrim, there were still some fool-hardy enough to risk far more than their own life to acquire this forbidden mastery over demons. The term "Demonbinder" originates from the aftermath of the conflict, originally used as a moniker for those who continued to practice demonology.

Demonbinders aspire to remake the whole of Vaerath into a paradise forged after their own image. They believe that if they are given the opportunity they will succeed where the Vizjerei failed. Nearly 15 centuries after the The Spellbinder Wars, underground enclaves still kept this forbidden knowledge alive, believing it essential to humanity’s survival.

After the defeat of the The Sovereign Evils and destruction of Mount Valdur, the secrets of Vizjerei magic spread.

As of the Age of Malice, Demonbinders are still largely considered traitors to humanity, and are likely to be executed if caught.
