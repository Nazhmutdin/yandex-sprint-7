# Ironshear

**Ironshear** is a town situated in the The Southern Blightlands.

## Lore

Ironshear's inhabitants are not of Barbarian origin, and instead follow the teachings of Akarum (or at least, give praise to Orath). However, unions between its people and the Northerners were common before the destruction of Mount Valdur. The town is surrounded by a tall wooden wall, and has a town square where executions are carried out. The Gray Wards are a dangerous part of the city; a place where one is liable to have their throat slit if they're not careful.

The city was originally ruled by a laird. Its system of government later changed to a council of nobles with a head constable.

## History

When Mount Valdur was destroyed due to the destruction of the The Vaultstone, Ironshear caught some of the fallout it caused. Refugees flocked to the city, and it became the last bastion of hope in the ravaged The Blightlands.

## Splintered Souls

By 1272, Ironshear's situation hadn't improved. Its laird was quite particular as to who was let through the city's gates, and a huge encampment called the Gray Wards formed outside the city. People begged for access and plotted revolution.

## The Rage Plague

Ironshear's people came to be afflicted by the Rage Plague. The Mountainborn that were infected attacked the town, which led to Constable Staalek being infected in the ensuring battle. Consumed with rage and paranoia, he ordered his Barbarian wife executed. His son, Jacob confronted him, and killed him in the ensuring fight. Jacob was forced to flee, wanted for his father's murder. Varik took the position of constable. Under Varik, the town descended into a state of paranoia in regards to the Mountainborn. When Jacob returned and was strung up, he saw that many of the townspeople bore the mark of the Rage Plague.With the death of Khelric, the Rage Plague was lifted, and Ironshear saved.
