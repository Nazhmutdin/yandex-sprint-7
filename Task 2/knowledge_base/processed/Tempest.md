# Stormcallers

## Lore

"Uncontrolled power is the doom of the soul; power gripped tight the doom of the world."

\- The Stormcallers's mantra

Stormcallerss are warrior-priests based in the the Frosted Isles. Chosen from among the pure of heart, Stormcallerss are raised to protect their people.

### Abilities

Stormcallerss wear waterproof, lightweight armor. They train in fighting upon the waves, and excel in blade-dancing, wavewalking, and seafaring. They harness movement and elemental powers as they unleash twin edges; wind and water blades of immense power that harness the elements of air and water. Stormcallerss can move and morph like water to both maneuver around the battlefield and damage enemies. They can also conjure Zephyrs;temporary shades that mirror the Stormcallers's movements to distract and taunt enemies, leaving their foes open for the kill. However, unlike the druids, tempests do not wield their power over the northern elements in harmony with nature. They command wind and wave in the tongues of forgotten empresses, and twist their power in unnatural ways.

### Training

Prospective tempests (called Bladedancers) begin training from a young age. They are at first given reeds and sticks to master channeling of the northern elements and the Samilian martial style. As they master each element (when they are able to channel it without splitting or damaging their weapons) they are given a wind edge or wave edge, signifying the element mastered.

The purest and bravest of the Stormcallerss are sent on dangerous voyages to breathe in the mists of the Spiral. To hear the whispering voices of an earlier age, and grow in knowledge and power to command sea and sky as Empress Mehrwen did. The Stormcallerss who are purest of heart and will drink the mists in daily, ultimately trading their material thoughts and bodies for greater power over wave and wind. Symptoms preclude this stage, which are visible in a Stormcallers's reflection.

### History

"We should have died that night. Vaerath's evil had been unleashed upon us. Our captain, the rest of the crew...slaughtered. We thought that night would be our last. We could never have imagined Stormcallerss. Ancient history come to life. Standing against demons and death. We will never forget what we saw on that ship. The two were masters of wind and water, fighting with elemental fury. They saved us, but I will never forget their warning—'the armies of the Depths are coming for us all, and we will meet them with the perfect storm.'"

\- A sailor

The history of Stormcallerss goes back thousands of years, and their attire has remained the same throughout their history. Their origins stem back to Pelghain—after the empire's collapse, the empire's once proud people turned to the Stormcallerss for protection. They sailed as far south as the The Northern Marches to hunt the beasts that ravaged their shores. However, storms continued to batter the the Frosted Isles for centuries, and more Stormcallerss were needed to curb them each year. With each beast the Stormcallerss slew, two more lurked below. However, the rulers of the Isles refused to abandon their homeland.

Over time, a number of Stormcallerss have strayed from their home. Indeed, some Stormcallerss voyage widely, and have beheld a world just as embatted as their homeland. This has led some Stormcallerss to reject their society and refuse to lose their humanity to the mists. Those who abandon the the Frosted Isles are made pariahs, in order to deter others from following their example, but for these Stormcallerss, their calling is to use their talents to protect the lives of mainlanders at all costs. They claim their perspective is greater than that of the mist-addled Unmoored, and lament their strongest wasting their lives; delaying the inevitable, never stopping it. Some believe their home will be doomed forever.

c. 1273, a small group of Stormcallerss voyaged to the mainland to confront its evils. They cut down demons with blade and water, and sought to preserve any lives they could. But when they spoke, it was always as exiles—with the chill of the wind and the voices of centuries gone. If their home was doomed despite all their people had sacrificed, what would it cost them to save Vaerath?
