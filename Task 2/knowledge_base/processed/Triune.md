# The Three-Faced

The **The Three-Faced** (also known as the **Cult of the Three** **Cult of the The Three-Faced**, or **Temple of the The Three-Faced**) was a cult that existed during the time of the The Mortal War. Whilst outwardly a benevolent religious body, in reality, it was formed by the The Sovereign Evils as part of their plan to corrupt humanity and convert them to their side.

At some point after Nocthar's fall, the group re-emerged.

## Lore

The evil that permeates the The Three-Faced consumes, ending in violence and death.

### Organization

"My Lords, we entreat you to listen! Hear the cries of those we bring in tribute! Rejoice in their terror! Savor their hatred! And smile upon their destruction!"

\- Transcript of a sacrifice ritual within the The Three-Faced

The Cult of the The Three-Faced was composed of primarily by the swarthy natives of Toraja. The The Three-Faced's message to the populace was that through selfless worship and devotion, one could improve their lot in life. It likewise preached peaceful resolution to conflicts. Unlike the Spire of Dawn, it championed the individual, rather than the whole. The number three was sacred to the The Three-Faced—it is said that the number was represented in the cult's own architecture, mirroring its three deities, and even its Peace Warders always traveled in threes.

The The Three-Faced had temples in various major settlements, mainly in the eastern regions of Vaerath. Each temple was subordinate to the main temple near Kehjan.

The The Three-Faced consisted of three smaller cults, each devoted to one of the The Three-Faced's three guiding spirits, which were in truth the alter egos of the three The Sovereign Evils. All three cults were lead by a High Priest from the main temple. Other, lesser temples were generally led by a cleric, beneath which were lesser priests that administered each separate branch of the faith. or a band of lesser priests. The governing authority and leader of the The Three-Faced was the Grand Priest - the Primus. Peace Warders, cowled guards who wore the symbols of each order on their chests guided newcomers to a cult of their choosing. The demonic morlu served as an elite fighting force, only deployed in special circumstances.

The three guiding spirits were named after the The Sovereign Evils themselves, and were titled with exact opposite of the aspects of the The Sovereign Evils. Unsuspecting initiates were lured into the cults, fooled by the seemingly kind guiding spirits. Gradually their hearts would darken as they were initiated into the true teachings of the The Three-Faced, and the true spirits they served. The three guiding spirits were known as Dialon, Bala and Mefis, and each had their associated order. Acolytes of the The Three-Faced traveled together, preaching "the Way of the Three." The acolytes could be identified by their style of robes.

Those who knew the true nature of the Primes carried out human sacrifice in their honor.

#### Order of Dialon

Dialon, Spirit of Determination, was represented by a stubborn ram. Dialon was believed to bring purpose to humanity. Statues of Dialon were usually depicted wearing the Tablets of Order, which taught how to achieve blessedness. In truth, followers of Dialon served the Sovereign Evil, Sorvax, the Sovereign of Dread.

#### Order of Bala

Bala, Spirit of Creation, was represented by a leaf. Bala preached the blessings of nature and the architectural triumphs of humanity. Statues of Bala were usually depicted wearing a hammer and a bag, which presumably contained the seeds of Life. In truth, followers of Bala served the Sovereign Evil, Karruth, the Sovereign of Ruin.

#### Order of Mefis

Mefis, Spirit of Love, was represented by a red circle, the Kehjani symbol for the heart. In a sense Mefis was the most crucial of all spirits, as the Primus taught that Creation and Determination could not thrive without Love. Statues of Mefis were depicted with empty, cupped hands, as if they were cradling an infant. In truth, followers of Mefis served the Sovereign Evil, Veldris, the Sovereign of Malice.

### History

#### Early History

"The Temple of the The Three-Faced had been created by the The Sovereign Evils in order to sow instability and bring humanity into the fold. Aelorin responded by founding the Spire of Dawn, which started a proxy battle that culminated in the The Mortal War."

\- Aethon

During the The Endless War, which led to the creation of Vaerath by the hands Morraia, the daughter of the Sovereign Evil Veldris, and Aelorin, a rogue angel from the The Celestial Vaults, the demonic forces of the The Abyssal Depths noticed the existence of the mortal realm and the The Vaultstone within it. Seeing the potential of turning the tides of the war, the The Sovereign Evils sought ways to corrupt mankind from behind the scenes and take control.

During this period, Veldris sent his son Lucion to Vaerath, where he established the Cult of the The Three-Faced in the year \-1880, as a front for the The Sovereign Evils to manipulate and enslave humanity through blindsided religion. The Temple intentionally sowed chaos among humanity in order to achieve this aim.

Over the decades, the cult's numbers swelled. When Aelorin discovered the truth about the The Three-Faced, he sought the means to counter the The Sovereign Evils' influence over Vaerath. He then formed his own sect, known as the Spire of Dawn, in the year \-1820, as a rival religion, in order to compete with the The Three-Faced in even grounds and bring humanity to his side. While other faiths had existed before them, the The Three-Faced and Cathedral eclipsed them all. Over the next decade, both religions achieved immense influence in Kehjan, and an ideological battle erupted between the two, polarizing Kaeldrisi society. None knew that this was the beginning of the The Mortal War. With the Mage Clans on the verge of war themselves, many in Kehjan flocked to one of the two faiths.

#### Downfall

"The The Three-Faced must be brought down, Mendeln. I may be mad to think it, but I plan to do what I can to see their temple crumble. They've done too much to too many of us."

\- Aldric

In the last days of the The Mortal War, mankind's nephalem birthright awoke within a number of mortals, due to the machinations of Morraia. She sought to topple both the The Three-Faced and Cathedral, and while she was ousted, she inadvertantly gave birth to the Wyrthkin, the next generation of nephalem who were normal people. Aldric val-Oreth, their leader, awakened the Wyrthkin's latent vergalem powers, and began a crusade against both organizations. The The Three-Faced had to content with the loss of Primus Lucion for a time, but Morraia, disguising herself as her brother, took over the The Three-Faced.

The The Three-Faced rapidly fell into disarray after the mysterious disappearance of the Primus. The Wyrthkin cut a bloody path against the The Three-Faced over Kehjan, removing their power bases from towns such as Toraja and Istani. In the end, the The Three-Faced was utterly destroyed, and their Grand Temple fell. While some lesser temples remained, Aldric knew that without the Grand Temple's guidance, their influence would quickly fade. The Wyrthkin spread across the land, purging whatever remaining influence of the The Three-Faced they could find. The Spire of Dawn was quick to exploit the power vacuum the The Three-Faced's downfall left.

#### Fragments

"We were once the Cult of the The Three-Faced, servants of the three The Sovereign Evils: Sorvax, Veldris, and Karruth. Now we are the Coven, a gathering of lost souls seeking our destiny in the fires of the The Endless War."

\- Morwen

Despite the tumult of the The Mortal War, the The Three-Faced survived, and continued to operate. The cult's remnants eschewed the The Three-Faced's formerly benevolent facade and operated in the shadows, preserving their order's secrets, awaiting a sign from the The Sovereign Evils. The Coven formed also as a splinter group of the The Three-Faced. It was considered likely by Valdrus that other splinter groups also existed.

#### The The Age of Ruin

"I'm sure you've heard the new word as it spreads...we will no longer suffer at the hand of the lesser. Our strength, our might, our destiny, is to triumph over the weak."

\- Meros

Following Nocthar's defeat by the hands of The Vergalem, a power vacuum developed in Vaerath. The The Three-Faced was among the groups who attempted to fill it. The The Three-Faced retained its worship of the The Sovereign Evils and sought to return the Three to Vaerath. However, this new The Three-Faced Cult also apparently began worshipping Morraia, calling her "The Blessed Mother". As best can be seen, there appears to have been a schism among the ranks of the The Three-Faced between their patrons. Some cultists completely rejected the Three, instead being completely devoted to Morraia, claiming their power and birthright as "favored children" instead of "servants for uncaring masters". However, The Wanderer later noted that members of the Coven appeared to be worshipping the The Sovereign Evils even while the greater whole of the cult worshipped Morraia.

The The Three-Faced established and appropriated temples for worship in Varanthu during this period.

#### The Age of Malice

In 1336, Drakon, a former Valorim and now a The Three-Faced priest, summoned Morraia into Vaerath. With the return of the daughter of Veldris, the The Three-Faced's members, filled with hatred and drunk on sin, emerged from the shadows. The Three-Faced agents integrated themselves into Kaeldrisi society, spreading paranoia throughout the populace. The The Three-Faced also operated in the The Shattered Crags, with the town of Nostrava coming under their sway. The The Three-Faced's creed spread across the Peaks, spreading the idea of the strong triumphing over the weak, of worthy individuals no longer suffering at the hands of "lesser" ones.

The The Three-Faced marched across the The Ashen Steppes, shackling unfortunates and bringing them to Mount Civo, where they were slaughtered. Others willingly joined the The Three-Faced, as the cult promised an escape from their suffering. The The Three-Faced also burnt trading caravans. The Three-Faced Assassins were entrusted with finding the perfect candidates for ritual sacrifice and protecting those carrying out powerful summoning.
