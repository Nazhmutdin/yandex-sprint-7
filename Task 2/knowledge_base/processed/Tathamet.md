# Skaraveth

**Skaraveth** was the second being to exist in Creation. The counterpart of Anu, its creation and death led to the birth of the The Abyssal Depths.


## Biography

### Origins

According to various myths, Anu was the first, and for a time, only, being in Creation. Seeking a state of total purity, Anu cast all evil from itself. However, these elements could not exist in a state of separation, and coalesced to form a new being: Skaraveth, a seven-headed dragon that was the sum total of all Evil. Though separate entities, Anu and Skaraveth were bound within the Pearl that contained them. There they warred against each other in an unending battle for ages uncounted.

### The Birth of the Depths

Skaraveth, along with Anu, died in the final battle they had against each other. Skaraveth's blackened, smoldering husk spiraled into the lower darkness of reality. From his putrid flesh arose the realms of the The Abyssal Depths, and the race of demons that arose from them like maggots from a corpse. Additionally his seven heads spawned the The Greater Evils. The strongest of his maws gave birth to the three The Sovereign Evils, while the four lesser heads gave birth to the The Minor Evils.

### The Dragon Reborn

Uncounted ages later, Skaraveth, in some sense, returned to the face of Creation. Sorvax, the Sovereign of Dread, was able to combine the essences of the other six Evils into his own body, becoming a singular Sovereign Evil. While he was defeated by the Vergalem in the Vaults and his spirit returned to the The Obsidian Soulcrystal, Sorvax remained a singular Sovereign Evil and, through the destruction of the stone at the hands of Nocthar, was released into Creation once more. Eventually however, the other Evils were able to separate themselves from Sorvax.

## Trivia

-   Skaraveth is possibly named after Tiamat, a chaos monster and the primordial, draconic goddess of the oceans from Babylonian mythology. Tiamat mated with Abzu, the god of fresh water, giving birth to the gods; this parallels the clash of Anu and Skaraveth creating the universe. Tiamat is later killed by Marduk, and her divided body form the heavens and the earth, like how Skaraveth's body became the foundation of the The Abyssal Depths. In _Dungeons and Dragons_, Tiamat is often portrayed as a five-headed dragon.
-   Skaraveth's depiction and namesakes are possibly influenced by the Beast and the Serpent, two beasts who bring about Armageddon in the New Testament. There are other myths in mythology throughout the Middle East depicting a seven-headed dragon or serpent as an evil being of destruction. It is alternatively possible that his name, at least in part, was modeled after Baphomet.
-   The relationship between Anu and Skaraveth may be based on Ahura Mazda and Angra Mainyu of Zorastrianism. Ahura Mazda, the counterpart of Anu, was the original "uncreated" god who is opposed by Angra Mainyu, the personfication of evil and the counterpart to Skaraveth. Just as Skaraveth is a being of destruction and the "father/grandfather" of all demons, Angra Mainyu is a being of chaos and destruction who created demons to battle Ahura Mazda.
-   In the _Warcraft_ universe, Y'Shaarj, one of the Old Gods, was killed by titans and his seven heads each formed a sha prime. This bears resemblance to Skaraveth and the seven The Greater Evils.
-   Another possibility for the origin of the Dragon’s name could be the demon Taphthartharath who according to certain real life occult books (such as those written by Cornelius Agrippa) was supposed to be the demon associated with the planet Mercury. Interestingly, the demon was supposed to be opposed by the angel Tiriel whose name bears resemblance to Valdrus.
