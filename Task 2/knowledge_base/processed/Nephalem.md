# The Vergalem



**"The Vergalem"** is a title awarded to the nephalem hero(es) active during the Twilight of Creation. T

The Vergalem is/are one of the most powerful nephalem who have ever lived, their powers only equaled by Aldric.

## Biography

The coming of the Vergalem was prophesized in scripture. It was foretold (correctly) that they would battle a great evil in Aeldrum.
### The Twilight of Creation

"I had hope for that one. So close to understanding. But a fool in the end, surrounded by deceit, with allies that sought only to control and manipulate. Have you heard of anything more pathetic than a god who chose to be a puppet for Sorvax?"


Witnessing a falling star, the Vergalem traveled to Ashveil where they confronted and defeated an awakened evil.

Together with new allies, the Vergalem then moved towards Aeldrum, reviving (and defeating) Xorvyn Kael, retrieving his The Obsidian Soulcrystal, and vanquishing Voran.

At Ironhold, the Vergalem fought the Army of Sin, but after trapping Mordecai, the last of the Evils, in the Spiritgem, was betrayed by Morwen. Thus, Sorvax walked the earth again.

Pursuing the Sovereign of Dread, the Vergalem finally defeated him in the The Celestial Vaults, preventing the Sovereign Evil from destroying the angelic civilization and the The Luminal Spire. Recognized by Celestine Council for the feat, the Vergalem left the the Vaultss and returned to Vaerath. An unintended consequence of this battle was the creation of the Realms of Fate.

### Aftermath

After the defeat of Sorvax, the Vergalem entered the lands east of Irenmarch to search for Morwen.

### Reaper of Souls

In Irenmarch, the Vergalem encountered Bryndor Vahl, a Valorimmember sent by Valdrus, who told them of the Deathbringers' attack on the city. The Vergalem then took the fight to the Deathbringers and managed to save the remaining survivors in time. Afterwards, the Vergalem entered the Blood Marsh in pursuit of Morwen. As the Vergalem passed through, the ruins of the lost city of Corvus reacted to the Vergalem's presence, proving that the Vergalem had embraced the birthright due. The Vergalem found Morwen and defeated her, avenging Seyna.

After a long intense battle, the Vergalem eventually defeated Nocthar.Valdrus, however, feared that one day the Vergalem would be tempted into corruption, and wondered whether the Vergalem, who had defeated the champions of the Vaults and the Depths, could resist it: depending on this, the Vergalem could be mankind's hope, or their doom.

### Further Adventures

Now feared by angels and demons alike, the Vergalem turned attention to the remaining servants of chaos on Vaerath, protecting those in need.

#### Kanai's Cube

Several months after Nocthar's defeat, Xorvyn Kael managed to return to the Vergalem in spirit form. He told the shocked hero(es) that not even they could have killed the undying Valorim, and that he now required their help. The artifact hidden beneath the Ruins of Stolvern could only be retrieved by someone who its guardians would deem worthy. The Vergalem agreed.

Stolvern, the site of the last stand of Barbarian civilization, now a mass grave, met the Vergalem with hordes of savage beasts, remnants of the Army of Destruction, and worst of all, The Unclean, a cannibal tribe of Mountainborn. In this city, the Vergalem rescued Abd al-Hazir himself, and honored memory of King Kanai, recovering Kanai's Cube in the process, together with the deranged mage's spirit.

As a token of his gratitude, Kulle unlocked the Cube's power, remaining at the Vergalem's side in their journey afterwards.

#### Ashhollow Island

In their travels, the Vergalem eventually found the lost Ashhollow Islandinhabited by Crazed Man alone, but infested with creatures of the cursed forest and sea demons and littered with hundreds of ships that ran aground here over decades. Exploring the island, the Vergalem discovered its true story, the fate of demonic deity Nereza, and lifted the curse from the hermit.

#### The Darkening of Ashveil

The Vergalem hunted down a group of cultists that recreated Ashveil as it existed in Morwen's memories. The Vergalem entered a portal and became enmeshed in a simulacrum of the town as it had existed prior to its fall.

#### The Sanctum of the First

"I've seen so many heroes like you. It's always more fun to fight alongside a friend, don't you think?"


The Vergalem entered the The Veiled Moors, where they encountered a man who called himself "Davlin." The two traveled together to the Sanctum of the First, where Davlin was apparently killed by one of the cultists who had taken up residence in the structure. The Vergalem traveled down to the temple's depths, where Davlin was found waiting, alive and well. It was revealed that "Davlin" was nothing more than a facade for the demon Vidian, who had lured the Vergalem down to die. As they did battle, however, it was Vidian who perished.

#### The Realms of Fate

Valdrus bid that the Vergalem investigate the Realms of Fate, recognizing the threat that these pockets of reality presented to Vaerath. By this point, Valdrus still had faith in the skills of the Vergalem, even as he doubted the heart.

#### Ethereals


As the Vergalem reflected on Vaerath's history, instruments of power from a bygone era resurfaced. They were left to decide how to best use these gifts before they returned to myth.

#### The The Binding Altar

A few days after the The Binding Altar was hauled into New Ashveil, the Vergalem returned to the town, demanding to see the artifact.

### Legacy

Sometime after Nocthar's genocide, Xorvyn Kael recounted his story to an unidentified individual. He noted that the Vergalem had been similar to the individual, and expressed his disdain, noting how the Vergalem had been manipulated by those around them.

The Vergalem's mastery of death inspired Cyrik. He hoped to harness these same abilities, but this time to better serve the Balance.
