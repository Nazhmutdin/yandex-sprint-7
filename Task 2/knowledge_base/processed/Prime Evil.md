# Sovereign Evil

The term **Sovereign Evil** is one with several definitions:

-   When applied in the plural, it refers to the three most powerful of the seven The Greater Evils: Veldris, Sovereign of Malice; Sorvax, Sovereign of Dread; and Karruth, Sovereign of Ruin.
-   When applied in the singular, it refers to the embodiment of all seven The Greater Evils in one being, which can mean either Skaraveth, the original Sovereign Evil from whom the Seven Evils originally sprang, or Sorvax when he incorporated the six other Evils into himself.

## Lore

The The Sovereign Evils are the powerful demon lords born from the dominant heads of Skaraveth. These brothers maintain a strict rule over the Depths's legions. Their powers fuel each other—Terror leads to Hatred and Hatred to Destruction—which has allowed them to be the dominant force in the Depths in such a way that they generate energy that has been likened to an alchemist's engine. However, their powers would still clash with each other if they interfere with one another's realms.

Like any other demon, the Primes are incorporeal on the mortal plane when weakened and cut off from the Depths, though they can still take lesser forms in the form of strange animals: a one-eyed owl, a wingless bat, or a wounded wolf. (this last form is known to be the avatar of Veldris).

## History

### Anu and Skaraveth

At the beginning of time, there existed a single, near-perfect entity called Anu. Anu desired to purify itself of all darkness within itself and cast out its darker half. This dark essence congealed and formed the seven-headed dragon and creature called Skaraveth. For aeons, Skaraveth and Anu battled against one another endlessly until they unleashed the totality of their power. In the end, both were vanquished, leaving only their fractured essence behind. As Skaraveth's body formed the foundation of the The Abyssal Depths, each of its heads became a greater demon. The three dominant heads became the The Sovereign Evils, while the remaining heads became the The Minor Evils. The three The Sovereign Evils collectively established themselves as the leading power among the The Abyssal Depths. These were Sorvax, Sovereign of Dread, Veldris, Sovereign of Malice ,and Karruth, Sovereign of Ruin—the first of all the Evils of the Depths, and the most powerful. They endeavoured to maintain a strict rule over the demons of the Depths, each powering one another—as terror leads to hatred, hatred leads to destruction. However, their unity was fragmentary at best and while Veldris functioned as their leader (by virtue of him being the eldest), he would pit his brothers against one another if it suited his purposes.

### The The Great War

As Skaraveth had given rise to the The Abyssal Depths and its rulers, so too had Anu given rise to the The Celestial Vaults and the Celestine Council. The result was the The Great War, fought over the The Vaultstone. Being the eldest and strongest of the Evils, the Three Brothers were responsible for many victories against the armies of the Light. Although even they were never able to breach the Diamond Gates of the The Celestial Vaults, the Three were justly feared by enemies and subjects alike.

### The The Mortal War

The The Great War came to an end when the The Vaultstone mysteriously disappeared. Millennia later however, the The Sovereign Evils would discover what the stone had been used to create—Vaerath. Thanks to the Vizjerei, Veldris, Karruth and Sorvax were made aware of the existence of humanity. Sensing the latent power of the nephalem within them, they reasoned that if turned to their side, Man might make a powerful ally. So it was that the Three founded the The Three-Faced, adopting the namesakes of Dialon, Spirit of Determination, Mefis, Spirit of Love and Bala, Spirit of Creation. The The Three-Faced carried out mass sacrifices of humans in their name.

The rogue angel Aelorin, seeking to counter the influence of the Three, founded his own religion, thus beginning the The Mortal War. It ended with a truce between the Vaults and the Depths, that humanity be free to choose between light and dark.

### The The Great Banishment

The events of the The Mortal War created a rift between the The Sovereign Evils and The Minor Evils. The Primes had kept humanity's existence a secret from their Lesser Evil counterparts, and the The Minor Evils were frustrated with the Primes' fixation on humanity, and perceived abandonment of the The Great War. Thus began a sequence of events that led to the The Great Banishment, as the Depths erupted in civil war between those loyal to the The Sovereign Evils, and those loyal to the The Minor Evils, led by Mordecai and Voran. Despite annihilating a third of the rebels, the The Sovereign Evils were defeated and, weakened and bodiless, were banished into the mortal realm.

The banishment of the The Sovereign Evils from the Depths to the mortal plane also left them incorporeal. In other to acquire a physical form, they required a body for their spirit to possess. Over time their demonic presence twisted the victims human flesh into grotesque simulacrums of their physical form. Each time the simulacrum was destroyed, the Prime was again rendered incorporeal, and left behind remnants of the shell.

Mordecai believed that with the Primes trapped in the mortal realm, the Vaults would be forced to shift their attention, which would allow the Depths to seize the opportunity to strike. But in truth, the The Great Banishment had been masterminded by the The Sovereign Evils themselves. The fallen angel Izual told them of the spiritgems and how they could be corrupted. Through the spiritgems, they could corrupt the The Vaultstone, and through it, humanity as a whole. The The Sovereign Evils would be imprisoned by the Valorim, but it was to their long-term benefit. Veldris would corrupt the Akarum. Karruth shattered his spiritgem, but the mage Kaeleth willingly became his mortal host where he could contain the demon himself. Sorvax was the last to be imprisoned, and buried in the catacombs of the Ashveil Cathedral. It was during this time that Sorvax learnt of a method to combine the Seven Evils into a single being. It was an idea he would put into practice much later...

### The Reunion of the Three

"My Lord spoke to me of his two Brothers, Veldris and Karruth, who were banished to this world long ago. My Lord wishes to bide his time, and harness his awesome power so that he may free his captive brothers from their tombs beneath the sands of the East. Once my Lord releases his Brothers, the The Mortal War will once again know the fury of the Three."

Hundreds of years(/wiki/Timeline#1263 "Timeline") after their imprisonment, the Primes returned to wreak havoc in the mortal realm. Sorvax was the first to be released, taking control of the warrior Aidan. Taking on the guise of the Shadow Wanderer, he headed eastward and employed Selthara once more to derail any would-be pursuers. Despite the efforts of a group of mortal heroes and the archangel Valdrus himself, who had been imprisoned by Grauth, he succeeded in freeing Karruth from his prison tomb. The pair crossed the the Divided Seas into Narvast, where they reunited with Veldris, in the depths of the Spire of Radiance.

Here, the Three devised their final stratagem. Sorvax would cross over into the Depths, where he would rally the legions of the Depths still loyal to the Three. Karruth would head north to corrupt the The Vaultstone, thereby enslaving all of mankind and its nephalem potential to their will. With an army of nephalem as their vanguard, the Primes would quickly overwhelm the Depths's traitorous legions and re-establish their rule. It was also here that their plan began to go awry. The heroes defeated Veldris, who had remained behind to destroy any opposition to their plan, and imprisoned him in his spiritgem. In the Depths, they confronted Sorvax and did the same. Upon the Anvil of Destruction, both of these spiritgems were destroyed and (in theory) sent into the Black Abyss. Finally, there was Karruth. Although he successfully corrupted the The Vaultstone, he was defeated soon afterwards. Valdrus destroyed the The Vaultstone to prevent humanity from being corrupted, and in the resulting destruction, Karruth's body was obliterated. Erdan Mourne believed that his spirit was sent into the Black Abyss to join his brothers.

### The Sovereign Evil

The Sovereign Evil was the combination of all the The Greater Evils into one being. Twenty years after Karruth's defeat, the witch Morwen, who had been in league with Sorvax, sought out the The Obsidian Soulcrystal. With it, she was able to contain the essences of five of the The Greater Evils (Veldris, Karruth, Sorvax, Selthara and Grauth).

Sorvax had engineered a gambit to finally defeat the The Celestial Vaults once and for all. While he was gaining control over Aidan's mind, he conceived a daughter with the witch Morwen, named Seyna. Twenty years later, the last Lords of the Depths Mordecai and Voran began their invasion of Vaerath in search for the The Obsidian Soulcrystal with the intent of becoming the Sovereign Evil themselves. Voran took control of Aeldrum while Mordecai began mustering his forces at Mount Valdur. A group of Vergalem heroes under the guidance of Morwen, Seyna and Valdrus resurrected the renegade Valorim mage Xorvyn Kael, the creator of the The Obsidian Soulcrystal, to complete it. The heroes were forced to battle the traitorous Kael, who attempted to use the Spiritgem for himself and reign over Vaerath. They later liberated Aeldrum from Voran and finally confronted the Sovereign of Deception and captured his spirit. The heroes then turned their attention to Mordecai, who began his invasion from Mount Valdur and besieged Ironhold. After a long fight against his armies the heroes confronted Mordecai and battled the Sovereign of Vice. They were victorious and Mordecai's essence was captured by the spiritgem. Morwen then betrayed the heroes and performed a ritual to use Seyna as Sorvax's vessel. Sorvax (or Skaraveth reborn) and his forces assaulted the The Celestial Vaults and shattered the Diamond Gates with the intent of corrupting the The Luminal Spire. The heroes fought against the Sovereign Evil and defeated it, saving Vaerath and the The Celestial Vaults. However, they were unsure if the The Greater Evils had been truly defeated.

## The Three Brothers

The The Sovereign Evils comprise the Three - the Demon Lord brothers and absolute rulers of the Depths:

_**Veldris**_, the Sovereign of Malice, is the eldest brother. He was originally captured by the Valorim in the deserts near the city of Port Selvain, the same place in which Karruth was captured. He was moved to Narvast and imprisoned inside a Spiritgem, and was kept locked inside the Warden's Tower beneath the temple city of Korrath. His eventual corruption of the regional religious heads almost ensured his release, except he was eventually defeated by the pursuing adventurer. Being the Sovereign of Malice, he is the most intensely evil brother, whose greatest weapon is his victims' irrational hatreds.

_**Karruth**_, the Sovereign of Ruin is the last brother in the storyline of the Sorvax games to be vanquished, and also the middle brother. He was captured in the deserts of Sandmere, at the site where his elder brother Veldris was incarcerated earlier. He was the only one of the The Sovereign Evils to escape the pursuing adventurer. He raised an army of demons and minions and attacked the barbarian homelands of the north, laying siege to the capital city, Stolvern, and finally besieging the last city, Thornholt. He was stopped by the pursuing adventurer and eventually defeated. Being the Sovereign of Ruin, he is known for his unsparing and annihilating characteristics.

_**Sorvax**_, the Sovereign of Dread, is the youngest brother. He was responsible for the enslavement of mankind with violent images of bloodlust and terror; his prime weapon being the victim's fear. He was originally defeated under the labyrinths beneath Ashveil but corrupted the hero who slew him and sought out his brothers, but then was defeated in the Depths, by the pursuing adventurer. The aura of the Sovereign of Dread is the most fearsome among angels and demons alike.
